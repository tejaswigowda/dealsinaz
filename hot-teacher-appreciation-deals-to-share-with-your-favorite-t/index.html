Onto the deals that have been sent over (if you have one to add, please e-mail me at alicia@dealsinaz.com):

Organ Stop Pizza: "Organ Stop Pizza offers a FREE PIZZA for Teacher Appreciation Week! Come into Organ Stop Pizza in Mesa between May 4 to May 8th, 2015 from 5 p.m. - 9 p.m. and get a gift card for a free pizza. Any size pizza, no limit on toppings. Value up to $20.

Must be an employed K-12 teacher and show valid school ID. (Working at a school does not qualify.)Free Pizzas are for dine-in only. Free pizza coupon may be redeemed immediately or on a future visit. Coupons expire 8/31/15"

McDonalds:

"Arizona McDonald’s restaurants will honor teachers across Arizona with “special perks” onTeacher Appreciation Day, Tuesday, May 5. Teachers who purchase a McCafé beverage and show their school identification at their local McDonald’s restaurant will receive a keytag valid for a FREE small McCafé beverage every day until Dec. 31, 2015, while supplies last. With the free McCafé keytag, teachers will receive their choice of a McCafe beverage. The McCafe menu includes: Hot and Iced Coffee; Hot Mochas, Lattes, and Premium Hot Chocolate; Iced Mochas and Frappes; Real Fruit Smoothies; and McCafe Shakes."

Grimaldi's: "In celebration of Teacher Appreciation Week from May 4th though May 8th, Grimaldi’s Pizzeria is giving teachers 15 percent off their entire order. Teachers who show their school ID will receive a discount at any of the Phoenix or Tucson locations. "

Papa Murphy's: "On Thursday, May 7th, Papa Murphy's will take 50% off pizza with a teacher ID. From open to close— get half off any pizza, any size at regular price—excludes mini-murph, sides and desserts at all participating Phoenix locations. Hours may vary per store. Go to www.papamurphys.com for a location near you."

Butterfly Wonderland: " Butterfly Wonderland thanks Valley teachers for all of their hard work in celebration of National Teacher Appreciation on Saturday, May 2nd! Teachers will receive $3.00 off admission and 15% off all purchases in the café and gift shop. Teachers must present a valid ID to receive the discount."
