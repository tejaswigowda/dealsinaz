HOT from Zulily! Two FREE Starbucks Drinks When You Join Starbucks Rewards!

Feb 25, 2015 · Alicia Markham

This is HUGE! Head over here to get a new Zulily account if you don't already have one (it's free and they have all kinds of fun deals).

Then click here to check out a deal they have with Starbucks. When you join Starbucks Rewards, you'll get TWO FREE DRINKS of your choice!

I'm a member of Starbucks Rewards (I have a gold card since I blog at Starbucks so often). When you get to Gold Card status you'll get a free food or beverage of your choice after every 12 stars (be sure to check out separately for each item...you get one star per check out, not based on what you buy). You load money to and pay with your card (you can make any gift card a rewards card...you can also use a mobile app). You also get free coffee and tea refills (which is HUGE for me). You'll also get a free food or beverage of your choice on your birthday!

Before you get to gold status, you'll also enjoy fun perks!

This is a no-brainer deal if you love Starbucks! Enjoy!
