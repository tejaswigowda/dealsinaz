Mark Your Calendar NOW for Fee Free Days to See the National Parks (There are 15 in AZ)!

Jan 5, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you're getting your dates together for 2017 vacations, you'll definitely want to add these days to your calendar. These are the days you will pay NO ADMISSION FEES for National Parks. Arizona has 15 National Parks and they are all worth a visit!

The no fee days for 2017 are:

  • January 16: Martin Luther King Jr. Day
  • February 20: Presidents' Day
  • April 15-16 and 22-23: Weekends of National Park Week
  • August 25: National Park Service Birthday
  • September 30: National Public Lands Day
  • November 11-12: Veterans Day Weekend

Check out the National Parks in AZ:

Casa Grande Ruins National Monument

Glen Canyon National Recreation Area

Grand Canyon National Park

Lake Mead National Recreation Area

Montezuma Castle National Monument

Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument

Petrified Forest National Park

Pipe Spring National Monument

Saguaro National Park

Sunset Crater Volcano National Monument

Tonto National Monument

Tumacácori National Historical Park

Tuzigoot National Monument

Walnut Canyon National Monument

Wupatki National Monument

If you're planning a weekend up north, keep in mind that many of these parks are relatively close to each other.

Enjoy!
