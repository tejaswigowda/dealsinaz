Donate a Toy at Fashion Square On Saturday, December 4th, Get a Free Ticket to Disney on Ice!

Nov 26, 2021 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona

This is a FANTASTIC give and get deal! Head over here for more information about the toy drive happening Saturday, December 4th at Scottsdale Fashion Square.

"This holiday season, those who go the extra mile for Valley children in need will get something magical for themselves in return.

The first 1,000 merry-makers who stop by the South Parking Lot at Scottsdale Fashion Square between 10 a.m. and noon on Saturday, December 4 to donate a new, unwrapped toy during the Scottsdale Fire Department’s Annual Toy Drive will receive a complimentary show ticket to Disney On Ice presents Dream Big* (courtesy of Feld Entertainment) which features favorite Disney friends such as Mickey, Minnie, Miguel, Moana, Elsa, Belle, Genie, and more highlighting all the magic and adventure of Disney’s tales through world-class figure skating. Audiences will be transported to exciting worlds where heroes of every kind seek to fulfill their dreams at a live experience families will never forget!

*limit one voucher per person/per donation – Tickets received in exchange for donations are available for use for select showtimes when Disney On Ice presents Dream skates into the Footprint Center in Phoenix Jan. 13–16, 2022.

Members of the Scottsdale Fire Department will pick up the collected toys and distribute them among its affiliated community charities, including the Paiute Neighborhood Center, Cardon Children’s Hospital, Honor Health Hospitals and Arizona Helping Hands.

HOLIDAY TOY DRIVE INFORMATION

Holiday Toy Drive at Scottsdale Fashion Square

South Parking Lot

7014 E. Camelback Road

Scottsdale, AZ 85251

Saturday, December 4, 2021

10:00AM – 12:00PM"
