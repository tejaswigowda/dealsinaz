Veterans Day Deals Vets and Active Military Can Enjoy TODAY (11/12)!

Nov 12, 2018 · Alicia

I love that we get to celebrate Veterans for two days this year! Here's a list of freebies Vets and Active Duty Military can enjoy TODAY (11/12). Just bring along your military ID and you'll be good to go.

Phoenix Zoo: Veterans and Active Duty Military can get in free along with up to FIVE guests today (11/12)! Head over here for more information.

Harkins Theatres: Veterans and active duty military can enjoy a free small popcorn on 11/12 with ID.

Famous Dave’s BBQ: Locations in Peoria, Mesa, Gilbert, and Chandler is celebrating current and former military with a FREE 2 meat salute on Monday, November 12th!

Denny’s: Vets and active duty military can stop by Denny’s on November 12th from 5 AM to noon for a free create your own grand slam.

Original Breakfast House: Vets and active duty military eat free on Monday, November 12th at the Original Breakfast House in Phoenix! They’ll have entertainment all day, and lots of fun for all!

Jackson’s Auto Spa: Vets can enjoy a cleaner car with a free full service car wash at Jackson’s Auto Spa on Monday, November 12th.
