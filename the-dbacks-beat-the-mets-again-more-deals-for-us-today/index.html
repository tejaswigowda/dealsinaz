The Dbacks Beat the Mets Again! More Deals for Us Today!

May 17, 2017 · Alicia Markham

The Dbacks beat the Mets again 5-4 last night and that means we get the same deals today (5/17) that we did yesterday!

Head over here to order Papa Johns online and save 50% off regular menu price with code DBACKS50. This deal is good the day after every Dbacks WIN!

Every time the Dbacks score 5 or more runs, the next day you get 3 free tacos at Taco Bell with the purchase of a large drink from 4-6 PM. The Dbacks scored 5 runs yesterday so that means FREE tacos today (5/17)!

Every time the Dbacks “jack” a home run out of the park, the next day we get a free Jumbo Jack with purchase of a large drink (offer good all day). We definitely scored a home run yesterday so FREE Jumbo Jacks for all today (5/17)!

Head over here to get the Circle K app if you don’t have it. For every Dbacks home game they have a strike out meter. Based on the number of strikes, you’ll get a free item the next day in the “rewards” section of your app. Today you can pick up free whoopie pie with the app (though I did hear some locations don’t have them which is a bummer).
