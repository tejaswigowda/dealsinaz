As Low as $7.65 (Usually $20) for Two Tickets to the Scottsdale Culinary Festival's Great American Picnic!

Feb 6, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Head over here to get two tickets to the Scottsdale Culinary Festival's Great American Picnic for as low as $7.65 on Sunday with code LOVE15 (usually $20). Two Saturday tickets will run $9.35 with the code. This is one of my favorite events of the year, it's so much fun if you love food!

"Join Arizona's tastiest fundraiser -- the 37th Scottsdale Culinary Festival -- with this deal for two general admission tickets to their signature event, The Great Arizona Picnic. Presented by the nonprofit organization, Scottsdale League for the Arts, the Picnic features more than 50 restaurants, top chefs, culinary demonstrations, live music on three stages, and more. You'll choose from Saturday or Sunday admission and have access to a slew of activities inside the Great Arizona Picnic.

Proceeds raised from the Scottsdale Culinary Festival's events are distributed to local Arizona arts charities through the Scottsdale League for the Arts' annual grants program. Bring extra cash to purchase food and beverages, which are sold separately. Additional admission is also required for specialty cocktail and craft beer areas; these can be purchased onsite."
