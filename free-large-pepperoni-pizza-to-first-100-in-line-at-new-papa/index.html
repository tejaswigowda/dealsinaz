Free Large Pepperoni Pizza to First 100 in Line at New Papa Murphy's Saturday!

Oct 2, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Near Bell and Thompson Peak in Scottsdale? Head to the newest Papa Murphy's on Saturday, October 3rd. The first 100 people in line will get a free large pepperoni pizza. Everyone else will get large pepperoni pizzas for $4.

Here's more info they sent over:

Starting at 10:00 a.m. on Saturday, October 3, the first 100 customers in line will receive a free large pepperoni pizza. After the giveaway ends, large pepperoni pizzas will be priced at $4 on Saturday.

Additionally, the new location will offer customers pizza-tasting, games, prizes and other kid-friendly activities throughout the day on Saturday.

Customers can text AZ096 to 90421 for a chance to win the grand prize, of free pizza for a year from Aug. 15 to Oct. 3.*

Enjoy!
