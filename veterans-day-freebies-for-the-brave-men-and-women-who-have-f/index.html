Veteran's Day Freebies for The Brave Men and Women Who Have Fought for Our Country!

2023-11-08 · Alicia#EXPIRED#Free#HOT DEAL#Restaurants

Veterans Day is a fantastic time to say THANK YOU to all of the brave men and women who have given so much to defend our freedom. Lots of valley businesses are offering freebies to Active Duty Military and Veterans with ID on 11/10 or 11/11 (read the details to find out when the offer is good). Here are a few (if you know of others please e-mail them to me at alicia@dealsinaz.com). I'll keep updating as I find them!

Applebees:

"For the 16th consecutive year, we’re offering Veterans and Active Duty Military a special Veterans Day meal on Saturday 11/11, where they can select from 8 delicious entrées at Applebee’s across the country (Dine-in only)."

California Pizza Kitchen:

"California Pizza Kitchen give thanks to Veterans and active U.S. military members for their services with a complimentary meal November 11, 2023...We invite all veterans and active military to have a meal on us from our special menu. Thank you for your courage and sacrifice. Your service deserves ours! Dine with us and receive a complimentary non-alcoholic beverage + choice of one entrée from our prix fixed menu Upsells and additions may not be included in the promo."

Dave and Busters:

"Calling all Veterans & Active Military! This Veteran's Day weekend, grab a FREE entrée up to $20 + $10 Power Card* for a weekend of fun & good vibes at D&B! Swing by with the whole fam for a Veteran's Day you'll never forget!"

"*Promotional. VALID 11/10/23-11/12/23. Get a free entrée up to $20 and a $10 Power Card or recharge. Must present DD214 veteran ID or active-duty ID to redeem. Promotion valid one per veteran or active military person per day. Power Card activation fee may apply. Not valid with any other offers. Other restrictions may apply. See store for details."

Denny’s: Is offering all active duty and retired veterans a FREE Original Grand Slam breakfast from 5 AM until noon 11/10.

Freddy's Frozen Custard and Steakburgers: "Honoring our heroes! Join us this Veterans Day, 11/11/23, as we salute those who have served. All Veterans and current military personnel will receive a complimentary Freddy's Original Double with Cheese combo meal card. Redeemable until November 30, 2023. No purchase is required" Thanks Candia!

Great Clips:

"On Saturday, November 11, 2023, veterans and active service members can go into any U.S. Great Clips salon and get a free haircut or a free haircut card for a future visit. In addition, non-military customers who get a haircut on Veterans Day at Great Clips salons will also have the opportunity to pay it forward. They can receive a free haircut card to give to an active service member or veteran they know as a way of saying thank you. The cards can be redeemed for a free haircut from November 12 through December 1, 2023."

Jackson's Car Wash:

(From email they just sent): " Happy Veterans Day. We want to thank you for your service by giving all active and retired military members a free basic full-service wash." They go on to say it's good November 10-12th!

Macayo’s:

"For Veterans Day, we are proud to offer all Active & Non-Active Military Personnel a free entrée on us! Join us Saturday, November 11th to enjoy a free entrée off our special Veterans Day Menu.

VETERANS DAY

Saturday, November 11th

Valid for all Active & Non-Active Military Personnel

Dine In & Macayo Takeout Only

Special Veterans Day Menu

- Fiesta Salad

- Carnitas Dinner

- Chicken Poblano

- Macayo’s Original Chimichanga

- Sonoran Enchilada Trio

- Baja Burro

- Carne Asada Burro"

Original Breakfast House: Veterans and active-duty military will eat free on Friday, November 10th. There's a big party for everyone! Head over here for more information on their facebook page.

Sport Clips: “Many Sport Clips locations will offer free haircuts to active duty U.S. service members and veterans on Veterans Day, November 11, 2023, with valid proof of service. Thank you for your service!” Head over here to see a list of participating locations.

Enjoy!
