Veterans Day is this weekend and to celebrate the brave men and women who have fought for our country, many valley restaurants are offering freebies! Bring along your military ID and you'll be good to go! If you know of other great deals to share, please e-mail me (alicia@dealsinaz.com) and I'll add them to the list.

DEALS ON SUNDAY (11/11):

Romano's Macaroni Grill: Sunday Vets and active duty military can stop by for a free order of Mom's Ricotta meatballs and spaghetti.

Fired Pie: Sunday Vets and active duty military can stop by for a FREE entree.

California Pizza Kitchen: Sunday CPK is offering Veterans a free meal from a special menu.

Sport Clips: Veterans can enjoy a free haircut on Sunday at participating locations. They will also be donating $1 from all other haircuts purchased on Sunday to a Veteran scholarship fund.

DEALS ON SUNDAY and MONDAY (11/11 and 11/12):

Famous Dave's BBQ: Locations in Peoria, Mesa, Gilbert, and Chandler is celebrating current and former military with a FREE 2 meat salute on Sunday, November 11th and Monday, November 12th!

DEALS ON MONDAY (11/12):

Denny's: Vets and active duty military can stop by Denny's on November 12th from 5 AM to noon for a free create your own grand slam.

Original Breakfast House: Vets and active duty military eat free on Monday, November 12th at the Original Breakfast House in Phoenix! They'll have entertainment all day, and lots of fun for all!

Jackson's Auto Spa: Vets can enjoy a cleaner car with a free full service car wash at Jackson's Auto Spa on Monday, November 12th.

DEALS for ALL:

This is my favorite weekend of the year to shop small gifts at Kohl's! Sunday and Monday only you can head to my site for a code that will take $10 off your $25 purchase. If you have a Kohl's charge, you'll also get 30% off with another code and get free shipping on any size order. You can use all three codes TOGETHER and make unlimited orders! This is on top of sales! With all three codes, you'll spend $10.50 for a $25 item shipped!

If you're shopping in Tempe Marketplace or Desert Ridge Markeplace and spend $300 in one day, you'll get two FREE tickets to the Trans Siberian Orchestra! Even if you don't have anything to buy, you could get a $300 Visa gift card at Target to use anywhere!

Enjoy and happy Veterans Day!
