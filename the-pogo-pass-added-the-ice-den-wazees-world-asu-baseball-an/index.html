The Pogo Pass Added the Ice Den, Wazee's World, ASU Baseball and Softball, and More to the Already Great Passes!

Mar 9, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Spring Break is already here for some valley kids and it's close for many others! The Pogo Pass will help your family get out and have fun at places throughout the valley for the ENTIRE YEAR (including spring break). You can head over here to get one for $49.98, usually $124.95 with code ALICIA. If you've been following my blog for awhile, you already know that the Pogo Pass is the #1 Alicia's Deal of all time and I hear from so many readers how much fun their families have with it. Pogo Pass is A+ rated with the better business bureau.

They have added new venues to make the passes an even better value (the venues change from time to time and the value is incredible). Check out what's new with your pass: Ice Den Scottsdale/Chandler: One free skate per month during regularly scheduled public skate times. This is usually $9 for adults, skate rental is not included. Wazees World in Peoria: Two visits per year. Choose laser tag (usually $9) or cosmic golf (usually $5). ASU Sun Devil Athletics: Now you can get into ASU Baseball games, gymnastics meets, soccer games, women's basketball games, volleyball games, track and field meets, select softball games, and more with your pass. There are so many games to choose from and this is perfect for the budding sports star who would love to play in college (or for anyone who likes to watch sports)! Places you can still use your passes (subject to change): Phoenix Zoo: You'll enjoy two admissions per year. Since each admission for adults is usually $24.95, this will pay for your pass by itself! Sunsplash: You'll enjoy one general admission to Sunsplash. This is usually $35.99! Arizona Diamondbacks: You'll enjoy seats at two select Arizona Diamondbacks games per year. Choose from Saturday, May 13 vs. Pittsburg Pirates 5:10 PM, Friday, June 23 vs. Philadelphia Phillies 6:40 PM, Friday, July 21 vs. Washington Nationals 6:40 PM, Monday, August 14 vs. Houston Astros 6:40 PM, or Friday, September 22 vs. Miami Marlins 6:40 PM. *The registration button for tickets will appear on this page (2) weeks prior to game day, and close 48 hours prior to the event to allow for tickets to be generated. You must register your Pogo Pass to receive a ticket. Tickets for the Dbacks games are picked up at will call on game day. AZ Airtime: Two (2) visits to AZ Air Time for a One (1) hour jump session. Visits can be used at either of the two locations. Simply present your Pogo Pass at front desk for admission. One hour is usually $14, good Monday-Friday but holidays and special offers excluded (you may want to call ahead before you make a special trip). Must have non slip socks. Phoenix Rock Gym: You'll enjoy two admissions per year. Normal price is $15, gear is available to rent for $5. Skateland: Passholders will get 2 visits a month (skate rental not included)! This is good during normal business hours. Family skate is usually $7.50/person. Fat Cats: Passholders will get one game of glow golf per week and one game of bowling per month (shoe rental not included). The IDEA Museum: Passholders will get two visits to the museum (must be used by the passholder on different days). Admission is usually $8. Arizona Rattlers: *One (1) ticket per game for Three (3) Arizona Rattlers home games. Saturday, March 11 vs Colorado Crush 6:00 PM, Saturday, April 22 vs. Spokane Empire 6:00 PM, or Saturday, May 20 vs. Salt Lake Screaming Eagles 6:00 PM. *The registration button for tickets will appear on this page (2) weeks prior to game day, and close 48 hours prior to the event to allow for tickets to be generated. You must register your Pogo Pass to receive a ticket. Tickets for the Rattlers games are electronic, and will arrive via email in the days leading up to the game. Phoenix Mercury: *One (1) ticket per game, for Three (3) Phoenix Mercury home games. Saturday, May 27th vs. Dallas Wings 7:00 PM, Friday, June 30th vs. Minnesota Lynx 7:00 PM, Friday, July 14th vs. Minnesota Lynx 7:00 PM, or Thursday, Aug 24th vs. LA Sparks 6:00 PM. *The registration button for tickets will appear on this page (2) weeks prior to game day, and close 48 hoursprior to the event to allow for tickets to be generated. You must register your Pogo Pass to receive a ticket. Tickets for the Mercury games are electronic, and will arrive via email in the days leading up to the game. Tempe Paintball: *Each Pogo Pass member can attend (1) Visit to Tempe Paintball per month for Paintball or Nerf Wars. Reservations are required for both Paintball and Nerf Wars. Enchanted Island: Your Pogo Pass is good for two (2) visits to Enchanted Island Amusement Park for an All-Day Ride Pass. Admission to the park is free. Limited capacity, first come first serve. Some rides may require additional charge. An all day ride pass is usually $18.75. Stratum Laser Tag: Pogo Pass holders can attend Stratum Laser Tag two (2) separate times, Pogo Passes may be used on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays from 4:30-8pm. Regular price for one game is $9.99. COMING SOON: Urban Jungle Fun Park Things to know about the Pogo Pass: You can renew each year at a discounted rate and you'll automatically get the available venues for another year (they'll contact you a couple of weeks before your pass expires and let them know if you choose not to renew). Venues may change and passholders will be notified. As new venues are added, your pass will automatically include them. Each person in the family needs their own pass. You can do a fundraiser with Pogo Pass and make extra cash for your organization! Contact me at alicia@dealsinaz.com for more information. These will hopefully get you out and having more fun in our valley of the sun! Enjoy!
