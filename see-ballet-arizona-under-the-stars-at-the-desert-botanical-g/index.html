See Ballet Arizona Under the Stars at the Desert Botanical Garden for Half Off!

Apr 18, 2016 · Alicia Markham

What a fun way to combine two beautiful experiences! Head over here to pick up tickets to see Ballet Arizona perform at the Desert Botanical Garden for $18, usually $36. There is a $5 per ticket fee, and be sure to click on "send Karma codes" if you see the box below. You can send $10 off codes to your friends! Dates are selling out fast!

"Ballet takes on a whole new level of enchantment when performed outdoors, with the desert itself as its stunning backdrop. Ballet Arizona proved this in 2013, when it performed Topia at Phoenix's stunning Desert Botanical Garden, a mesmerizing work designed specifically for the venue and inspired by the natural beauty of the Sonora Desert landscape. Three years later, the company and its acclaimed artistic director Ib Andersen makes a long-awaited return to the venue, for another magical run of dance performances beneath the stars."
