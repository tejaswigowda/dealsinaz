The Dbacks Are BACK And They WON Last Night! Hot Deals You Can Pick Up Today (and How to Get Them After Every Game)!

Apr 8, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Diamondbacks baseball is BACK! We lost our opening day game to the Giants (who won the world series last year), but we WON last night!

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs at home OR AWAY (thanks Mike for the update), the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase. That means today (4/8) is free taco day!

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at Papajohns.com with promo code DBACKS50! That means today (4/8) is half off pizza day!

Anytime a Dback "jacks" a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since we had two big home runs last night, you can take advantage of this deal today (4/8).

Download the Circle K app. After every home game you'll get a deal based on the "strike-outs"! Check out the "deals" in the menu at the top of the app. Today (4/8) you can get a free cookie, brownie, or slice with your app (no purchase required).

The kids and I took Great Grandpa to opening day and had a blast! We are excited for lots of fun games this season.
