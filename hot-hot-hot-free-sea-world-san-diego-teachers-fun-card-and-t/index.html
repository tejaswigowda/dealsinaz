Certified teachers in Arizona can head over here to get a FREE Sea World San Diego Teacher's Fun Card and three extra passes . Teachers must be in the Department of Education database to qualify.

Check out the info:

"SeaWorld San Diego is proud to offer a complimentary Teacher Fun Card plus 3 free Single Day tickets to all active and certified K-12 California and Arizona credentialed school teachers. The Fun Card is valid for unlimited admission to SeaWorld® San Diego through December 31, 2016.

It is easier than ever to get your free Teacher Fun Card!

  1. Click on the green Teacher ID button on the right.
  2. Register online using the ID.me process.
  3. Check out through the SeaWorld San Diego website to receive your Teacher Fun Card and three complimentary tickets.
  4. Online registration and redemption at the park must occur by June 30, 2016."
