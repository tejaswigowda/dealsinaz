You could even use the site to buy gift cards for yourself before you make purchases for yourself!

Do you love Fox Restaurant Concepts? Through December 31st you'll get a free $20 bonus card when you spend $100 on gift cards. You can use the gift cards at Olive and Ivy, True Food, Flower Child, North, The Henry, Zincburger, and their other amazing restaurants. Think about the warehouse stores like Costco and Sams Club. While not everything is a good deal there, gift cards almost always are. They have discounts to places you probably wouldn't think of including restaurants, amusement parks, and lots more.
