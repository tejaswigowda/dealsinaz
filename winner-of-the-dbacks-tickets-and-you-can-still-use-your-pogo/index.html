CONGRATULATIONS to the winner of the Dbacks tickets BRYAN SAUCEDO!

Don't forget that you can use your POGO Passes to get FREE tickets to Friday's game! If you don't have POGO Passes yet, head over here and use promo code ALICIA to get them for just $39.98 each. If you use them everywhere they're good they're worth $1500! Everyone three and over needs their own pass to get into the venues.

Once you have your POGO Passes, click here to reserve your tickets for the September 12th game.

There's a brand new venue coming I think you'll love (and everyone with POGO passes will automatically have the new place added to their passes).

Some of the places the pass is good:

Sunsplash: Each pass holder can attend Golfland Sunsplash one (1) time. Your pass is good for one (1) SunSplash General admission ticket. Simply present your Pogo pass at Sunsplash Guest Service window to be scanned to obtain waterpark wristband. Regular price to get into Sunsplash is $29.99 so this almost pays for your pass!

Sea Life Aquarium: Each Pass holder can attend Arizona Sea life Aquarium two (2) times. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front desk to gain admission to the Aquarium. We did this last week and had a blast!

Phoenix Zoo: Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to the Phoenix Zoo two (2) times. To gain admission to the Zoo, simply present your Pogo Pass at the admission window. Some experiences require an additional fee. Admission to ZooLights and select special events is not included. They have all kinds of fun water stuff around to keep you cool.

Stratum Laser Tag: Pass holders can attend stratum two (2) times. Each pass holder receives one (1) complimentary game of laser tag each time they attend. Pass is good Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday from 4:30-9.

Arizona Diamondbacks: One (1) ticket per game per pass holder for two (2) home games at Chase Field for the MLB Regular Season. Check out the select dates here.

Phoenix Jump Street: Each Pass holder can attend Phoenix Jump Street two (2) times. Pass is good for a one (1) hour jump session each attendance. Pass is valid for twelve (12) months. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front counter to gain admission to Phoenix Jump Street on Monday and Wednesday. Limited capacity, first come first serve.

And LOTS LOTS more! Rattlers games, Brunswick bowling, Phoenix Rock Gym, Enchanted Island, Comedy Theater, Skateland, and Tempe Paintball are all included! Head over here to see everywhere the pass is taken. Don’t forget to use promo code ALICIA to get your passes for $39.98 each.
