Enjoy FREE Admission to the National Parks on Saturday! Also Celebrate National Junior Ranger Day AND Be Sure to Get Your Free Year Pass if You Have a 4th Grader!

2018-04-18 · Alicia

If you don't have plans on Saturday (4/21) keep in mind that it's a fee-free day at the National Parks (in honor of the first day of National Park Week)! Head over here for more information and think about checking out one of our beautiful parks (if you live in AZ and have never been to the Grand Canyon this is a perfect opportunity)!

April 21st is also National Junior Ranger Day. Your kids will have lots of fun becoming junior rangers at each park, they will learn so much too!

If you have a fourth grader this year, don’t forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2018.

My daughter Kaitlyn was in 4th grade last year and we definitely took advantage of the pass. We took a family road trip to Yellowstone in July and it was amazing. We made stops at other national parks along the way too.

Please share with anyone you know with a 4th grader or 4th grade teachers!
