FREE Cupcakes for "A's" for Students from Sprinkles in September and Free Cupcakes for Teachers!

Sep 9, 2014 · Alicia Markham

What a fun freebie from Sprinkles! Head to the Scottsdale location with your kids Tuesdays in September. If they bring in an "A" on a paper or test, they'll get a FREE cupcake (no purchase required)! This is good 9/9, 9/16, 9/23, and 9/30. I asked since my kids' teachers never seem to write "A" on a paper (they write 98/100, 19/20, etc.) and they said that anything equal to an A will work too!

They're not leaving the teachers out! Each Monday in September, teachers can flash their official school ID for a free cupcake when they buy one.

Enjoy!
