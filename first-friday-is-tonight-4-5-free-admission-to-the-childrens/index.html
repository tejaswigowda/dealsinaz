First Friday is TONIGHT (4/5)! Free Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Heard Museum, and the Phoenix Art Museum!

Apr 5, 2019 · Alicia

TODAY (April 5th) is the First Friday of the month and that means it’s a GREAT night to head to downtown Phoenix for free fun.

Admission is FREE to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM. Head over here for more information.

Admission to the Phoenix Art Museum is free April 5th from 6-10 PM. Head over here for more details. The DanceFest is tonight 6:30-10 and will feature performances from high school and college students along with professional dancers. Should be a great time!

The Heard Museum is offering free admission tonight (4/5) from 6-10 PM. Head over here for more information and for all of the special events going on.
