Cardinals Won Big...Free Dunkin Hot or Iced Coffee, Half Price Sonic Drinks and Slushes, Free Jumbo Jack, and More!

Sep 19, 2016 · Alicia Markham

The Cardinals won big yesterday 40-7 and that means deals for ALL of us!

Dunkin’ Donuts will give you a free medium hot or iced coffee coupon on their app the day after a win, look under Perks! I had to update my app for the coupon to show up and you must allow it to see your location (as this is a regional specific offer). I will DEFINITELY be taking advantage of this one!

Feel like Sonic? Head to your closest participating location to get drinks or slushes for half off the day after every Cardinals win!

Love Jumbo Jacks? Head to your closest participating Jack in the Box to get one for free with the purchase of a large drink the day after the Cardinals have a sack (win or lose). I just checked and the team had one!

Feel like Papa Johns pizza? Head over here and use code CARDINALS50 to get half off your regular menu price order today and the day after every Cardinals win!
