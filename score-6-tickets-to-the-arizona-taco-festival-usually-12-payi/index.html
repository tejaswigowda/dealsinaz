Score $6 Tickets to the Arizona Taco Festival (Usually $12)! Paying Half Price is So Much Sweeter!

Oct 2, 2018 · Alicia

Any fellow taco lovers?! Head over here to score tickets to the Arizona Taco Festival coming October 13th and 14th to Salt River Fields for just $6 each on Local Dines (one of my favorite half price dining sites EVER)!

Check out the details:

"Ticket Offer from LocalDines.com

Gift certificates from LocalDines.com are valid for a single day General Admission pass to Taco-topia on either Saturday, October 13th, 2018 (11AM-7PM) or Sunday, October 14th, 2018 (11AM-7PM). Just purchase (and print!) your certificate(s) and show up on the day of your choice! You will be treated to live bands, lucha libre wrestling shows, chef cooking demonstrations, hilarious on-stage contests, and access to more $2 tacos than you’ve ever seen! Many of the taco teams are serving tacos available ONLY at the Taco Festival, so this is pretty much the most important event ever!

Tacos, you say?

A General Admission ticket gets you access to Taco-topia. Inside the event, you'll find about 50 restaurants dishing out $2 tacos and bars and beverage stations serving margaritas, beer, water, and soda. Tokens are used throughout the festival – purchase them to use for tacos, drinks, and other items.

Parking

Parking will be free, but limited at Salt River Fields. We highly recommend carpooling or taking alternative transportation to the festival. Come early for free onsite parking or take advantage of additional free parking lots near the festival grounds with a fast and free shuttle service to the festival entrance. Check the Arizona Taco Festival website 1 week prior to the event for a detailed parking map and make a plan of action. Disabled parking: go directly to Salt River Fields and alert parking attendants upon arrival.

Pets?

Sorry, no dogs allowed - except service dogs, and Chihuahua Beauty Pageant participants (of course!)

Note: Purchaser's name and number of tickets will be at held at the Arizona Taco Festival entrance. ID is REQUIRED. Entire party must be present to gain entry. Please PRINT your LocalDines.com certificate(s) and bring to event. Certificates presented via mobile phone can't be accepted. LocalDines.com certificate(s) must be surrendered in exchange for General Admission entry, on either Saturday, October 13th, 2018 (11AM-7PM) or Sunday, October 14th, 2018 (11AM-7PM). Children 12 and under may enter for free! Must be 21+ to drink alcohol."
