Saturday, September 25th is National Public Lands Day and Everyone Gets in Free to the National Parks! If You Live in AZ and Have Never Been to the Grand Canyon, This is an Excellent Opportunity!

Sep 22, 2021 · Alicia#EXPIRED

Saturday, September 25th is National Public Lands Day and it's one of the free entrance days for national parks! If you can't make it this weekend, mark your calendar for Thursday, November 11th...that's the next fee-free day.

I know a lot of Arizona residents who have NEVER BEEN to the Grand Canyon. This is a great chance to see it for free (a private vehicle is usually $35)!
