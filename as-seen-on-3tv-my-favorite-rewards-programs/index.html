As Seen on 3TV: My FAVORITE Rewards Programs!

2014-11-04 · Alicia Markham

Thanks so much for tuning into Good Morning Arizona today! I hope you’ll “like” Alicia’s Deals on facebook, follow me on Twitter, and sign up for my daily e-mail list. I do my best to help you “live life better for less” every single day.

Stores and restaurants appreciate loyalty and many have great loyalty programs that will reward you for it.

Embedded from ad.doubleclick.net

Dunkin' Donuts are popping even more up all over town and they have a great DD Perks program. Click above to get signed up, when you load your app with at least $2 you'll get a totally free medium beverage and access to all kinds of fun perks. Every time the Cardinals win you'll get a free medium hot or iced coffee the next day. You'll also get a free medium beverage on your birthday and every time you hit 200 points. Each dollar you spend is worth 5 points. If you use wi-fi like I do, I've been very happy with their wi-fi service.

Starbucks has a great rewards program. Head over here to register your gift card or download the app. You'll get a free birthday drink or treat plus lots of fun offers. Once you hit green status you'll start getting free coffee or tea refills (love my iced green tea unsweetened). Once you hit gold status, they'll mail you a special gold card and you'll earn any free drink or food item for every 12 stars.

VERY IMPORTANT: Each transaction counts as a star, how much you buy makes no difference. If you have four drinks and a cookie, I would check out five separate times. You'll get five stars instead of one.

Head over here to join the Shell Fuel Rewards Network. You'll earn a few cents off each gallon of gas when you use your card. You can use the rewards along with Fry's Fuel points (which is awesome).

You already know how much I love Kohl's and now they have a new loyalty program (I love them even more now). Head over here to get signed up for Yes2You Rewards. You'll get a $5 reward for every 100 points (earn 1 point per $1 no matter how you pay).

If you have babies, you'll love this rewards program.
