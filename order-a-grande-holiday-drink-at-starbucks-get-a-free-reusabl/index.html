Order a Grande Holiday Drink at Starbucks Get a FREE Reusable Red Cup Today Only (11/7)!

Nov 7, 2019 · Alicia

The holiday drinks are BACK at Starbucks! Today only (11/7) you'll get a FREE reusable red cup when you buy a grande holiday drink while supplies last (you may want to call your location to make sure they still have cups before you make a special trip, mine has 20 left). Head over here for more information on their facebook page. The holiday drink choices this year are Peppermint Mocha, Caramel Brulée Latte and Toasted White Chocolate Mocha. I just called my Starbucks and they said you get $.50 off your drink if you bring in your cup after 2 PM, $.10 off before that (not sure if that's consistent at all Starbucks).

My favorite is definitely the Caramel Brulée Latte what is yours?
