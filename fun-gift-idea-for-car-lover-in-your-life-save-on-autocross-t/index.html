Fun Gift Idea for Car Lover in Your Life...Save Big on Autocross Tickets (Drive Exotic Cars With No Speed Limit)!

Dec 14, 2018 · Alicia

If you have a car lover in your life who needs nothing, this could be lots of fun. Head over here to save huge on autocross tickets. You'll pay $79-$139 plus fees depending on the option you choose instead of $149-$259.

"This will definitely get your engine running! Choose any high-performance dream car from Exotic Auto Experiences’ rental fleet and embark on their newest autocross adventure located at Arizona Motorsports Park. After choosing one of their Ferraris, Lamborghinis, Aston Martin, McLaren, or Nissan GTR, a course expert will train you to properly and safely operate your Super Car. The course leads you through straightaways, twists and turns at top speeds. In fact, there's no speed limit at all, so you can drive as fast or as slow as comfortable. Those age 9 or older can enjoy a ride-along experience with a course expert. Feel free to bring friends and family members as spectators to cheer you on, shoot pics and video the experience. Any way you slice it, you'll be in for the ride of your life."

The no speed limit part will get some people going!
