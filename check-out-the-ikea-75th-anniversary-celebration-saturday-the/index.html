Check Out the IKEA 75th Anniversary Celebration Saturday...The First 75 Ikea Family Members Get a $20 Gift Card!

Aug 15, 2018 · Alicia

If you love Ikea you may want to celebrate their 75th Anniversary on Saturday (8/18). They'll be giving away $20 Ikea gift cards to the first 75 Ikea Family members in line (gift card line starts at 9 AM). It's free to join Ikea Family, head over here to sign up if you haven't already.

Here's the information they sent me:

"Join us in-store for a fun weekend of activities and giveaways.

To celebrate, we’re bringing back some timeless favorites from the 50’s and 60s in our classic GRATULERA collection.

GRATULERA products available in store only starting on August 15, 2018, while supplies last.

Celebration date and time:

Saturday, August 18 through Sunday, August 19, 2018 9:30am-4pm. Times may vary by location,see local store for details. Not all stores may participate.

Gift card giveaway:

The first 75 IKEA FAMILY members at each store on Saturday, August 18 will receive a $20 IKEA Gift Card. See your local store for complete gift card giveaway rules and regulations. Gift card line up begins Saturday, August 18 at 9am.

Scan-to-win contest:

IKEA FAMILY members can scan their card to be entered for a chance to win one of ten $75 IKEA Gift Cards per store. Plus, one lucky national IKEA FAMILY member who scans their card will win a $750 IKEA Gift Card!

GRATULERA products not available online. At participating US stores only. Additional exclusions, terms and conditions may apply. Scan-to-win contest: IKEA FAMILY members can scan their card at a kiosk, check-out or IKEA Restaurant to be entered for a chance to win one of ten $75 IKEA Gift Cards per participating store. Not all IKEA stores may participate in event or offers. IKEA Paramus is closed on Sundays. Not all products may be available online or in all stores. See what is available at your local store by visiting IKEA-USA.com/stockavailability. Valid in the US only."

Enjoy!
