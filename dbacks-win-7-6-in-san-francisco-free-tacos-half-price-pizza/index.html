Dbacks Win 7-6 in San Francisco! Free Tacos, Half Price Pizza, and Free Jumbo Jacks Friday to Celebrate!

Apr 17, 2015 · Alicia Markham

The Dbacks beat the Giants in San Francisco Thursday night 7-6 in 12 innings (I stayed up for all 12 believe it or not)!

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. That means Friday (4/17) is free taco day!

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at Papajohns.com with promo code DBACKS50! That means Friday (4/17) is half off pizza day!

Anytime a Dback “jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since there were some huge home runs, you can take advantage of this deal Friday (4/17).

Let's keep winning Dbacks!
