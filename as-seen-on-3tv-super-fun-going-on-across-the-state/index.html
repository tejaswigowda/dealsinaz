As Seen on 3TV...FREE Super Fun Going On Across the Valley!

Jan 27, 2015 · Alicia Markham

The biggest game in the world is going on right in our own backyard and there are lots of fun, FREE events available to us to experience some of the excitement.

Head over here for more information about Verizon Super Bowl Central. This is a FREE event in downtown Phoenix. There will be lots of fun, live entertainment, even a Grand Canyon Experience rock climbing wall (there is a $5 charge to climb). The event starts on Wednesday.

The Glendale Chocolate Affaire is coming to Murphy Park Friday and Saturday. It's sponsored by Arizona's own Cerreta's Candy Company. This FREE event features over 40 chocolate purveyors and they promise lots of chocolate footballs and other big-game type sweets.

If you love music, head to the Super Music Series going on right outside University of Phoenix stadium in Glendale. The event goes January 28th-February 1st and is free! There will be free parking too, every day except game day. Click here for more information and for a list of bands that will be playing.

If you're hosting a party at home (or need something great to bring), head to Fired Pie. They're offering your 5th pizza for free that you get to design yourself!

Click here for more information.

Enjoy!
