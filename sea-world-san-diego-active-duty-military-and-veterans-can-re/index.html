Sea World San Diego: Active Duty Military and Veterans Can Request Up to Four FREE Tickets!

Apr 28, 2021 · Alicia

If you have any Veterans or Active Duty Military members in your life, please tell them about this deal from Sea World San Diego. Through May 16th Veterans can head over here to register to get up to four FREE admission tickets (must use before June 27th) For Active Duty Military men and women the deal is good through the end of the year.

Check out the Covid restrictions:

"Who is allowed to visit the park under the current CA state guidelines?

Attendance is limited to CA in-state visitors and out-of-state visitors who are fully vaccinated* and show proof of a fully completed COVID-19 vaccine (you are considered fully vaccinated two weeks after your second shot, or two weeks after the one-dose vaccine) per state COVID-19 restrictions. Please continue to monitor our website for updates on these restrictions and availability for non-California residents. CA in-state visitors may be asked to present proof of residency upon entry. Party sizes may not contain more than 3 households. To adhere to capacity guidelines and for the health and safety of our guests and employees, reservations are required to enter the park. SeaWorld will continue to operate with enhanced health and safety protocols for cleaning and sanitizing, physical distancing, face-covering requirements, and temperature screenings."

If you're vaccinated and would love a trip, enjoy!
