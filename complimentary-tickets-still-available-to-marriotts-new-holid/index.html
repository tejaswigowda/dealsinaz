Complimentary Tickets Still Available to Marriott's New Holiday Great Skate!

Dec 11, 2015 · Alicia Markham

How fun! Head over here to score COMP kids tickets to the Great Skate at the Marriott (there is a $3 ticket fee)! Adult tickets are available at a big discount too. Even better, you can send karma codes to your friends for $10 off (look for the box below).

The $10 off Karma codes CAN be used toward ticket fees so this could really be a 100% FREE activity for the kiddos (or just $1 for one adult and one kid).

Check out the details:

“Besides toasty holiday snacks like hot cocoa and s’mores by the firepit, and the chance to skate around a 2,400-square-foot outdoor rink, there’s an even greater reason to bring the whole family to the first annual Holiday Great Skate at Marriott: You’ll be gliding over a synthetic eco-friendly surface! Skate under the sun or stars — or stay all day and experience both — and join the fun for a new Scottsdale holiday tradition. Your ticket includes skate rentals, and a holiday menu will be available with food and drink treats for purchase.”

  • This voucher is valid for one admission on any date from December 9, 2015 – January 3, 2016. This voucher expires on January 3, 2016.
  • Skate rentals are included.
  • Sales can end at any time at the ticket supplier’s request or when tickets are sold out. Sales are set to end on Saturday, January 2, 2016 at 11:55pm MST.
  • * Ticket quantities are limited.
