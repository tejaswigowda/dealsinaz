ZooLights

As the Valley’s brightest holiday tradition, ZooLights at the Phoenix Zoo features hundreds of light displays illuminating the night sky. A three-story lighted tree highlights the many glimmering displays while the music-in-motion show features a wealth of light exhibits synchronized with holiday music. Meanwhile, guests can take photos with Santa while also experiencing a simulated snowfall every night.

Dinosaurs in the Desert will be open during ZooLights and features lifelike animatronic dinosaurs, a fossil dig for kids, photo ops, and more. Additional fees apply.
