It's Dbacks Opening Day (7/24) if it's definitely going to be different without fans! I was happy to find today that we still have deals to look forward to, head over here to check out the Updates page on their site for the 2020 season!

Papa John's DBACKS50

When the D-backs win at home or away, fans get 50% off all pizzas at regular menu price at papajohns.com the next day when they use promo code DBACKS50. Cannot be combined with any other offer. THIS OFFER VALID FOR PIZZA ONLY.

Promotions are subject to change and may not occur at every game.
