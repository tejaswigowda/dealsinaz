Lights of the World Lantern Festival

In the past, USA Today ranked this festival as one of the top 5 over-the-top Christmas lights from across the U.S. Now, back for their third consecutive year, Lights of the World Lantern Festival has combined the age-old tradition of paper lanterns with cutting edge technology for completely new and bright displays. There will be over 65 different lantern displays that extend from 18 to 60 feet, amounting to over 10 million colorful light that showcase different cultures and civilizations from around the globe. Additional entertainment like roaming dinosaurs, carnival rides, acrobatics, and hand-made art will also be available throughout the festival. Come enjoy the largest lights and lantern festival in North America as they brightening Phoenix’s skies from December 6, 2018 through February 3, 2018 at the Arizona State Fair.
