Pick Up Your Discount Tickets to the Larry Fitzgerald Celebrity Softball Game Before They're Gone!

Mar 21, 2019 · Alicia

Head over here to pick up your discount tickets to the Larry Fitzgerald celebrity softball game going on April 27th at Salt River Fields. You'll pay $14/ticket instead of $23.05.

"What You'll Get

  • General admission
  • Free admission for children 2 and under
  • Gates open at 5 p.m. | Home Run Derby at 6 p.m. | Game starts at 7 p.m.

2019 Larry Fitzgerald Celebrity Softball Game

  • Who’s in charge: veteran Arizona Cardinals receiver Larry Fitzgerald, a nine-time Pro Bowler who has racked up more than 1,000 catches and 13,000 yards in his career
  • Who else participates: 2018 saw big names like Brian Urlacher and Brandon Marshall, stay tuned for a full list of 2019’s celebrity participants
  • Whom it benefits: Fitzgerald’s own First Down Fund, an effort that provides support to organizations across the country looking to provide positive activities for kids so they don’t spend every day after school throwing rocks at their homework
  • What to expect: Celebrity athletes will launch balls into orbit during a Home Run Derby at 6 p.m., with the actual game beginning at 7 p.m. Fans in attendance will also have the chance to collect autographs and bid on memorabilia."
