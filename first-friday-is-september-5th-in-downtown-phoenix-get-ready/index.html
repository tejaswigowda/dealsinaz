First Friday is September 5th in Downtown Phoenix! Get Ready for Free Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Heard Museum, and the Phoenix Art Museum!

First Friday is this week in downtown Phoenix! If you haven't been before, the promoters describe it as: "A neighborhood celebration with art, street vendors, live music, entertainment, amazing food, great craft beer, wine & much more! No cover." One of my favorite things about First Friday is the opportunity to visit three of AZ's best museums FREE OF CHARGE!

The Children's Museum of Phoenix: Admission is free for all on September 5th from 5-9 PM. Take the kids for an exciting evening of fun and learning! Admission is usually $19 for everyone 1 and over...a family of 4 will save $76! Mark your calendar...admission will also be free on October 3rd!

The Heard Museum: Admission is free for all on September 5th from 4-8 PM. Admission is normally $26 for adults and $10 for children.

"Celebrate art, culture, and community with an evening full of inspiration and connection...all of our galleries will be open--including two standout exhibitions you won't want to miss."

"Come early and stay cool as you explore the museum, make memories in the Heardlings Family Activity Room, and stop by The Museum Shop for beautifully crafted items by Indigenous artists and authors. Our Coffee Cantina will also be open until 7:30 p.m. for refreshing Prickly Pear Lemonade, snacks, and adult beverages to enjoy under the twinkly lights outside the museum entrance."

Phoenix Art Museum: General admission is free for all on September 5th from 5-8 PM. "Separate tickets are required to view special exhibition The World of Anna Sui...at an additional cost. Click HERE to purchase special exhibition tickets."

"Featured programming includes:

· DIY zine workshop with Wasted Ink Distro

Create your own magazine inspired by punk, rock-and-roll, and other genres and cultural influences that have inspired fashion designer Anna Sui for decades

· Create your own accessory workshop with There Space

Use art nouveau motifs, bohemian patterns, and more to channel your own inner rock-star aesthetic

· Outfit inspo consultations with fashion designer and artist Leonor Aispuro

Learn how to spruce up any outfit drawing inspiration from nature and sustainable fashion sources.

· Pop-up shops with zines and accessories by Wasted Ink Distro and Artdillo Eclectic

· DIY ‘90s slap bracelet activity

· Posing + photoshoot with Mackenzie Rich from Afternoon Studios

· In-gallery sketching with Aileen Martinez

· Nostalgic ‘80s and ‘90s hits with resident DJ JME-LEE

· Drinks with ARTenders

· Docent-led Public Tours @ 5:30 pm + 6:30 pm"

Enjoy!
