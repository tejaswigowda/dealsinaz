Get Into the National Parks FREE This Weekend! There Are So Many to See in AZ!

Apr 17, 2015 · Alicia Markham

If you don't have a lot going on this weekend, it could be a perfect time to check out the beautiful National Parks our state has to offer. It always makes me sad when I hear about people who have lived here for awhile and have NEVER been to the Grand Canyon!
