Deal on a New Holiday Event...Illumination! I Can't Wait for This One!

Oct 12, 2017 · Alicia Markham

There's a new drive thru holiday light show coming to town called Illumination! It's over a mile long, has millions of lights, and should be spectacular! Right now you can head over here to get a car full of people in for $19.95 Monday-Thursday, usually $29.95! It's located just off the I-17 and Jomax.

"About Illumination Drive Thru Light Show

Illumination Drive Thru Light Show immerses visitors in one of the Southwest's largest drive-thru, holiday light shows. With millions of lights synchronized to holiday tunes, the show creates a spirited wonderland for individuals of any age to enjoy.

  • Home to the largest holiday ornaments in the country, including a 30' light up snow man and a 30' sphere Christmas tree; the only one in the US.
  • After the show - The Holiday Boulevard is a park, play and eat area that culminates the drive-thru experience with an interactive walking family area of kid’s jumper land, food vendors, selfie stations, Santa's Village and other holiday memory-making experiences. It can be accessed directly after the drive-through light display Illumination attraction with an easy right-hand turn into the parking area designated especially for The Holiday Boulevard area.
  • The food and drink - Strolling down the holiday boulevard, families can have a bite to eat and enjoy a popular holiday treat.
