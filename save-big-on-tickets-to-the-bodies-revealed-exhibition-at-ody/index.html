Save Big on Tickets to the Bodies Revealed Exhibition at OdySea!

Oct 23, 2018 · Alicia

Head over here to save big on tickets to the Bodies Revealed exhibition at OdySea! Adults will pay $15 for admission and the audio tour (usually $24.95) and kids will pay $11 for the same thing (usually $19.95).

"What You'll Get

  • What’s included: admission to BODIES REVEALED Exhibition with audio tour
  • Adult tickets valid for customers ages 13–64; child tickets valid for customers ages 2–12
  • Free admission for children under 2

BODIES REVEALED

BODIES REVEALED gives people an inside look into the human body. Real anatomical specimens are preserved and respectfully displayed in nine galleries. This provides an up-close view of the skeletal, muscular, reproductive, respiratory, and circulatory systems of the human body. For additional information visit the Exhibition’s website to learn more about this limited time exhibition in Scottsdale, Arizona. Location is adjacent to OdySea Aquarium and Butterfly Wonderland."

I've talked to a few people who LOVED this! They felt it gave their kids (and themselves) a good understanding of the human body. Check out the reviews, some thought it was too small.
