HOT HOT HOT! Discounted Lights of the World Tickets Are BACK and You Can Get an Extra 20% Off (Pay $11.20 Instead of $19)!

Nov 30, 2017 · Alicia Markham

I love that this is BACK and you can score an extra 20% off! Head over here to score a ticket to the Lights of the World Lantern Festival for $14, usually $19. Use promo code SALE3 (expires tonight, 11/30) to get the price down to $11.20. This is good anytime they’re open (November 16th through January 2nd from 5 PM to 10 PM, closed on December 26th).

Note: I did hear from some readers that you have to pay for parking ($10) and food, rides, and admission to the acrobats are extra fees.

“Lights of the World Lantern Festival combines an ancient Chinese tradition with modern-day technology. The showcase displays more than 70 brilliant displays, amounting to over 10 million colorful lights. As visitors walk through the multicolored masterpieces they’ll encounter a world of surprises, including friendly, life-like dinosaurs and the illuminations of a Christmas-themed dream. The family friendly event includes live acrobatic shows and international cuisine as well as carnival rides and games. Artisans will also be on hand, creating unique, handmade art to commemorate the celebration.”

This is going on at the Arizona State Fairgrounds.
