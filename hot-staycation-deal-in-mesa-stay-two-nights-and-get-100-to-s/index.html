Things to do in Mesa:

Desert Belle Cruises: "Sit back and relax on this 90-minute narrated cruise where you'll see towering canyon walls, dramatic desert vistas, and exotic Arizona wildlife. Listen as the captain describes the unique history of Saguaro Lake and the surrounding area. Don't forget your camera and binoculars." I went on this several years ago and had a great time!

The Arizona Commemorative Air Force Museum: "Commemorative Air Force Airbase Arizona is dedicated to preserving the history of those who served in military aviation. Worthwhile exhibits and presentations are more than hardware or weapons — they are about the people who went in harm’s way or who rose to countless challenges behind the front lines or from the home front." This is the #1 rated thing to do in Mesa on Trip Advisor, head over here to check out reviews from people who have been.

Cosmos Salon and Med Spa: If a day at the spa sounds fantastic, you may want to request your $100 gift card to Cosmos in Mesa. They offer hair and nail services, facials, peels, lash services, even Botox! It's a beautiful salon located in a beautiful part of Mesa very close to Las Sendas.

Featuring the largest Wurlitzer Theater Pipe Organ in the world, the master musicians play everything from Bach to rock while customers dine on delicious pizza, pasta, salad, soft drinks, beer, wine and delicious ice cream!"

The Original Blue Adobe Grill: They say this is "the place you hope to find in your travels." Lucky for us it's close by! Delicious!
