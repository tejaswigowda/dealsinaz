Check Out ASU Art Museum Family Days on Saturday! FREE and Tons of Fun Activities!

Feb 10, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you're looking for something fun and FREE to do on Saturday, think about checking out ASU Art Museum's Family Days! From 10AM-4PM kids 4-12 can enjoy a great time making their own art and seeing some amazing works.

Check out the info (or head over here to learn more):

"Stop by the ASU Art Museum any time on February 11 th , from 10 am to 4 pm for a day of art making and family fun! The event is free!

We will have eight different art making stations for fun art making including:

Neon tab bracelets

Basket weaving

Heart and star wooden magnets

Robot masks

Screen printing with designs made by John McVay

Mandalas

Buttons

Ceramic Pendants

Plus two artist lead activities!

Join Dressler Parsons, a Tempe based intermedia artist and explore ASU campus with your ears on 20 minute guided walks.

Have fun with Bobby Zokaites and create an inflatable sculpture with your family."
