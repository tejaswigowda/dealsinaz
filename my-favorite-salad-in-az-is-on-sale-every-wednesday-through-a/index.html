My Favorite Salad in AZ is On Sale Every Wednesday Through August...Find It at The Gladly, Citizen Public House, and Beginner's Luck!

Jun 25, 2024 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona#Restaurants

Arizona is full of outstanding salads and one of my all-time favorites is the Original Chopped Salad. Check out the yummy ingredients...Smoked Salmon, Couscous, Arugula, Pepitas, Asiago Cheese, Currants, Dried Sweet Corn, Tomatoes, and Buttermilk-Herb Dressing. It's beautifully presented and available at three great AZ restaurants...The Gladly, Citizen Public House, and Beginner's Luck. This salad is usually $18 but every Wednesday through August you can get it for just $12 (dine-in, take-out, or delivery).

I went to The Gladly for lunch today and our server said I was the only customer she'd met who knew about the deal. I knew I had to post about it. so you would know about it too... a hump day treat to look forward to!

This is the perfect meal for our AZ heat!

Enjoy!
