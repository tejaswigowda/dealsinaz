FREE Burrito at Pancheros With App!

Oct 27, 2016 · Alicia Markham

What a great freebie! Head over here to get a FREE burrito at Pancheros when you download their app for Apple or Android.

The free burrito will be in the rewards section, and if you use invite code AliciaFo8143 you'll get 20 extra points (or ask a friend for their code and you'll still get 20 extra points and they'll get free chips and queso....those aren't on my diet plan these days unfortunately)! Sign up with facebook you'll get another 25 points! For every 100 points you earn, you'll get a free entree.

The free burrito expires after seven days.

Their AZ location is near Scottsdale Road and the 202 in Tempe. Check out a map here.
