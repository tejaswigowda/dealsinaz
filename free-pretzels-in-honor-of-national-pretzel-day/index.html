FREE Pretzels in Honor of National Pretzel Day!

Apr 24, 2015 · Alicia Markham

National Pretzel Day is definitely a holiday I can get behind!

If you love pretzels, head to Wetzels on Sunday, April 26th! They’re hositng National Wetzel Day (which happens to be on National Pretzel Day)!

Check out the details on how to get your FREE original pretzel:

“Calling all Wetzel’s: our family reunion is next Sunday! Come in April 26 for your free original pretzel. Not a Wetzel (yet)? Start practicing this saying: “My name is ____ Wetzel, and I want my pretzel!” (US only, Terms & Conditions: http://bit.ly/NWD2015)”

If you have the Auntie Anne's Pretzel Perks app, check it for a FREE original pretzel this weekend! Head over here for more information.

If you're an Alicia's Deals reader in Flagstaff or Tucson, head to Preztelmaker (they're not in the Phoenix area currently) with a tongue twister on Sunday. You'll get a free soft pretzel! Head over here for more information.

Enjoy (how can you not?)!
