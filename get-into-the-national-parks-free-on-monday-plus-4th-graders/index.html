Get Into the National Parks FREE on Monday! Plus 4th Graders Get Their Families in Free All Year!

Jan 12, 2018 · Alicia Markham

Do you have Monday off for Martin Luther King Jr. day? You may want to take the kids to a National Park...admission is FREE in honor of the holiday 1/15.

If you can't do Monday, head over here for a list of the other fee-free days this year (4/21, 9/22, and 11/11).

If you have a fourth grader this year, don’t forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2018.

My daughter Kaitlyn was in 4th grade last year and we definitely took advantage of the pass. We took a family road trip to Yellowstone in July and it was amazing. We made stops at other national parks along the way too.

Please share with anyone you know with a 4th grader or 4th grade teachers!
