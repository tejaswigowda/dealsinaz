The Dbacks won both games of a double header Wednesday in Colorado! The first game was 13-7, the second game was 5-1. They've been rained out the past couple of days so it's nice to have a day FULL of wins!

We can all celebrate with deals Thursday!

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. Anytime a Dback “jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since there was a home run. you can take advantage of this deal Thursday (5/7).

Enjoy the deals!
