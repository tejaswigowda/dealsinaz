California Pizza Kitchen is Giving Away 10,000 Free Take and Bake Pizzas Through Grubhub Tuesday!

Oct 1, 2019 · Alicia

Update: I ordered my free pizza and they cancelled it. Not sure why but I did get $20 in Grubhub bucks.

Tuesday is a great day for free CPK Take and Bake Pizzas! Head over here to find out the details on the CPK facebook page. Starting at 11 AM Tuesday we can all request our freebie (Margarita, Pepperoni, or BBQ Chicken).

Head over here for all of the details!

"To participate in the 10,000 free CPK Take and Bake Pizza giveaway, guests can download the Grubhub app or visit www.Grubhub.com and choose from one of the following Take and Bake Pizza varieties on hand-tossed dough: The Original BBQ Chicken Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza or Margherita Pizza. There is no minimum purchase required and no delivery fee. The giveaway starts at 11 a.m. local time of your preferred CPK location and is available at all CPK

locations currently listed on the Grubhub app. Guests are encouraged to share their National Pizza Month celebration with #CPKTakeAndBake on Twitter and Instagram."
