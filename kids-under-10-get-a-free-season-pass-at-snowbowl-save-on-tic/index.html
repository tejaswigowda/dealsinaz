Kids Under 10 Get a FREE Season Pass at Snowbowl! Save on Tickets for Everyone Else and Rentals Too!

Dec 31, 2018 · Alicia

Rain here means snow in Flagstaff! If you're excited to get on the slopes and have kids under 10, head over here to pick up FREE season passes for them. If you plan ahead you can head over here and buy everyone else in your party lift tickets for a huge discount (prices start at $35)!

We went up the day after Christmas and had a great time!

Another savings tip...rent your equipment at Snow Mountain River on the way up. It was $25 for skis, boots, and poles, and $5 for a helmet. The people were really nice, head over here for more information!
