Reminder: Celebrate Teacher Appreciation Day With Deals!

May 3, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Teacher Appreciation Day is May 3rd this year (TOMORROW) and I’m trying to put together all of the deals I can find to celebrate the WONDERFUL teachers our kids learn from! Thank you so much for all you do!

Chipotle: “In honor of Teacher Appreciation Day, we’re giving all educators a BOGO. Teachers, faculty and staff: bring in your school ID to any Chipotle in the U.S. on Tuesday, May 3rd, 2016 from 3pm–close to receive a BUY-ONE/GET-ONE BURRITOS, BOWLS, SALADS, or ORDER OF TACOS. “

Organ Stop Pizza: Teachers can “come in to Organ Stop Pizza in Mesa between May 2 and May 6th, 2016 during regular business hours, (5 p.m. – 9 p.m.), and get a gift card for a free pizza*. Any size pizza, no limit on toppings. Value up to $20.

Must be an employed K-12 teacher and show valid school ID. (Working at a school does not qualify).

*Free Pizzas are for dine-in only.

*Free pizza coupon may be redeemed immediately or on a future visit.

*Coupons expire 8/31/16″

World of Beer: “World of Beer (WOB) taverns nationwide are offering teachers a free Angry Orchard cider or Sam Adams beer on Teacher Appreciation Day, May 3. Whether guests are coming for a drink after the bell rings or thinking about pairing their brew during dinner plans, WOB wants to reward America’s educators by inviting them to enjoy a cold one at locations across the country by presenting a valid teacher ID. Guests in Arizona, Texas, North Carolina, Ohio, Virginia and New York can anticipate five dollars off their check in lieu of a free beer.

“At World of Beer, we want to recognize and reward our educators for their work and devotion to shaping minds,” said Paul Avery, president and CEO of World of Beer. “We hope that teachers across the country pencil us into their plans on May 3 and join us in raising a glass to all their hard work.”

Papa Murphy’s: On Monday, May 2, 2016, Papa Murphy’s will kickstart Teacher Appreciation Week with a very special offer. Teachers can take 50% off a pizza with a teacher ID. From open to close on Monday, May 2nd only— get half-off any pizza, any size at regular price—excludes mini-murph, sides and desserts at all participating Valley locations. Hours may vary per store. Go to www.papamurphys.com for a location near you.

McDonalds: “Phoenix and northern Arizona McDonald’s restaurants will thank local teachers with a special Teacher Appreciation Day offer available only through the McDonald’s Mobile App. The Mobile App one-day offer for a “Free Medium McCafé Beverage” will only be valid for 24 hours on Teacher Appreciation Day, May 3!

Phoenix and northern Arizona teachers are encouraged to download the McDonald’s Mobile App and register to receive the free offer at participating McDonald’s restaurants. No purchase is necessary.

“We appreciate the hard work and dedication of our teachers and hope they download our convenient Mobile App to enjoy a free McCafé beverage on Teacher Appreciation Day,” said Michelle Slayton, president of McDonald’s Owner/Operator Association of Phoenix and Northern Arizona. “Whether it’s our freshly made coffee or our cool smoothies, rich shakes or a tasty frappé, we’ve got something guaranteed to make their day.”

Teachers will receive their choice of a McCafé beverage. The McCafé menu includes: Hot and Iced Coffee; Hot Mochas, Lattes, and Premium Hot Chocolate; Iced Mochas and Frappes; Real Fruit Smoothies; and McCafé Shakes.” I’ve heard this free drink will be available on the app for all of us (not just teachers)!

Fluid Martial Arts Academy (Tempe): “Teachers & school staff and their families train free for the entire months of May and June at Fluid Martial Arts. New members only.

We offer: Kenpo Karate, Brazilian Jiu Jitsu(ground fighting), Judo(take downs), Muay Thai kickboxing, wrestling.

Classes offered:

• 3-5 year olds in our Tiny Tigers(we have 8 cute little ones)

• 6-15 Youth program

• Adult classes

• Day and evening classes available

• Open Monday thru Saturday”
