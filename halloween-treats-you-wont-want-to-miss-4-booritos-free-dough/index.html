Halloween Treats You Won't Want to Miss ($4 Booritos, Free Doughnuts, and More)!

Oct 31, 2019 · Alicia

We can ALL enjoy Halloween treats this year no matter how old we are!

Chipotle: Stop in to get a $4 entree when you wear a costume after 3 PM (boo-ritos anyone?!).

Baskin Robbins: Halloween happens to fall on the 31st so that means we can stop into Baskin Robbins for $1.70 scoops or pick up two prepackaged quarts for $9.99.

Sonic: Sonic is getting in the spooky spirit with $.50 corn dogs all day!

Krispy Kreme: Anyone who comes in with a costume on gets a FREE doughnut!

Edible Arrangements: Come in costume to get a FREE eyeball treat. I called and found out this eyeball is made of white chocolate covered pineapple.
