Seriously, there IS such thing as a free lunch! Head to Foosia in Scottsdale Tuesday, January 13th from 11-3 for a completely FREE meal! [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/foosiafresh) for more information on their facebook page. They offer bowls (they're set up a lot like Chipotle) with your choice of rice, noodles, or greens, meat, sauce, veggies, and toppings. Bowls are usually $8-$9.25.

They are located at 13610 North Scottsdale Road.
