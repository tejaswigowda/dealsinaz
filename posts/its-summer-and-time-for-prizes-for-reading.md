It's summer and that means it's time to sign up for summer reading programs! Many libraries have prizes for babies through adults, so it's definitely worth checking out! [Head over here to print off the summer reading journal for Branes and Noble if you have a 1st-6th grader.](http://dispatch.barnesandnoble.com/content/dam/ccr/pdf/2017/summer-reading/summerreading_tearpad_eng.pdf?x=y) Your child will earn a totally free book just for reading 8 books!

![ma](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/ma.png)

[Are you in Maricopa county? Head over here to sign up for your library reading program!](https://maricopacountyreads.org/) There are programs for ALL AGES and you just need to read 20 minutes a day! I'm super excited about the BOGO Chipotle coupon, the free ear buds, the free book, the Benihana coupon, and more!

Enjoy!
