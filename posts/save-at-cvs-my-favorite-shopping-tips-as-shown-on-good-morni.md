The first thing everyone should do when they get to CVS is go to the Coupon Center. Each store has one and you can either scan your CVS card or use your phone number (so nice since I never have my card with me) to get lots of coupons printed just for you. Grab an ad too to check out the sales and ExtraBucks deals.

![reasons](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/razo.png)

I went straight to the Gillete Venus razor. I had [this printable manufacturers' coupon for $2 off](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=118&aff_id=4227&url_id=103&aff_sub5=MTgyMDkxNDY=&affsrc=1)and a CVS coupon for $4 off any razor purchase of $10+ that printed off at the coupon machine (may not print for everyone). I also earned $5 ExtraBucks.

One thing that's very important to remember about ExtraBucks is that you can use them right away (I ALWAYS do). You'll have to check out a couple of times but you won't forget them in the car or let them expire.

![neutral](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/neutro.png)

Then I bought three Neutrogena wipes packs. I had a manufacturers coupon (from the free Wall Street Journal I get each day), and a CVS coupon that printed off when I got to the store. I used the $5 Extrabucks I earned on the razor and earned another $10 in Extrabucks.

![rev](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/rev.png)

Then I bought two Revlon eye shadows. They were on sale buy one get one half off and I had [two of these printable coupons for $2 off your Revlon eye product (you can print off two](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=118&aff_id=4227&url_id=103&aff_sub5=MTgyMzkwNDE=&affsrc=1)). Matching the sale to the coupons that meant eye shadow for way less than $1 each. I also bought deodorant, cereal, and a few other things that were on sale and used the $10 ExtraBucks I earned on the Neutrogena products. My total bill was just over the cost of tax.

![beaut](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/beaut.png)

Another fun CVS perk that very few people actually take advantage of is the ExtraCare Beauty Club. You can [sign up here for free](http://www.cvs.com/promo/promoLandingTemplate.jsp?promoLandingId=beautyclub-member)and you'll earn $5 in Extrabucks for every $50 in beauty products you spend. You'll also get $3 in Extrabucks for your birthday, a 10% off pass after you enroll, and more.

Know the rules and how to maximize your savings with these key tips and you'll be amazed at how much you save.
