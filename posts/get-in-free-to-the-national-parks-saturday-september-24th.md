Saturday, September 24th is National Public Lands Day and to celebrate you can get into the National Parks including the Grand Canyon and lots of other great AZ parks FOR FREE!

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) for more information and keep in mind that you can also get in free November 11th in honor of Veterans Day.

Check out the national parks in AZ:

**Arizona**

[Casa Grande Ruins National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/cagr)

[Glen Canyon National Recreation Area](http://www.nps.gov/glca)

[Grand Canyon National Park](http://www.nps.gov/grca)

[Lake Mead National Recreation Area](http://www.nps.gov/lame)

[Montezuma Castle National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/moca)

[Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/orpi)

[Petrified Forest National Park](http://www.nps.gov/pefo)

[Pipe Spring National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/pisp)

[Saguaro National Park](http://www.nps.gov/sagu)

[Sunset Crater Volcano National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/sucr)

[Tonto National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/tont)

[Tumacácori National Historical Park](http://www.nps.gov/tuma)

[Tuzigoot National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/tuzi)

[Walnut Canyon National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/waca)

[Wupatki National Monument](http://www.nps.gov/wupa)

![everykid](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/everykid.png)

If you have a child in the fourth grade, [head over here](https://www.everykidinapark.gov/) to score a free family pass good for admission to the parks for this entire school year!

Enjoy!
