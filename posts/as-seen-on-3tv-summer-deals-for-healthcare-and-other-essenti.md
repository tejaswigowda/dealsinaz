Healthcare and other Essential workers have been keeping things going throughout these months of quarantine and there are some deals just for them!

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/headsp-300x150.png)

Headspace realizes that healthcare workers have been particularly overburdened through this Covid 19 crisis and is offering them Headspace Plus for free through 2020. This is a guided meditation app and can help put more zen in your life. [Head over here](https://www.headspace.com/health-covid-19) to see if you qualify and get signed up.

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/boy-1-300x273.png)

The Boyce Thompson Arboretum is thanking all medical professionals with FREE admission through Summer 2020. This beautiful place is located one hour east of Phoenix and offers three miles of garden paths and trails. There are over 2600 different plants featured from deserts around the world. [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/btarboretum/photos/a.122176053263/10157599889768264/?type=3&theater) for more information on their facebook page.

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/vene-1-300x141.png)

The Venetian in Las Vegas is offering community heroes a free night when they book a night through their Share the Love program. Essential workers include people working in healthcare, education, auto repair, laundry services, grocery stores, and more. [Head over here](https://www.venetian.com/offers/share-the-love/get-love-offer.html) for more information.
