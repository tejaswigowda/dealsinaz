National Donut Day is THIS FRIDAY (6/5)! On this delicious holiday, we can all find free donuts in multiple places. Here's where to look (if you know of others, please email me at alicia@dealsinaz.com and I'll add them to the list):

![](asset:images/wp/2026/06/download-2026-06-03T102234.408.png)

Randy's Donuts: Stop in for a free classic donut from 6 AM until 12 PM. If your name is Randy (any spelling), you'll also get a free cup or sticker! [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/share/r/174mgjcbDd/) to see the post on their Facebook page.

![](asset:images/wp/2026/06/download-2026-06-03T102701.767.png)

Krispy Kreme: Stop by anytime during business hours to get a free donut (they spell them doughnut) of your choice (some specialty varieties are excluded). [Head over here](https://www.krispykreme.com/offers/national-donut-day?fbclid=IwY2xjawSNQtJleHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETFqTGMxdEV6bFlGTWwyWE96c3J0YwZhcHBfaWQQMjIyMDM5MTc4ODIwMDg5MgABHoUt2jofk_awdjTWZSf71ishb3Fv8SdwzHerlDMwnD0qwplTx3yREmw5N9ui_aem_qWmK89zpzYA8jGKW34H69A) for the details.

![](asset:images/wp/2026/06/download-2026-06-03T103148.148.png)

Dunkin': Get a free donut when you buy a drink. [Head over here](https://news.dunkindonuts.com/news/national-donut-day-2026) for the details.

Enjoy!
