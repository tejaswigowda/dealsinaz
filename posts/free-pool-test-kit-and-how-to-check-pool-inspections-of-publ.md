[Head over here to request a FREE pool test kit](https://waterandhealth.org/healthy-pools/pool-test-kit-chlorine-ph-strips/). Swimming is such a big part of summer here in AZ and it's critical to #1 watch our kids around water and #2 make sure the water we're swimming in is healthy.

If you're visiting a resort or public pool in AZ soon, [head over here to look up their county inspection reports](http://envapp.maricopa.gov/WaterWaste/SwimmingPool). It's easiest to search by address. Knowledge is power (and health)!
