First, let's talk cost. For the average person, the [price of Prime](https://amzn.to/44jEYUB) is $139/year or $14.99/month (which works out to $179.88/year if you use all 12 months). You can [head over here](https://amzn.to/44jEYUB) to get a free 30-day trial if you want to try it out. For [18 to 24-year-old young adults and students, Amazon is offering a free 6-month trial of Prime](https://www.amazon.com/gp/f.html?C=AGVK6RXK1TMY&K=315XP0P51OE5&M=urn:rtn:msg:20250701003941d57712193fed4849925c94c30420p0na&R=LAZNCG81E9GG&T=C&U=http%3A%2F%2Famazon.com%2Fjoinyoungadult%3Fref_%3Dpe_3519780_1261535740&H=M0KTQASTU1I22BHHAZNRB9UKLNQA&ref_=pe_3519780_1261535740). [Head over here](https://www.amazon.com/gp/f.html?C=AGVK6RXK1TMY&K=315XP0P51OE5&M=urn:rtn:msg:20250701003941d57712193fed4849925c94c30420p0na&R=LAZNCG81E9GG&T=C&U=http%3A%2F%2Famazon.com%2Fjoinyoungadult%3Fref_%3Dpe_3519780_1261535740&H=M0KTQASTU1I22BHHAZNRB9UKLNQA&ref_=pe_3519780_1261535740) to sign up (after the trial is up, you'll pay $7.49/month or $69/year). Young adults/students receive all of the benefits of Prime PLUS 5% cash back on purchases (10% on Prime Day). For EBT/Medicaid cardholders, [Amazon offers 50% off a Prime membership ($6.99/month). Head over here](https://amzn.to/44wPfLR) for all of the details and qualifications for this program.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/IMG_2027-1.jpeg)

Second, let's talk about benefits. There's a whole lot more to Prime than just free shipping. The membership benefits I use all the time are free [Prime Delivery](https://amzn.to/3IfBSrY) (same-day to two-day shipping on almost everything determined on availability), [Prime Video](https://amzn.to/3TpIAOs) (I've found lots of series that I've enjoyed with my husband), [Amazon Prime Music](https://amzn.to/44zsGWK) (over 100 million free songs and podcasts. NOTE: I DO NOT go for [Amazon Music Unlimited](https://amzn.to/4nOWQhQ) for $10.99/month when my Alexa asks), [Amazon Fresh](https://amzn.to/4lIUGOY) (convenient fresh grocery delivery, I love that they often offer promo codes for extra $$ off), [Amazon Prime Visa (5% cash back on Amazon purchases, 7% on Prime Days, plus they have a $200 bonus for getting a card this week)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/BT00LN946S?externalReferenceId=44e5e904-8639-4467-aba9-8ed09444dcb7), [Prime Reading](https://amzn.to/44EgTH3) (free books I can read on my Kindle app), and [Amazon First Reads](https://amzn.to/40AaPOm) books of the month (brand new titles that are always fun to read). I've used [Prime Prescription Savings](https://amzn.to/3I9wPJP) in the past, and it saved me a TON of $ off a prescription. For my family, the $11.58/month we spend on Prime (we pay the $139 annual fee in one payment) is well worth it.

If you're already [paying for Prime, Amazon has put together a full list of included benefits](https://amzn.to/44EgTH3). I highly recommend you check out this list and take advantage of everything you can:
