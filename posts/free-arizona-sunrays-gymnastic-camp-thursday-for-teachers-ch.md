They are offering SuperCamp from 8:30-3, and if you register by 4/24 with promo code #RedforED you'll get half off. [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/ArizonaSunraysGymnasticsDanceCenter/) for more information. SUPERCAMP is usually $78 a day (so $39 with promo code).

Please let teachers you know their children can go FREE with teacher ID.
