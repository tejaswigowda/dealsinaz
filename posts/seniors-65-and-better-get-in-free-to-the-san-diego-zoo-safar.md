If you have a trip to the San Diego area planned in February and you're 65 or better, you're in luck! [Head over here](https://redirect.viglink.com/?format=go&jsonp=vglnk_164382267741610&key=7c61e10a3a0f932079ecc8152116d621&libId=kz5tipdb0102l77t000DL68p7cvqm&loc=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.freestufftimes.com%2F&v=1&out=https%3A%2F%2Fsdzsafaripark.org%2Fseniors-free%23&ref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.freestufftimes.com%2F&title=Free%20Stuff%20Times%20%E2%80%A2%20Free%20stuff%2C%20samples%2C%20and%20everything%20free%20posted%20all%20day%20long!&txt=https%3A%2F%2Fsdzsafaripark.org%2Fseniors-free%23) to find out more about the San Diego Zoo Safari Park's Seniors Get In Free Offer!

"During the month of February, all visitors age 65 and older receive free admission to the Safari Park. Enjoy the sights on an Africa Tram, see the platypuses and other Aussie species at Walkabout Australia, wander through Tiger Trail, take a stroll around Mombasa Lagoon, and relax with refreshments on the patio at Kijamii Overlook. There's a world of sights to see—all at the Safari Park!

**For more information about Seniors Free, check out the Seasonal Events section of our** [**FAQ page**](https://zoo.sandiegozoo.org/help-center#seasonal-events)**.**

_Seniors 65+ must present valid photo ID at turnstiles for free entry. Parking not included._"

Thanks [Free Stuff Times](https://www.freestufftimes.com/free-san-diego-zoo-safari-park-admission-for-seniors-during-february)!

Enjoy!
