That means we can enjoy the deal today 8/17! Cannot be combined with any other offer. THIS OFFER VALID FOR PIZZA ONLY.

**Taco Bell 5-Run Promo**

Each time the [D-backs score five (5) runs or more at home or away, fans can get three (3) free seasoned beef crunchy tacos](https://www.mlb.com/dbacks/fans/update#offers) with the purchase of a large drink from 4-6 p.m. the following day (8/17) at participating Arizona Taco Bell locations.

### CIRCLE K STRIKEOUT METER

[Download the Circle K app](http://www.circlekaz.com/dbackspromo) to get your strikeout meter coupon offer after every home game. Items available on the coupon are determined by the number of batters D-backs pitchers strikeout during each game. Today (8/17) the deal is a free Circle K Chips!

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/426d400b-770b-4369-83d6-bc3256c93fc0-1-300x265.png)

### D-BACKS JACK HOME RUN PROMOTION

If any D-backs player “Jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their local Jack in the Box restaurant the next day to receive a free Jumbo Jack with the purchase of a large drink. Today (8/17) we can take advantage of the deal since there were a couple of home runs!

_Promotions are subject to change and may not occur at every game._
