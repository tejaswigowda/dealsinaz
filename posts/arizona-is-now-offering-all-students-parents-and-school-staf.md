The Arizona Department of Health Services has expanded their free mask program to include all students, their parents, and school staff members in need five free masks (anyone part of a vulnerable population is still welcome to request free masks). You can [head over here to place your order, they will be mailed to you.](https://azdhs.gov/preparedness/epidemiology-disease-control/infectious-disease-epidemiology/index.php?fbclid=IwAR2W2yBeJyssLUM6Zd4J9xgYJt2mdxgKctQ6yjuaP4rKUfVqSvkO43GbHRM#novel-coronavirus-order-masks)

Check out the details of the free mask program:

"It is important that every Arizonan does their part to prevent the spread of COVID-19, which includes wearing a mask whenever you are in public, washing your hands frequently, and maintaining physical distance from others.

We want to ensure every Arizonan has access to face masks and we have partnered with Hanes to provide free [face masks](https://www.cdc.gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/prevent-getting-sick/how-to-wear-cloth-face-coverings.html) to Arizonans that need them the most. We encourage anyone in a vulnerable population, who is a student (or parents on behalf of students) or school staff member, or who may not be able to purchase a mask to place an order below. Each order will provide 5 washable, reusable cloth face masks, one order per household. If you have questions about ordering or for information about how this program works, visit our [FAQs page](https://azdhs.gov/preparedness/epidemiology-disease-control/infectious-disease-epidemiology/index.php#novel-coronavirus-order-faqs). For questions about your order or the product itself please contact Hanes at 1-800-503-6698."

Mask Up AZ!
