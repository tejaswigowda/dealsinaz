In honor of Presidents Day Weekend, you can get into all of the National Parks FOR FREE February 14th-16th.

[Head over here](http://www.nps.gov/findapark/feefreeparks.htm) for more information and other free dates.

We of course have the Grand Canyon, but [head over here](http://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm) for a HUGE LIST of beautiful parks you could explore!

We love Sunset Crater Volcano (unlike anywhere I've been), Montezuma Castle, Walnut Canyon, and Wupatiki. There are still a lot on the list that we need to visit.

Enjoy!
