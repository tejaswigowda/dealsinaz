Still figuring out where to host your fantasy football draft party? [Head over here](http://www.buffalowildwings.com/en/fantasy-football/) for more information about the draft kit you'll get for free when you request to have your party at Buffalo Wild Wings.

You'll get a draft board an player stickers, a brag flag, a last place license plate frame, a pocket schedule, a turf drink sleeve, and coupons. This is while supplies last only. You can also get [freebies at Hooters](https://www.hooters.com/football/home.aspx) (personally I think Buffalo Wild Wings sounds better but hey, I thought I should mention it).
