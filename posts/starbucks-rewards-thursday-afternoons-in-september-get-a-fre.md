If you love fall drinks (pumpkin spice anyone?!) you'll want to add Thursday afternoons in September to your calendar. Starbucks Rewards members can score a FREE fall drink when purchasing another one. It's totally free to join, [head over here](https://www.starbucks.com/) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/11318869-7056-4bee-9674-5f4f461b9dd9.png)

Check out the details: "\*Valid 9/7, 9/14, 9/21, AND 9/28/23 after 12:00 p.m. at participating Starbucks stores in the US. Buy any handcrafted fall drink, and get one of equal or lesser value free ($10 max value). Limit one per member per week. Qualifying drinks include any size hot/iced/blended versions of Pumpkin Spice Latte, Pumpkin Cream Cold Brew, Iced Pumpkin Cream Chai Tea Latte, Chai Tea Latte, Apple Crisp Oatmilk Shaken Espresso, Caramel Apple Spice, and Apple Crisp Oatmilk Macchiato. Adding Pumpkin Cream Cold Foam and/or other fall flavor modifiers to all other drinks does not qualify.

Cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. "

Enjoy!
