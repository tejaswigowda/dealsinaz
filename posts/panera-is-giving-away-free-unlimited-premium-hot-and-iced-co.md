If you have a Panera close by and love coffee, this is a DEAL! [Head over here to get signed up for their My Panera+ coffee subscription for FREE](https://www.panerabread.com/en-us/mypanera/mypanera-coffee-subscription.html) through September 7th. You'll be able to order a FREE hot or iced coffee every two hours at locations all over the place.

[Head over here](https://www.panerabread.com/en-us/mypanera/mypanera-coffee-subscription/faqs.html?intcmp=hero-4-con-c-summercoffee-faq) for answers to frequently asked questions.

If you don't want to be charged after September 7th (it's $8.99 a month plus tax to keep the free coffee going), you will need to cancel.

This could definitely perk up your summer!
