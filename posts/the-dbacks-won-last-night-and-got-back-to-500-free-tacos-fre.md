The Dbacks beat the Texas Rangers last night 7-4 and finally got back to .500 (on their 13th try)! Now they're in second place in the National League West and definitely on the playoff hunt! Hopefully they can keep this momentum going! I'd love to see another World Series at Chase Field!

We get deals today to celebrate (7/9)!

![taco](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/taco.jpg)

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. That means today (7/9) is free taco day!

![papa](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/papa1.jpg)

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at [Papajohns.com](http://order.papajohns.com/order.html) with promo code DBACKS50! That means today (7/9) is half off pizza day!

![jumbo](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jumbo.png)

Anytime a Dback “jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since there were two home runs yesterday, you can take advantage of this deal today (7/9).
