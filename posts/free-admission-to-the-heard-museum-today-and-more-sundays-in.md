Looking for something to do this Sunday afternoon? Why not head to the Heard Museum? Admission is FREE today (July 13th) and will be free on the 20th and 27th too!

[Head over here for more information.](https://www.facebook.com/HeardMuseum/photos/a.171645797535.131266.34013712535/10152216439307536/?type=1&theater)

Adults are usually $23, kids are usually $12.50 (so that would be a cool $71 for my family).

They are open until 5 PM.

From their facebook page...

"come to the Heard, enjoy the frosty air conditioning and all of our terrific exhibits and activities. (\*A $5 gate fee per person is charged to non-museum members who wish to visit "BUILD! Toy Brick Art at the Heard.")"

Enjoy!
