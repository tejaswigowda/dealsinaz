This is SUCH a great show and a great way to get the kids involved in the election! Head over here to save half off tickets to the Childsplay performances of Rock the Presidents: A Musical Guide Through History going on Saturday and Sunday at 4 PM. There is a service fee, when it's all said and done you'll pay $17 for adults, $14.25 for kids.

History meets rock, pop and folk music in this high-octane romp through the nation's presidents -- from George Washington to Barack Obama. The show spans 223 years of history using songs, photos and film, resulting in a funny and fascinating look at our leaders. Nationally recognized youth theater company Childsplay brings all 44 presidents to life in ways that will delight children and parents alike, using an all-original soundtrack. USA Today called the show "a blast of guitar-crunching, fist-pumping, lighter-flicking awesomeness," and now you can enjoy it at the Tempe Center for the Arts.

It's not Hamilton, but it's amazing, and the tickets are a much better deal!
