The Phoenix Open officially starts today! Everyone gets in FREE today and tomorrow (1/28) for Ford Free Days. Keep in mind that kids under 17 get in free with an adult.

If you know someone who has served in the military or as a first responder (EMT, firefighter, or police officer), [let them know that they can head over here t](https://wmphoenixopen.com/spectator-info/military-and-emergency-services-personnel/)o get a FREE voucher for themselves and a guest.

### "FREE TOURNAMENT ADMISSION FOR FIRST RESPONDERS AND U.S. MILITARY

The Waste Management Phoenix Open tournament hosts, The Thunderbirds, invite all First Responders, as well as all Active, Reserve, Veterans and Retired U.S. Military personnel, plus one accompanying individual, to be their guests (free of charge) at the 2020 Waste Management Phoenix Open. This marks the 19th year The Thunderbirds have extended this small gesture of thanks to the heroes who’ve given so much for our country.

**TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE FREE ADMISSION OFFER:**

- [You must verify your status through Sheer ID.](https://wmphoenixopen.com/spectator-info/military-and-emergency-services-personnel/)
- Eligible personnel must present their official [ID](https://wmphoenixopen.com/wp-content/uploads/military_credentials.pdf) or badge along with ticket voucher at the main tournament entrance.
- Business cards and pay stubs will not be accepted as forms of ID.
- Tickets are limited to two per day, per each official [ID](https://wmphoenixopen.com/wp-content/uploads/military_credentials.pdf).
- Standard tournament spectator policies will be in enforced, including no firearms allowed."
