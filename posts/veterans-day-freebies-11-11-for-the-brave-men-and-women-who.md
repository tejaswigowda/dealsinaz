![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Free-Meals-for-Military-on-Veterans-Day-1024x768-1.jpg)

Veterans Day is a fantastic time to say THANK YOU to all of the brave men and women who have given so much to defend our freedom. Lots of valley restaurants and car washes are offering freebies to Active Duty Military and Veterans with ID on 11/11. Here are a few (if you know of others please e-mail them to me at alicia@dealsinaz.com).

[**Bar Louie**](https://www.barlouie.com/): "Raise a glass to those who’ve served our country. Then tell them to come get their FREE Craft Burger anytime this Veteran’s Day. Offer valid for all active or retired military and Veterans, from open to close on Thursday, November 11th. \*Dine-in only. With valid military ID. You may choose any Craft Burger of choice!"

[**BJ’s Restaurants**](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay): Current and former military who dine in-restaurant on November 11th will enjoy a complimentary meal from the select menu ([see here](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay)) and a Dr. Pepper beverage.

[**California Pizza Kitchen**](https://www.facebook.com/californiapizzakitchen/videos/3147102142243590)[**:**](https://www.cpk.com/Vets_Day_2020) "This Thursday, November 11th, all veterans and active-duty service members will receive a complimentary beverage + choice of one entrée. As a special Thank You, dine-in and receive an exclusive offer for you to use on your next visit from November 12th-20th, 2021."

[**Denny’s**](https://www.dennys.com/#/location): Is offering all active duty and retired veterans a FREE build your own Grand Slam breakfast from 5 AM until noon 11/11.

[**Dunkin’ Donuts**](https://news.dunkindonuts.com/blog/veterans-day-free-donut): All active duty military and veterans can enjoy a free donut on 11/11!

[**Great Clips**](https://www.greatclips.com/about-us/giving/thank-a-veteran?fbclid=IwAR0C8nRVL0oJBQ6kidyNoaD0KqQYUihPLkQXoNyZsjBbBI-BJoMe_qIx-o4): "While not all of us have served in the military ourselves, most of us know a service member who we would like to thank. With Great Clips’ annual promotion, we give everyone a way to thank an active or veteran military service member on Veterans Day. And we give members of the military more than a one-day event to redeem their free haircut card.

This year on Veterans Day – Thursday, November 11 – veterans and active military members can visit a U.S. Great Clips salon and receive either a free haircut that day or a free haircut card. In addition, non-military customers who get a haircut on Veterans Day at Great Clips salons can pay it forward. They will receive a free haircut card they can give to an active service member or veteran as a way of saying thank you. The cards can be redeemed for a free haircut from November 12 through December 10, 2021."

[**Logan’s Roadhouse**](https://www.facebook.com/Logans.Roadhouse/photos/a.399826919321/10159668366009322/?type=3): "This Veterans Day, we’re inviting all veterans, active duty and retired U.S. military personnel to enjoy a free meal\* between 3-6 p.m. from our American Roadhouse menu at participating locations."

[**Macaroni Grill**](https://twitter.com/MacaroniGrill/status/1457784996178059267/photo/1): "Help us spread the word. This Veterans Day, 11/11, all veterans and active military receive a free Mom's Ricotta Meatballs + Spaghetti with military ID. [#MacGrillGratitude](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MacGrillGratitude?src=hashtag_click)"

[**Macayo’s**:](https://www.facebook.com/macayosrestaurants/photos/a.227982827509/10159512183257510/) "Join us for Veterans Day, Thursday, November 11th, 2021 to enjoy a Free Entrée from our Veterans Day Menu.

Our Veterans Day Menu Includes:

– Fiesta Salad

– Carnitas Dinner

– Chicken Poblano

– Macayo’s Original Chimichanga

– Sonoran Enchilada Trio

– Baja Burro

– Carne Asada Burro

Valid for Dine-In Service, Takeout & Macayo Delivery

For Online Orders, Promo Code – VETERANS

Valid for all Active & Non-Active Military Personnel. Military ID required. No cash value. Not to be combined with any other offers or discounts. One Free Entrée per Military Personnel. Excludes tax & tip."

[**Original Breakfast House**:](https://www.facebook.com/OBHPHX/photos/a.571469249602828/4342499282499787/) Veterans, active-duty military, first responders, and nurses eat free on Thursday, November 11th from 6 AM to 2 PM. [Head over her](https://www.facebook.com/OBHPHX/photos/a.571469249602828/4342499282499787/)e for more information on their facebook page.

[**Sport Clips**](https://www.sportclips.com/promotions-partnerships/help-a-hero/help-a-hero-2020)**:** "Many Sport Clips locations will offer free haircuts to active duty U.S. service members and veterans on Veterans Day, November 11, 2021, with valid proof of service. Thank you for your service!" [Head over here](https://www.sportclips.com/promotions-partnerships/help-a-hero/help-a-hero-2021) to see a list of participating locations**.**
