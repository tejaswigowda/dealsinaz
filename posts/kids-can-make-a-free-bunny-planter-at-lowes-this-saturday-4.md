[Head over here](https://www.lowes.com/events/register/build-a-bunny-planter-workshop) to sign your kids up to make a FREE bunny planter at Lowe's this Saturday! You'll have everything there ready for your child to successfully build their project (along with helpers).

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/db5a16c3-9046-4091-926c-7b68e8b0cb42.png)

You could make a whole morning of building and [head over here](https://www.homedepot.com/workshops/) to sign up the kids to make a FREE poolside birdhouse at The Home Depot this Saturday too (scroll down to in-store kids workshops and click "sign up"). Your kids will receive their awesome project (it could definitely attract some birds), along with an apron, commemorative pin, and certificate.

I LOVED these workshops when my kids were little! Please let me know how it goes!
