I LOVE THIS! If you're near the Challenger Space Center, stop in to pick up FREE advance screening passes to see Tomorrowland (while supplies last). I called and they said they had lots available (but you may want to call ahead)! Each person receives up to two passes and each pass gets up to two people in (so if I went myself I would be able to get my family of four into the screening).

Check out the specifics:

"An Out-Of-This-World Opportunity...

Challenger Space Center has your FREE tickets to the early screening of Tomorrowland! Stop by the center this week to pick up your special pin and physical tickets! We are open 10:00am-4:00pm and although no admission is required, we invite you to view the center and take a guided tour!

Date: Tuesday, May 19th

Theater: Harkins Tempe Market Place

Time: 7:00 PM

Passes: must be picked up at 21170 N. 83rd Ave | Peoria, AZ 85382

For questions please call (623)-322-2007"

This is definitely the summer movie I'm most excited to see!

Enjoy!
