If you're a fan of Dairy Queen, you won't want to miss Free Cone Day! On March 20th everyone can stop in for a FREE vanilla cone (no purchase necessary) at participating locations. I went to the location near 44th street and Thomas in Phoenix and they even let me get a chocolate cone! YUM!

[Head over here](https://news.dairyqueen.com/announcements/details/2025/Free-Cone-Day-Is-Back-at-Dairy-Queen-On-March-20-Fans-Can-Celebrate-by-Enjoying-a-Free-Vanilla-Soft-serve-Cone-at-Participating-DQ-Locations/default.aspx) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rita_s_Italian_Ice_Frozen_Custard_First_Day_of_Spring_Giveaway-2.jpg)

You can also stop by Rita's Italian Ice on March 20th for a FREE 6 oz. cup of your choice. They're featuring their brand new Skittles flavor. [Head over here](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ritas-italian-ice-celebrates-first-day-of-spring-with-free-italian-ice-giveaway-and-new-skittles-italian-ice-flavor-302396105.html) for more information.

In AZ, Rita's locations are in Phoenix (Bell and Tatum) and Glendale.

Enjoy!
