If you feel like going to the fair,on Wednesdays until 6 PM we can all get in free with a donation of 5 cans of food!

[Head over here](https://azstatefair.com/deals/) for more information!

"FREE admission until 6pm with charitable donation of 5 cans of nonperishable food to benefit St. Mary’s Food Bank, courtesy of Sanderson Ford and Operation Santa Claus. All gate discounts must be used at time of purchase. Not valid in combination with any other offer. \*Valid every Wednesday _until 6pm._

**Unfortunately, we aren’t able to accept:** Bottled Water, Baby Food, Ramen Noodles, and items in glass containers.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/readride.png)

Kids can also read three books to get three carnival rides. Head [over here for the printable form](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/FINAL-2018-Read-Ride-Form.jpg) you’ll take to the guest services booth for the free rides.

Update: Kaitlyn did this and we recommend using the free rides on the high ticket options. If you go on the 14 ticket rides the free book deal is worth $21...definitely worth doing!

Enjoy!
