Click [here](https://maricopacountyreads.org/) to sign up for the summer reading game through Maricopa County Libraries. It's open to all ages and offers great prizes you earn by reading.

If you're going to read anyway, why not get a free pass to an Arizona State Park like Slide Rock (usually $20-$30 per car)?! There are so many Arizona State Parks to explore! There are prizes to win from Raisin' Canes, Peter Piper Pizza, the Phoenix Mercury, and more.

Enjoy and get reading!
