### Pumpkin Nights at the Princess

- **Pumpkin Nights Firework Display**: The sky covered in sparkly and colorful fireworks which patrons can enjoy from Pumpkin Patch and Jack-o-Lantern lawns weekly Thursday - Sunday at 9 p.m.
- **Patches Seasonal Shop**: An array of the seasonal “Must Haves” including PJ’s, plush, fall treats, gifts, LED light up toys, and more
- **Princess Plaza Fountain Lantern Launch**: Upon arrival families and friends will receive a complimentary lantern they can launch in the Princess Plaza Fountain as a display of hope and positive intentions.
- **Pumpkin Patch**: A variety of pumpkins sitting on a rustic farm truck and available for purchase
- **Great Pumpkin Photo Opportunity**: Farm truck everyone will enjoy and a chance to make life-lasting memories with just one camera click
- **The Pie House**: Foodies can order homemade pies from the campfire and recreate this magical atmosphere once they’re back at home
- **YOYO Swing Chair Ride**: Little and not so little thrill-seekers can feel the wind in their hair and enjoy spectacular views while sitting on a mesmerizing chair ride (42” minimum height requirement)
- **Spidermania Ride**: Must be less than 150lbs to ride. (36” minimum height requirement)
- **The Sizzler Ride**: Must be 42” tall if riding with an adult/ (52” minimum height requirement if riding solo)
- **The Super Slide Ride**: (42” minimum height requirement)
- **Hay Maze**: A multi-acre labyrinth is a perfect place to get lost and hopefully find a way back
- **Pumpkin Nights fuel**: Visiting all the activities can really increase one’s appetite but luckily there are s’mores and concessions with seasonal signature beverages and a full bar offering available for purchase.
