**What to buy versus what to wait for:**

Memorial Day weekend is a great time to stock up on summer essentials. I found lots of great deals on clothing, sandals, patio furniture, sunscreen, personal products, and large appliances. If you're in the market for a TV, computer, tablet, or small appliance, I recommend waiting until Prime Day in June for the biggest savings.
