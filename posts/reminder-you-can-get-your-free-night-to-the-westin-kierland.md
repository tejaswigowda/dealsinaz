Just a quick reminder that now through June 18th while supplies last you can head to Scottsdale Quarter and spend $500 in any two stores to get a FREE night at the Westin Kierland resort (you'll pay resort fee). They are including American Express gift cards in this amazing deal so you could theoretically buy a $500 AmEx gift card to use ANYWHERE the card is accepted and a Menchie's frozen yogurt (or stop into any of their other stores for something). [Head over here](http://scottsdalequarter.com/events-news/event/beginning-june-5-2017---free-one-night-stay-at-the-westin-kierland) for all of the details of the Westin Kierland promotioni and [head over here](http://scottsdalequarter.com/gift-cards) for more information about where to buy an AmEx Scottsdale Quarter gift card (it looks like it's at the mall management office with a $3.75 purchase fee).

From June 5 through June 18, 2017, guests will receive a voucher for a free one night stay at The Westin Kierland when they spend $500 or more at two or more stores or restaurants at Scottsdale Quarter in a single day. Take same-day receipts to Warby Parker during normal store hours to redeem voucher.

\*Terms and conditions:

- Guests must redeem same-day receipts at Warby Parker, located in The Quad by the fountain, during normal store hours.
- Advance reservations are required.
- Call 480-624-1202 and mention “SQGWP.”
- Rooms are based on availability.
- Offer does not include Resort Service Charge or Parking Fees.
- Offer is valid for one-night stay from June 19, 2017 to Sept. 4, 2017.
- Blackout dates: 7/13, 8/2, 8/3, 8/4 – Blackout dates are subject to change, based on availability.
- Voucher must be received and stay booked and redeemed by same individual.
- Identification will be required at check-in.
- Limited to one voucher per person, per promotion.
- Voucher has no cash value.
- Guests must cancel 48 hours prior to stay.
- Not valid with other offers or promotions.

Enjoy!
