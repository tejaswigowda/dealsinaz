Do you have a Frozen lover in your house? Beat the heat and visit Elsa and Anna Saturday, July 26th from 11-12 at Baby Lux in North Scottsdale. [Click here](http://www.babyluxonline.com/events/index.html) for more information and directions.

From their [facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/BabyLux)...

"We are just 6 days away from Elsa and Anna joining us at [Baby Lux](https://www.facebook.com/BabyLux) Saturday from 11:00am-12:00pm. Join us for party favors, giveaways, hot cocoa gone "COLD" (Chocolate Milk) and chocolate chip cookies! We will also have a photographer on hand to capture all of your precious memories with our two favorite sisters!"

Baby Lux is an adorable store, especially if you're expecting or have a baby shower coming up!

I ran into a friend at a froyo shop the other day who told me her daughter waited for HOURS to see Elsa and Anna at Disneyland. Hopefully this won't have quite as long of a wait!
