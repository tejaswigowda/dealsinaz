If you're looking for something fun to do on Tuesday (March 14th) you might want to [head to the Desert Botanical Garden. Admission is FREE for all (as it is the second Tuesday of each month)](https://www.dbg.org/visiting#free) and they're even including the beautiful butterfly pavillion! I just called and the very nice woman on the phone said it is spectacular this year!

Hours are 8AM to 8PM and normal admission is $24.95 for adults, $12.95 for kids (I like free way better)!

Enjoy!
