Coffee lovers rejoice it's National Coffee Day today (9/29)! Here's where to find a cup of joe for free:

![coffeedoughn](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/coffeedoughn.jpg)

**Krispy Kreme** is giving away a FREE coffee and original glazed doughnut to everyone today! [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/events/1937856463107749/) for more information and be sure to call your location to confirm they are participating.

![cartel](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/cartel.png)

**Cartel Coffee Lab** is giving everyone a free large cold brew or large hot drip coffee today when you register your e-mail address in store. [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/CartelCoffeeLab/?fref=ts) for more information on their facebook page. Thanks Angelica!

![coffeeday](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/coffeeday.jpg)

Stop by **The Refuge** in Phoenix for a free small cup of coffee or cold brew all day today (9/29). [Head over here](http://therefugeaz.com/category/blog/) for more information. Thanks Lindsay!

![sip](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/sip.jpg)

**Sip Coffee and Beer House** in Scottsdale and **Sip Coffee and Beer Garag**e in Phoenix will be giving away free hot coffee today when you mention you heard about the promotion on facebook. Thanks Carrie!

**Circle K**- If you have [their app](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/circle-k/id566580292?mt=8) look under "deals" tab for a free medium coffee (thanks [Bargain Believer](http://www.bargainbeliever.com/blog/2016/09/national-coffee-day-is-thursday-929-free-coffee-and-doughnut-at-krispy-kreme-and-other-offers/))!

Isn't today a PERFECT day for coffee?!

Enjoy!
