This has passed, hope the wonderful veterans and active duty military got to enjoy some of the freebies! Looking forward to next year!

Veterans Day is coming up next Wednesday (11/11) and many businesses want to thank the brave men and women who have served our country so honorably with freebies. For all of the deals, I would recommend bringing proof of service. If you have any deals to add, please e-mail me at alicia@dealsinaz.com. Thanks to Nikki, Regina, Mark, Angela, Sean, Lennie, and everyone else who has e-mailed me so far!

[**Applebee's:**](https://www.applebees.com/en/veterans-day) They are offering veterans and active duty military a free meal from their Veterans Day menu as space permits in their dining room. [Head over here](https://www.applebees.com/en/veterans-day) for more information, I called two AZ locations and they both had messages saying they are participating.

[**Bar Louie**](https://www.barlouie.com/): "As a thank you for your service, this Veteran’s Day, allow us to serve you! Wednesday, November 11th Louie nation is offering a free craft burger or flatbread of your choice to all active or retired military."

**Bashas', AJ's, and Food City**: The Bashas' family of stores is offering 10% off groceries to all active duty military and Veterans with ID on 11/11.

[**BJ's Restaurants**](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay): All current and former military with ID can enjoy an entree up to $14.95 and a Dr. Pepper on 11/11. [Head over here](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay) for more information.

[**Buffalo Wild Wings**](https://www.buffalowildwings.com/en/promos/veterans_day_10pc/): "Join us on November 11 for dine-in or call your local sports bar for takeout. And get a free order of 10 boneless wings + fries as a thanks. "

[**California Pizza Kitchen:**](https://www.cpk.com/Vets_Day_2020) Head [over here](https://www.cpk.com/Vets_Day_2020) to check out the special complimentary Veterans Day menu available for active duty and retired military.

[**Chili's**](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day): "We look forward to once again showing our gratitude with a FREE veterans day meal as a small way to honor your service and the sacrifices you’ve made for our country." Head [over here](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day) to check out the special menu.

[**Denny's**](https://www.dennys.com/#/location): Is offering all active duty and retired veterans a FREE build your own Grand Slam breakfast from 5 AM until noon 11/11.

[**Dunkin' Donuts**](https://news.dunkindonuts.com/news/veterans-day-2020): All active duty military and veterans can enjoy a free donut on 11/11!

[**Great Clips:**](https://www.greatclips.com/about-us/giving/thank-a-veteran) Active Duty and Retired military can stop in for a free haircut today OR a card for a future haircut.

**Kneader's**: They are offering active duty and retired military free chunky cinnamon French toast before 11 AM on 11/11.

[**Logan's Roadhouse**](https://11rixf1f9u5k3c8r324dgxl6-wpengine.netdna-ssl.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/CORP-Vets-Menu.pdf): [Head over here](https://11rixf1f9u5k3c8r324dgxl6-wpengine.netdna-ssl.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/CORP-Vets-Menu.pdf) to check out the special menu for veterans and active duty military good from 3-6 PM on 11/11.

[**Macaroni Grill**](https://twitter.com/MacaroniGrill/status/1325460021543497729): All active duty and retired military get a free Mom's Ricotta Meatballs and Spaghetti with military ID.

[**Macayo's**](https://www.facebook.com/macayosrestaurants/photos/a.227982827509/10158704474102510/): All active and retired military with ID will enjoy a free entree on 11/11 from their Veterans Day menu! Head [over here](https://www.facebook.com/macayosrestaurants/photos/a.227982827509/10158704474102510/) for more information on their facebook page!

[**National Parks**](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/veterans-and-gold-star-families-free-access.htm): Gold Star families now get free entrance into national parks with ID all the time! [Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/veterans-and-gold-star-families-free-access.htm) for more information.

[**NYPD Pizza**:](https://www.facebook.com/NewYorkPizzaDepartment/photos/a.10157385352861955/10158504872671955/) All participating locations will be giving a free pizza and a fountain drink to all active, retired, former and reserve military on Veteran’s Day at NYPD Pizza. Thanks Nicole!

[**Original Breakfast House**](https://www.facebook.com/OBHPHX?fref=ts): Veterans can enjoy a free meal and there will be lots of fun at the restaurant all day! Check out more info on their [facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/OBHPHX?fref=ts)!

[**Outback Steakhouse**](https://twitter.com/Outback/status/1325105746439237633): Active and retired military get a free bloomin' onion and beverage on Veteran's Day.

[**Sport Clips**](https://www.sportclips.com/promotions-partnerships/help-a-hero/help-a-hero-2020)**:** Participating locations are offering free haircuts to veterans and active-duty military all day 11/11. [Head over here](https://www.sportclips.com/promotions-partnerships/help-a-hero/help-a-hero-2020) to find a participating store near you.

[**Starbucks**](https://stories.starbucks.com/stories/2020/starbucks-offers-free-coffee-to-veterans-active-duty-and-military-spouses-on-veterans-day/): All current and former military with ID can enjoy a free tall hot brewed coffee on Veterans Day 11/11! [Head over here](https://stories.starbucks.com/stories/2020/starbucks-offers-free-coffee-to-veterans-active-duty-and-military-spouses-on-veterans-day/) for more information.

[**Valle Luna**](https://www.facebook.com/vallelunamexicanrestaurant/photos/a.196374453734692/3614464761925627/): Active, retired, and reserve military get a free meal today from 11 AM until close.

[**Yard House**:](https://www.yardhouse.com/events) All active duty and retired military will enjoy a free appetizer 11/11 with ID.
