If you know an AZ teacher who wants to attend SeaWorld in 2024, please share this! They can [head over here to request a FREE fun card](https://seaworld.com/san-diego/tickets/teacher-free-admission/) that will get them into the park any day this year with a few blackout dates (not valid for SeaWorld San Diego admission on May 26, July 7, 13, 20, 27, and Sept. 1).

Enjoy!
