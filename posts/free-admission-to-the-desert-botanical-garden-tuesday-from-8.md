Tuesday (October 14th) is the second Tuesday of the month and that means FREE admission to the Desert Botanical Garden from 8 AM- 8 PM! [Head over here](http://www.dbg.org/about-the-garden/before-you-visit#free) for more information about the free admission opportunity!

![monarch](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/monarch.jpg)

Even more exciting, the [Monarch butterfly exhibit is BACK](http://www.dbg.org/events-exhibitions/mariposa-monarca-monarch-butterfly-exhibit)! I just called and confirmed that this is included FOR FREE today too (it's usually an extra charge on top of admission).

General admission is usually $22 for adults, $10 for kids 3-12, $12 for students, and $20 for seniors. The butterfly exhibit usually adds an extra $3.50/person.

If you're on fall break and have two adults and two kids, you just saved yourself $78 by going today!

Enjoy!
