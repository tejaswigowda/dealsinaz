~~Saturday, September 25th is~~ [~~National Public Lands Day and it's one of the free entrance days for national parks~~](https://home.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm)~~!~~ If you can't make it this weekend, mark your calendar for Thursday, November 11th...that's the next fee-free day.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/knowable-022619-1-1200x630-1.jpg)

I know a lot of Arizona residents who have NEVER BEEN to the Grand Canyon. This is a great chance to see it for free (a private vehicle is usually $35)!

Check out all of the participating Arizona National Parks:

[Glen Canyon National Recreation Area](https://www.nps.gov/glca)

[Grand Canyon National Park](https://www.nps.gov/grca)

[Lake Mead National Recreation Area](https://www.nps.gov/lame)

[Montezuma Castle National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/moca)

[Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/orpi)

[Petrified Forest National Park](https://www.nps.gov/pefo)

[Pipe Spring National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/pisp)

[Saguaro National Park](https://www.nps.gov/sagu)

[Sunset Crater Volcano National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/sucr)

[Tonto National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/tont)

[Tumacácori National Historical Park](https://www.nps.gov/tuma)

[Tuzigoot National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/tuzi)

[Walnut Canyon National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/waca)

[Wupatki National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/wupa)

Enjoy!
