If you're anywhere near Crayola Experience in Chandler, this is a fun freebie for the kids! [Head over here](https://www.crayolaexperience.com/national-crayon-day-giveaway) to register for a day to get a free box or two of 32 crayons from the "pick your pack" wall. It's way more fun to draw with your favorite colors! There are new Colors of Kindness crayons available too...we could definitely use more kindness in this world!

Check out the details:

### and PICK OUT your favorite colors!

Choose a pickup day that's best for you! **You must register and download a voucher** to pick up your FREE crayon box and pick out 32 crayons of your choice at a specific Crayola Store location. Please note that store hours are different than Crayola Experience attraction hours. _This giveaway does not include entry to the Crayola Experience attraction._

Don’t wait! Offer runs March 31-May 31, 2023, and is on a first come, first served basis.

**LIMIT TWO (2) VOUCHERS PER HOUSEHOLD. DUPLICATE ORDERS WILL BE CANCELED. VOUCHERS CAN ONLY BE REDEEMED DURING THE DATE YOU SELECT AND CANNOT BE RESCHEDULED.** "
