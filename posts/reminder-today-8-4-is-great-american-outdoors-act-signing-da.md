Today (8/4) is Great American Outdoors Act Signing Day, and to celebrate, everyone gets FREE admission to the National Parks! If today doesn't work, [you can head over here to see the full list of fee-free days.](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1207/start-planning-your-2025-national-park-trip.htm) September 27th (National Public Lands Day) and November 11th (Veterans Day) will also offer free admission to all!

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/findapark/index.htm?s=AZ) to see a full list of the National Parks, Monuments, and Historical Sites in AZ. We are lucky to have the Grand Canyon AND so many other wonders to visit CLOSE BY!
