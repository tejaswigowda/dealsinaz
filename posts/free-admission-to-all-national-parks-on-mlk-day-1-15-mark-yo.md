Why not visit a National Park like the Grand Canyon this MLK Day? It'll be chilly but so beautiful! As a bonus, everyone gets FREE entrance to all National Parks this Monday, January 15th. [Check out the full list](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) of free entry days:

- **January 15:** [Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/mlk-day.htm)
- **April 20:** First day of [National Park Week](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/national-park-week.htm)
- **June 19:** [Juneteenth National Independence Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/juneteenth.htm)
- **August 4:** Anniversary of the [Great American Outdoors Act](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/infrastructure/gaoa.htm)
- **September 28:** [National Public Lands Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/public-lands-day.htm)
- **November 11:** [Veterans Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/veterans-day.htm)

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm) to check out all of the National Parks in AZ!

Enjoy!
