You MUST pre-register online; you can't get this offer at the park[. "SeaWorld San Diego is proud to offer FREE 2026 Teacher Fun Cards to active and certified K–12 classroom teachers in Southern California and Arizona.

**Register by February 15 and visit by May 31 to take advantage of this exclusive offer and enjoy unlimited admission through December 31, 2026! Plus, now through March 31, you'll receive one FREE guest ticket when you register to share the fun of SeaWorld with a friend.**

**Why SeaWorld Supports Educators**

- Research and education are key pillars of SeaWorld’s mission.
- SeaWorld recognizes the critical role educators play in shaping future global citizens.
- To amplify educational efforts, SeaWorld offers free admission for Southern California and Arizona certified K–12 classroom teachers.

**About the Teacher Fun Card**

- Valid for **unlimited admission** to SeaWorld® San Diego through **December 31, 2026.**
- **Register by February 15** and **visit by May 31** to activate your card and enjoy unlimited admission through 2026.

**How to Get Your Free Teacher Fun Card**

- Pre-register online using the **ID.me** process to receive your eVoucher.
- Click the **Teacher ID** button (top-right), select **Sign Up**, and enter your email (personal email recommended) and password.
- [**Sign up for SeaWorld emails**](https://seaworld.com/san-diego/sign-up-for-email/) to receive special deals and updates."
