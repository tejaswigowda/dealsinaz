The Dbacks beat the Giants in San Francisco Thursday night 7-6 in 12 innings (I stayed up for all 12 believe it or not)! ![taco](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/taco.jpg)

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. That means Friday (4/17) is free taco day!

![papa](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/papa1.jpg)

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at [Papajohns.com](http://order.papajohns.com/order.html) with promo code DBACKS50! That means Friday (4/17) is half off pizza day!

![jumbo](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jumbo.png)

Anytime a Dback “jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since there were some huge home runs, you can take advantage of this deal Friday (4/17).

Let's keep winning Dbacks!
