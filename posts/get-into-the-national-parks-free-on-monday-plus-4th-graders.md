[Do you have Monday off for Martin Luther King Jr. day? You may want to take the kids to a National Park...admission is FREE in honor of the holiday 1/15.](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm)

If you can't do Monday, [head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) for a list of the other fee-free days this year (4/21, 9/22, and 11/11).

![everykid](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/everykid.png)

If you have a fourth grader this year, don’t forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! [Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids](https://www.everykidinapark.gov/get-your-pass/) (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2018.

My daughter Kaitlyn was in 4th grade last year and we definitely took advantage of the pass. We took a family road trip to Yellowstone in July and it was amazing. We made stops at other national parks along the way too.

Please share with anyone you know with a 4th grader or 4th grade teachers!
