1\. Furniture. There are lots of summer clearance events going on. Right now you can save huge at the [Living Spaces summer clearance event](http://www.livingspaces.com/morePromotions.aspx?pid=6). They have sectionals, bedroom sets, and 5-piece counter sets all under $500. Stick to what's on sale and you'll do really well. It never hurts to ask a manager for extra discounts.

![staplesc](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/staplesc.png)

[At Staples right now](https://dealsinaz.com/www.jdoqocy.com/click-4314883-10435034?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.staples.com%2FHP-18-5010-AIO-Desktop-PC%2Fproduct_573053) you can pick up an HP all-in-one desktop PC for $329.90, usually $499.99. They marked it down AGAIN to $231.90 and it sold out almost right away. Keep looking for great deals as computer discounts should be plentiful!

At [Best Buy](https://dealsinaz.com/www.bestbuy.com/site/Global/College-Student-Deals/pcmcat276200050000.c?id=pcmcat276200050000&pageType=REDIRECT&issolr=1&searchterm=college%20student%20deals) and [Apple stores](https://dealsinaz.com/store.apple.com/us/browse/campaigns/education_pricing), you can get an even bigger discount on certain computers and tablets if you're headed to college.

![frys](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/frys.gif)

3\. Bottled Water. At Fry's you can get a 24-pack of bottled water for $1.99. Definitely a stock up price.

![grill](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/grill.png)

You can [get this gorgeous 5-burner grill for just $258](https://dealsinaz.com/linksynergy.walmart.com/link?id=y2DT8GO2AJA&offerid=223073.22018226&type=2) right now at Walmart.

![ketc](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/ketc.jpg)

5\. Condiments. It's grilling time and a great time to stock up on condiments. Right now at Fry's you can get Hunt's ketchup or Kroger BBQ sauce for just a buck. SWEET!

I saw a few pairs of women's jeans at Old Navy for under $10.

![josabank](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/josabank.png)

Check out this [3-button wool herringbone sportcoat for $22.40](http://www.kqzyfj.com/click-4314883-10357302?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.josbank.com%2Fwebapp%2Fwcs%2Fstores%2Fservlet%2FReferralView%3FcatalogId%3D10050%26storeId%3D11001%26productId%3D101927%26p_id%3D2%26link_id%3D2&cjsku=7585), regular price $450!
