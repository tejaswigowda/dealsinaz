Whataburger locations are excited to celebrate teachers this week! Through May 6th, teachers can bring a school ID to get a FREE breakfast item between the hours of 5 and 9 AM. [Head over here](https://stories.whataburger.com/whataburger-to-spotlight-local-educators-during-national-teacher-appreciation-week-may-2-6/) for more information. I called the location near Tatum and Cactus and confirmed they are participating, I would call yours before you make a special trip!

Please share with a teacher in your life!

Enjoy!
