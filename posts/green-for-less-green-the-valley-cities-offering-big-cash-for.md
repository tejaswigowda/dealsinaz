Dreading overseeding this year? If you have a grass lawn and have been considering getting rid of it for desert landscaping or turf, several AZ cities will pay you to do it. Every program requires pre-approval before work begins, so check your local guidelines first! Check out each city's rebate structure and link to application below:

**Phoenix**

- [**The Deal:**](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/waterservices/supply-conservation/save-water/conservation-incentives/residential-grass-incentives.html) **$2.00 per square foot**.
- **The Cap:** Up to a massive **$10,000 maximum payout** per project.
- **The Fine Print:** Requires a minimum removal of 250 square feet of live, healthy grass. Projects must be completed within 6 months of your pre-approval date.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Phoenix Residential Grass Incentives Portal](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/waterservices/supply-conservation/save-water/conservation-incentives/residential-grass-incentives.html)

**Mesa**

- [**The Deal:**](https://wateruseitwisely.com/blog/the-city-of-mesa-is-turning-grass-removal-into-big-water-savings/) **Up to $4,200** per residential property.
- **The Cap:** The standard rebate is normally capped at $2,100, but thanks to temporary matching sponsorships from SRP and Google, the incentive is doubled!
- **The Fine Print:** Homeowners must remove at least 500 square feet of grass and replace it with desert-adapted low-water plants covering at least 50% of the converted area.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Mesa Grass-to-Xeriscape Program Application](https://apps.mesaaz.gov/xeriscape/)

**Gilbert**

- [**The Deal**](https://www.gilbertaz.gov/departments/public-works/water/water-conservation/rebates/residential-grass-removal-rebate)**:** Up to **$1 per square foot** plus a **$1,000 desert plant bonus**.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$3,000 total** ($2,000 base cap for removing 2,000+ sq ft of grass, plus the $1,000 plant incentive).
- **The Fine Print:** Minimum conversion area is 200 square feet. Rebates are applied directly as a credit to your town utility bill.
- **Apply Here:** [Town of Gilbert Water Conservation Rebates](https://www.gilbertaz.gov/departments/public-works/water/water-conservation/rebates)

**Scottsdale**

- [**The Deal:**](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/water/water-conservation/rebates/residential) **$2.00 per square foot**.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$5,000** for residential properties.
- **The Fine Print:** Scottsdale runs their program in seasonal application cycles. Make sure to get city pre-approval before initiating any part of your removal project.
- **Apply Here:** [Scottsdale Water Residential Rebates Page](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/water/water-conservation/rebates/residential)

**Chandler**

- [**The Deal**](https://www.chandleraz.gov/residents/water/water-conservation/rebate-and-incentive-programs/residential)**:** **$1.50 per square foot for grass to Xeriscape**.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$2,000 max** for single-family residences.
- **The Fine Print:** Requires a minimum removal of 500 square feet of non-synthetic grass. The new layout must achieve 50% living plant coverage at maturity.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Chandler Landscape Incentive Guidelines](https://www.chandleraz.gov/residents/water/water-conservation/rebate-and-incentive-programs/large-water-use/rebate-policy)

**Glendale**

- [**The Deal:**](https://www.glendaleaz.gov/Community/City-Services/Water-Services/Water-Conservation-and-Sustainable-Living/Water-Conservation-Rebates) **$1.00 per square foot** for a xeriscape transition, or **$0.25 per square foot** if installing artificial turf.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$3,000 max** for existing home desert landscape conversions. (Artificial turf caps out at a lower $250 max).
- **The Fine Print:** Tiered scales determine the exact payout based on the size of the lawn you pull out.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Glendale Water Conservation Program](https://whiterhinoturf.com/phoenix-turf-rebates-guide/)

**Peoria**

- [**The Deal:**](https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/water-services/water-conservation/rebate-programs/xeriscape-conversion) **Scaled flat rates** based on the total area removed and plant canopy coverage.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$3,000 max**.
- **The Fine Print:** Payout tiers start at $1,500 for converting 1,000 to 1,499 sq ft (with 50% plant canopy) and scale up to $3,000 for projects over 2,500 sq ft.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Peoria Xeriscape Conversion Rebate](https://www.peoriaaz.gov/government/departments/water-services/water-conservation/rebate-programs/xeriscape-conversion)

**Tempe**

- [**The Deal**](https://www.tempe.gov/government/public-works/water/water-conservation/rebates/grass-removal-conversion)**:** **$0.50 per square foot**.
- **The Cap:** Up to **$2,000** per home, per fiscal year.
- **The Fine Print:** The newly converted desert area must feature at least 30% living plant coverage.
- **Apply Here:** [City of Tempe Grass Removal & Conversion Portal](https://www.tempe.gov/government/public-works/water/water-conservation/rebates/grass-removal-conversion)
