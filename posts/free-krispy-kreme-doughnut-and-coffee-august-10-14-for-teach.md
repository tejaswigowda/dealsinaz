Teachers have something to look forward to next week at Krispy Kreme! August 10th-14th they can stop into their closest participating location for a FREE doughnut and coffee. [Head over here](https://www.krispykreme.com/promos/educatorappreciation) to check out the full promotion they're running!

"All week 8/10 – 8/14, teachers can enjoy a sweet treat on us: one free Original Glazed® doughnut and brewed coffee; just show your teacher ID at checkout to redeem this offer. It’s our way of saying thank you for all that you do."

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/4bee2a69-90dc-4834-a60e-6053bbb6b5f8-1-300x151.png)

"Tuesday, August 11 is Straight “A” Tuesday! Stop by your local Krispy Kreme for a FREE Straight “A” Dozen with any dozen purchase. Then, share the Straight “A” Dozen with any educator in your life! Teachers, mentors, parents, siblings, neighbors, friends … you’re all making the grade. The Straight "A" Dozen contains nine Original Glazed® doughnuts and three Straight "A" doughnuts filled with Classic Kreme™ and topped with chocolate icing and sprinkles.

Stop by your local Krispy Kreme on 8/11 OR to redeem online, follow these simple steps:

1\) On 8/11, click [**here**](http://www.krispykreme.com/shop/order-start).

2\) Start your order and add one Straight “A” Dozen and one dozen of your choice to your cart.

3\) Use promo code "STRAIGHTA" at checkout to redeem this sweet offer for delivery or pickup. "

Enjoy!
