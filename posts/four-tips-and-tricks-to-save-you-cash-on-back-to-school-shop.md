It's hard to believe that it's already time for back to school shopping!

Tip #1: Check the ads every Sunday. Starting this Sunday I'm going to be putting together a list of my favorites each week. Remember that you can price match at Walmart and Target. At Walmart you don't even have to have the ads with you!

Tip #2- Keep in mind that there are tons of printable coupons available for school supplies. I [put together a list here.](http://dealsinaz.com/deals/2014/07/hot-new-school-supplies-coupons-print-now.html) [Check out coupons.com](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=118&aff_id=4227&url_id=103&aff_sub5=MTgzMzkxNTA=&affsrc=1) from time to time, they always have new coupons popping up!

![bestbuyyyyy](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/bestbuyyyyy.png)
