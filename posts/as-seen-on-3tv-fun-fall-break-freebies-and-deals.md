I found some great fall break freebies and deals that promise lots of fun without breaking the bank!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/culture.png)

Use your library card to get free admissions to museums all over the valley with the Act 1 Culture Pass. You can actually "check out" two free admissions to fun places like Arcosanti, the Heard Museum, the Phoenix Art Museum, the Irish Cultural Center, and many other museums you may have never been to. You can go online to your library and see availability then go "check out" admission. We used the Culture Passes all the time when my kids were little and I truly believe they are an excellent resource, [head over here](https://act1az.org/culture-pass/) for more information. It's amazing how much you'll learn!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/read.jpg)

The State Fair is happening and Fall Break is an excellent time to check it out! [Every Wednesday we can all get free admission from noon-6 PM when we bring in six cans of non perishable food for St. Mary's Food Bank (note that's 6 cans per person)](https://azstatefair.com/deals-2/). If you can't go then, [head over here to find discount tickets](http://tracking.groupon.com/r?tsToken=US_AFF_0_207410_1838767_0&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.groupon.com%2Fdeals%2Fgl-arizona-state-fair-2019%3Fz%3Dskip%26utm_medium%3Dafl%26utm_campaign%3D207410%26mediaId%3D1838767%26utm_source%3DGPN). You'll save almost half off what you'd pay at the gate and you won't have to stand in line. If you have kids 5-14 make sure you have them participate in the [Read and Ride Program! Fill out a sheet you'll find here](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/2019-Read-Ride-Form-1.pdf) with three books your child has read and they'll get three FREE carnival rides!
