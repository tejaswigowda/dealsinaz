We are definitely in this Coronavirus pandemic quarantine together. Life has completely changed for all of us in the last week or so, especially for restaurants. While restaurant dining rooms are closed, many AZ hot spots are still offering take-out and/or delivery options. This is a fantastic way to help support your favorite restaurants and enjoy a great meal you didn't have to cook...mental health break! _The Today Show_ published a great article about food/delivery safety during Coronavirus, [head over here](https://www.today.com/food/it-safe-order-food-delivery-coronavirus-takeout-safety-t176183) to check it out. I was the take-out person at PF Changs Tempe all through college 20 years ago and still love ordering out with a passion.
