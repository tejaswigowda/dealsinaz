Don't know what to do with all of the Halloween candy in your house? Think about participating in the Halloween candy buyback program. [Head over here](http://www.halloweencandybuyback.com/) to find a participating dentist near you. Most offices have a specified time to come with candy. Kids will earn around $1/lb and the troops will get a sweet treat in the mail.

Participating dentists include:

[J. Phillip Centers for Family and Cosmetic dentistry in Chandler](https://www.facebook.com/DrJPhilipp/photos/a.437016092980848.118605.370880892927702/983369775012141/?type=1&theater). Bring in candy Monday, November 3rd from 9am-5pm and earn $1/lb. (up to 5 pounds).

[Affiliated Pediatric and Orthodontics in Scottsdale](https://www.facebook.com/APDOpc/photos/a.203397298384.157307.152309593384/10152849943268385/?type=1&theater). Bring in candy Monday, November 3rd or Tuesday, November 4th to the Scottsdale office or Tuesday, November 4th or Wednesday, November 5th to the Grayhawk office.

[Feldman Orthodontics in Surprise.](https://www.facebook.com/SurpriseSmiles/photos/a.327947026402.158011.250197346402/10152322946871403/?type=1&theater) Bring in candy all week during normal office hours and kids will earn $1/lb.

And many others! [Click here](http://www.halloweencandybuyback.com/) to see the full list.

I love this program!
