If you love ballet, this is an excellent opportunity to see incredible performances FOR FREE. [Head over here to check out more information about Ballet Arizona's Ballet Under the Stars](https://balletaz.org/ballet-under-the-stars/)

"Join us this fall as we kick off the 2021-2022 season with free events & dance performances for the whole family in Phoenix and across the Valley. During our Ballet Under the Stars performances, you’ll have a chance to see several exciting contemporary and classical ballet excerpts!

Free and open to the public, Ballet Under the Stars allows Arizona communities to enjoy dance in a unique outdoor setting complete with a stage, lighting, costumes, and beautiful Arizona weather.

Bring a blanket or lawn chair and enjoy a varied program, ranging from classical to contemporary. You won’t want to miss this perfect show for the entire family – it is a great way to introduce ballet to your children for the first time!

All performances begin at 7 pm. FREE

- **Wednesday, September 29** – Fountain Park, Fountain Hills
- **Thursday, September 30** – Sahuaro Ranch Historic Park, Glendale
- **Friday, October 1** – Estrella Lakeside Amphitheater, Goodyear
- **Saturday, October 2** – Steele Indian School Park, Phoenix"

Enjoy!
