Rain here means snow in Flagstaff! If you're excited to get on the slopes and have kids under 10, [head over here to pick up FREE season passes for them.](https://www.snowbowl.ski/free-kids-passes/) If you plan ahead you can [head over here](https://www.snowbowl.ski/the-mountain/buy-online-and-save/) and buy everyone else in your party lift tickets for a huge discount (prices start at $35)!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/kid.png)

We went up the day after Christmas and had a great time!

Another savings tip...rent your equipment at Snow Mountain River on the way up. It was $25 for skis, boots, and poles, and $5 for a helmet. The people were really nice, [head over here](https://www.snowmountainriver.com/ski-rentals/) for more information!

Enjoy!
