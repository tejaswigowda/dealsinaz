Thanks to Alicia's Deals reader Amy for letting me know that the [Boyce Thompson Arboretum is offering medical professionals FREE ADMISSION with credentials through this summer (2020)](https://www.facebook.com/btarboretum/photos/a.122176053263/10157599889768264/?type=3&theater). They want to thank these brave men for their bravery and hard work..

Admission is usually $15 for adults and $5 for kids 5-12.

The Arboretum is located an hour outside of Phoenix.
