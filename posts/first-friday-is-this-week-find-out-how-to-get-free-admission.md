[First Friday](https://dtphx.org/do/first-friday-1) is this week in downtown Phoenix (September 4th)! If you haven’t been before, the promoters describe it as: “A neighborhood celebration with art, street vendors, live music, entertainment, amazing food, great craft beer, wine & much more! No cover.” One of my favorite things about First Friday is the opportunity to visit three of AZ’s best museums FREE OF CHARGE!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/download-2025-09-04T134941.220.png)

[The Children’s Museum of Phoenix](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/series/open-for-free-first-friday-night/): Admission is free for all on September 4th from 5-9 PM (first come first serve). Take the kids for an exciting evening of fun and learning! Admission is usually $19 for everyone 1 and over…a family of 4 will save $76! Mark your calendar…admission will also be free on October 2nd, November 6th, and December 4th!

![](https://phxart.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/DSC02184_o2.jpg)

[Phoenix Art Museum](https://phxart.org/firstfriday/): General admission is free for all on September 4th from 5-8 PM. Keep your eyes peeled for my much younger sister Angelica who works there. She'll be so happy to see you! Enjoy!
