TODAY (December 6th) is the First Friday of the month and that means it’s a GREAT night to head to downtown Phoenix for free fun.

Admission is FREE to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM. [Head over here](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/events/free-first-friday-night-2019-12-06/) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/phoenixartmuseum.jpg)

Admission to the Phoenix Art Museum is free December 6th from 6-10 PM. [Head over here](http://www.phxart.org/event/97e1794d-cba9-f126-250b-8d0688dfcd30) for more details.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/heardehard.png)

The Heard Museum is offering free admission tonight (12/6) from 6-10 PM. [Head over here](https://heard.org/event/first-friday-in-december/) for more information and for all of the special events going on.

This is a great way to get out, have fun, and learn A TON (without breaking the bank)!

Enjoy!
