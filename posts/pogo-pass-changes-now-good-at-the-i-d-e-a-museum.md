There are some hot things happening with the POGO pass. The great news is that it's now good at the i.d.e.a. museum! You can still [head over here](http://www.pogopass.com/?tracking=538564ac43871) to get a pass for $39.98 with promo code ALICIA (that's 60% off!). Our family has passes and it's so much fun to use them!

A message I got from them...

"Pogo Pass Holders will receive 2 free admissions to the i.d.e.a. museum with no restrictions other than it won't be available during opening exhibition weekends, which occur 3 times per year.

\*For your information, the AZ Museum of Natural History is no longer on the pass and National Comedy Theatre has changed from 6 visits to 3 visits. We try really hard to offer a variety, but part of our pass is that it is constantly changing (almost always for the better!) the value and variety on the pass is always going to be INCREDIBLE and we hope families understand that!"

My favorite spots are still on the list:

[Sunsplash:](http://www.pogopass.com/golfland-sunsplash) Each pass holder can attend Golfland Sunsplash **one (1) time**. Your pass is good for one (1) SunSplash General admission ticket. Simply present your Pogo pass at Sunsplash Guest Service window to be scanned to obtain waterpark wristband. Regular price to get into Sunsplash is $29.99 so this almost pays for your pass!

[Sea Life Aquarium](http://www.pogopass.com/arizona-sea-life-aquarium): Each Pass holder can attend Arizona Sea life Aquarium **two (2) times**. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front desk to gain admission to the Aquarium. We did this last week and had a blast!

[Phoenix Zoo](http://www.pogopass.com/phoenix-zoo): Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to the Phoenix Zoo **two (2) times**. To gain admission to the Zoo, simply present your Pogo Pass at the admission window. Some experiences require an additional fee. Admission to ZooLights and select special events is not included. They have all kinds of fun water stuff around to keep you cool.

[Stratum Laser Tag](http://www.pogopass.com/stratum-laser-tag): Pass holders can attend stratum **two (2) times**. Each pass holder receives one (1) complimentary game of laser tag each time they attend. Pass is good Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday from 4:30-9.

[Arizona Diamondbacks](http://www.pogopass.com/arizona-diamondbacks): One (1) ticket per game per pass holder for **two (2) home games** at Chase Field for the MLB Regular Season. Check out the [select dates here](http://www.pogopass.com/arizona-diamondbacks).

[Phoenix Jump Street:](http://www.pogopass.com/phoenix-jump-street) Each Pass holder can attend Phoenix Jump Street **two (2) times**. Pass is good for a one (1) hour jump session each attendance. Pass is valid for twelve (12) months. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front counter to gain admission to Phoenix Jump Street on Monday and Wednesday. Limited capacity, first come first serve.

And LOTS LOTS more! Rattlers games, Brunswick bowling, Phoenix Rock Gym, Enchanted Island, Comedy Theater, Skateland, and Tempe Paintball are all included! [Head over here](http://www.pogopass.com/?tracking=538564ac43871) to see everywhere the pass is taken. Don’t forget to use promo code ALICIA to get your passes for $39.98 each (everyone over two will need their own pass). If you go everywhere it’s worth $1500.

Enjoy!
