[If you're looking for a fun fall festival, think about checking out the Tempe Sister Cities Four Peaks Oktoberfest](http://fourpeaksoktoberfest.com/). It's going on October 11th-13th at Tempe Town Lake and admission is totally FREE! Be sure to enjoy a bratwurst and check out the fun areas for the kiddos. I have two personal Oktoberfest stories...first I went to Germany as a student ambassador in high school with Sister Cities from the money raised from this event...if you know a high school Junior in Tempe tell them to apply, [you can find more information here!](http://www.tempesistercities.org/exchange-programs/student-exchange/tempe-sister-cities-2016-student-ambassador-selection-forms/) Second I ran into my husband at this Oktoberfest nineteen years ago. I am a big fan for life.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/hal.jpg)

One of my favorite places to find unique Halloween costumes is thrift stores. [I especially love Savers, they have locations around town](http://www.tempesistercities.org/exchange-programs/student-exchange/tempe-sister-cities-2016-student-ambassador-selection-forms/) and the donations of clothing and household items benefit Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Arizona, an organization near and dear to my heart. J[oin the Super Savers club here](https://sscportal.savers.com/login) and get a coupon for $3 off your $10 purchase or mark your calendar for October 14th when clothing will be half off. Thrifting can be so much fun (and much easier on your wallet)! The treasures you'll find!
