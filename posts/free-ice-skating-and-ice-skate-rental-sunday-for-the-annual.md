The Poppy Cup is back on Sunday, May 3rd at the Scottsdale Ice Den from 6-7:30! Bring cans of food to donate to St. Vincent De Paul and get free skating and skate rental or just sit in a warm room!

Howler from the AZ Coyotes will be there and it should be lots of fun for the whole family. So many Alicia's Deals readers have had a great time skating and supporting this great cause in the past. The temperature is supposed to hit 101 degrees tomorrow, this sounds like a wonderful way to cool off!
