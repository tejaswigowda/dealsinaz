I'm sure you already know how much I love Studio Movie Grill, and this is such a great promotion they offer for special needs kids and their families. [Head over here](https://www.studiomoviegrill.com/Movies.aspx?F=movie_1858_0_0) for more information about the Special Needs Screening of Zootopia going on March 12th in Scottsdale. They'll have screenings April 23 (The Jungle Book), May 7 (Ratchet and Clank), and May 28 (the Angry Birds Movie) that you may want to write on your calendar now.

![zooooo](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/zooooo.png)

A little bit more information about the event: "Special Needs screenings are free for children with special needs and their siblings. Everyone else pays before noon matinee price. Special Needs screenings run with the lights up and the sound down so the children are free to laugh, play, sing and even dance while they watch. Seating will be first come, first served."

Enjoy!
