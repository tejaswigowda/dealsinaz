Love Chipotle guacamole? You're in luck! I was so excited to see that they SHARED their recipe on their facebook page!

"Ingredients:

2 ripe Hass avocados

2 tsp lime juice

2 tbsp cilantro (chopped)

1/4 cup red onion (finely chopped)

1/2 jalapeño, including seeds (finely chopped)

1/4 tsp kosher salt

1\. Choose the right avocado: squishy yet firm with a nice dark green color on the inside.

2\. Cut the avocado in half and remove the pit.

3\. Scoop the avocados and place in a medium-sized bowl.

4\. Toss and coat with lime juice.

5\. Add the salt and using a fork or potato masher, mash until a smooth consistency.

6\. Fold in the remaining ingredients and mix well.

7\. Taste the guacamole; adjust seasoning if necessary."

Another Chipotle tip: I always get a single taco (it's way less expensive and won't completely kill your diet). Bonus: It's only $.75 to add guacamole to a single taco (if they look at you like you're nuts tell them there IS a single taco guacamole add button on the register)!
