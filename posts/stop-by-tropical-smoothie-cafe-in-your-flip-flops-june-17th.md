Mark your calendar for this Friday, June 17th. In honor of National Flip Flop Day, Tropical Smoothie Cafes will give free 12-ounce Sunrise Sunset Smoothies to everyone in flip-flops from 2-7 PM. [Head over here](https://www.cheryls.com/fathers-day-cookie-sampler-fdy22-277231) for more information. The Sunrise Sunset Smoothie is a combination of strawberries, pineapple, mango, and orange juice...yum!

There are currently 15 Tropical Smoothie Cafe locations in the greater Phoenix Area...I highly recommend calling yours before making a special trip to confirm they are participating!

Enjoy!
