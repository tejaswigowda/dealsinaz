You'll save money at stores all over town like Target, Walmart, Fry's, Safeway, Bashas, Albertsons, Walgreens, even Sprouts and Whole Foods. [Target Cartwheel: If you ever shop at Target, you should absolutely have the Cartwheel app.](http://cartwheel.target.com/) They add extra discounts to items all over the store. I love that there is a scanner, so you can scan anything you're buying to see if there's an extra Cartwheel discount. The savings can be significant and it's so easy and fun to use once you get the hang of it. Don't forget that you can get a [Target Red Card](http://goto.target.com/c/28199/174072/2092) to come right out of your bank account and they'll take 5% off all of your Target purchases. ![walmartsavings](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/walmartsavings1.png)

[Walmart Savings Catcher](https://savingscatcher.walmart.com/dashboard): We should all know by now that Walmart will match competitors ads on their products. If you're too busy to deal with the ads, they have a great solution. Snag the [Savings Catcher app](https://savingscatcher.walmart.com/dashboard) and take a couple of seconds to scan your receipt or type in the numbers after you shop Walmart. They will check the competitors ads and if they find that you paid more than you would have somewhere else, they will give you a Walmart eGift Card for the difference. It gives you a little bit of piece of mind that you really are getting the best prices.
