Important: no appointments are necessary but you [must bring in this completed form](http://ktar.com/shortal/walgreens/images/wag_var_eng.pdf) (and children under 7 are not eligible to get the free shot). Head [over here](https://ktar.com/story/2803183/ktar-news-to-partner-with-walgreens-for-free-flu-shots/) for more information.

I got the flu last year and did not have a flu shot (it was awful). I will never again go without the annual vaccine!

If you do have insurance, you should be able to get the vaccine for free at pretty much any grocery store. If you go to Target or CVS you'll also get a $5 off your $25 purchase coupon.
