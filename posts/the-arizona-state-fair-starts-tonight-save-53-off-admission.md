### Arizona State Fair

- **The Arizona State Fair Tradition:** Even older than the place it celebrates—the Arizona State Fair was already 28 years old when Arizona was granted statehood in 1912.
- **Attractions:** All the classic rides will be there, including Le Grande Wheel and the Skyride, along with fried food on sticks, local and professional still and live art from around the community, and special exhibits and attractions such as “Native Spirit,” which shares the dance and culture of the First Nations people.
- **Concerts:** Headliners throughout the month include Billy Currington, The Cult, Stryper, Pat Benatar, and Lee Rice.
- **Grandstand events:** Buckle up for some of the favorite sports of the Southwest, from rodeo riding to figure-eight racing.
