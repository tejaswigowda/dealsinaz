Today's the day!

Chipotle wants you to try their Sofritas entree. I've had it and I liked it! It definitely has a meaty texture (even though it's technically vegan). Buy a burrito, bowl, salad, or order of tacos with Sofritas today (January 26th) and bring your receipt back January 27th-February 28th for a free entree of your choice. [Head over here for more information](https://www.facebook.com/chipotle/photos/a.132022259252.104096.124149619252/10153006939514253/?type=1&theater).
