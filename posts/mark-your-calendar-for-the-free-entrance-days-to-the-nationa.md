Mark your calendars for the 2020 free entrance days to the National Parks!

- **January 20:** [Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/mlk-day.htm)
- **April 18:** First day of [National Park Week](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/national-park-week.htm)
- **August 25**: [National Park Service Birthday](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/nps-birthday.htm)
- **September 26:** [National Public Lands Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/public-lands-day.htm)
- **November 11:** [Veterans Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/veterans-day.htm)

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) for more information.

![everykid](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/everykid.png)

If you have a fourth grader this year, don’t forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! [Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids](https://www.everykidinapark.gov/get-your-pass/) (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2020.

Enjoy!
