![honey](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/honey.jpg)

To make the baked brie I preheated the oven to 350 degrees. I put the brie on a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper and drizzled it with the Savannah Bee Co. honey for cheese (I remember when Ina Garten put brie with honey and have loved it ever since). I would definitely recommend this honey, it really added a nice flavor to the baked brie. I baked for around 7-8 minutes and plated it with raspberries on top (SO EASY even I could do it). It was absolutely DELICIOUS with the Primizie handmade crisp breads.

![albertsons1](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/albertsons1.jpg)

The board with the cheddar, mozzarella, apricots, olives, water crackers, and salame was really easy to put together too. It made for a wonderful Friday night dinner with a glass of wine.
