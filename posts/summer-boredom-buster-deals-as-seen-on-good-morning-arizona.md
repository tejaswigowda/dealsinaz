![mmr](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/mmr.png)

The McDowell Mountain Ranch Aquatic Center is a great place for the kids. There's a water slide, a lazy river, a fitness center, and lifeguards everywhere! Right now you can head over here to get a pass that will get your entire family from Memorial Day to Labor Day for $140 if you're a Scottsdale resident, $210 if you're not. That sale goes through the end of May, in June prices go up to $400 for residents, $600 for non residents. [Head over here](http://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/Assets/Public+Website/sports/MMR+Family+Pass.pdf) for more information. A day pass is $6 for Scottsdale residents, $9 for non-residents.

![culture](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/culture.png)

You can pick up Culture Passes at local libraries. Enjoy two free admissions to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Arizona Science Center, the Phoenix Zoo, and lots of other fun places around town. The passes can be hard to get (they go fast), but some libraries ([like Scottsdale)](http://www.scottsdalelibrary.org/culture_pass) let you check availability online. The ASU libraries have culture passes now too! [Head over here](http://www.act1az.org/findapass) for more information. There are even select performances available for free at some libraries.

![kids](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/kids.png)

Dbacks games are cool and comfortable (love that roof) and a great summer activity! Children should definitely [join the Dbacks Kids Club](http://arizona.diamondbacks.mlb.com/ari/fan_forum/kids_club_form.jsp). Kids get prizes for the first 20 games they attend (there are some great prizes).

![chew](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/chew.png)

Kids can [head over here to join the No Chew Crew](http://arizona.diamondbacks.mlb.com/ari/fan_forum/nochewcrew_form.jsp). Kids promise to say no to chewing tobacco (which is a fabulous idea) and get a free toothbrush and ticket to a Dbacks game in return.

![bases](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/bases.png)

Also, another great reason to stay after games for kids run the bases on Sundays, the Dbacks will donate $1 to PCH for every runner to cross home plate. [Head over here](http://arizona.diamondbacks.mlb.com/ari/ticketing/kidssundays.jsp) for more information.

Enjoy the deals and GO DBACKS!!!
