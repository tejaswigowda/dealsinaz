Veterans Day is a fantastic time to say THANK YOU to all of the brave men and women who have given so much to defend our freedom. Lots of valley restaurants are offering freebies to Active Duty Military and Veterans with ID on 11/11. Here are a few (if you know of others please e-mail them to me at alicia@dealsinaz.com).

[**Applebee's**](https://www.applebees.com/en/veterans-day)**:** "This Veterans Day, Friday, November 11, Applebee’s Neighborhood Grill + Bar will give thanks to all active-duty military, veterans, Reserves, and National Guard with the offer of an in-restaurant complimentary full-size entrée from an exclusive menu. And when dining at Applebee’s on November 11, these special guests will also receive a $5 Bounce Back Card to redeem for dine-in, To Go, or delivery within a three-week redemption window. Visit [Applebee's](https://www.applebees.com/en/veterans-day) website or [click here](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221103005205/en/Applebee%E2%80%99s%C2%AE-to-Offer-Veterans-and-Active-Duty-Military-a-Free-Meal-on-Veterans-Day-Friday-November-11) for details." Thanks, [Military.com](https://www.military.com/discounts/applebees-veterans-day-military-discount)!

[**Bar Louie**](https://www.barlouie.com/): "This Veterans Day we’re honoring the men and women who have served and are serving in the US Armed Forces, with a FREE Craft Burger of their choice. \*Dine in only. Valid on 11/11/22 only, with a valid military ID. Offer may vary by location." [Head over here](https://www.barlouie.com/) for more information on their website.

[**BJ’s Restaurants**](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay): Current and former military who dine in-restaurant on November 11th will enjoy a complimentary meal from the select menu ([see here](https://www.bjsrestaurants.com/VeteransDay)) and a Dr. Pepper beverage.

[**Denny’s**](https://www.dennys.com/news/dennys-salutes-military-heroes-free-grand-slams-veterans-nov-11): Is offering all active duty and retired veterans a FREE build your own Grand Slam breakfast from 5 AM until noon 11/11.

[**Dunkin’ Donuts**](https://news.dunkindonuts.com/blog/veterans-day-free-donut): All active duty military and veterans can enjoy a free donut on 11/11!

[**Great Clips:**](https://fb.watch/gJ99BeeqVO/) "This [#VeteransDay](https://www.facebook.com/watch/hashtag/veteransday?__eep__=6%2F&__tn__=*NK-R), we're continuing our favorite tradition of showing gratitude and appreciation for veterans and active military members by offering free haircuts in salon"

[**Logan’s Roadhouse**](https://logansroadhouse.com/): This Veterans Day, Logan's Roadhouse is inviting all veterans, active duty and retired U.S. military personnel to enjoy a free meal\* between 3-6 p.m. from our Veteran's Day menu at participating locations.

[**Macaroni Grill**](https://twitter.com/MacaroniGrill/status/1590370016427667456): "Help us spread the word. This Veterans Day, 11/11, all veterans and active military receive a free Mom's Ricotta Meatballs + Spaghetti with military ID. [#MacGrillGratitude](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MacGrillGratitude?src=hashtag_click)"

[**Macayo’s**:](https://www.facebook.com/photo?fbid=493463412827384&set=a.452421243598268) "For Veterans Day, we are proud to offer all Active & Non-Active Military Personnel a free entrée on us! Join us Friday, November 11th to enjoy a free entrée off our special Veterans Day Menu."
