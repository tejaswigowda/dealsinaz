National Donut Day is getting more and more exciting! Stop by your closest participating Edible Arrangements location to get their "donut" free on Friday (6/1)! [Head over here for more information on the Arcadia facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/EdiblePhoenixArcadia/photos/a.537018349826984.1073741828.529225127272973/851268071735342/?type=3&theater), I also saw [Chandler](https://www.facebook.com/events/188164388678673/), [Scottsdale](https://www.facebook.com/EdibleArrangementsScottsdale/photos/a.827529177438623.1073741828.827395010785373/883397278518479/?type=3&theater), and [North Scottsdale](https://www.facebook.com/events/1690511621034956/) are participating. I would definitely call your store before you make a special trip.

"Crafted with crisp, donut-shaped Granny Smith Apple slices hand-dipped in real, gourmet chocolate, this new treat brings a whole new meaning to freshly made donuts. Don’t miss your chance to try one for free! NOTE: Limit one donut per customer. While supplies last."

Enjoy!
