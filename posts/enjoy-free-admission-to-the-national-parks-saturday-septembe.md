September 24th is National Public Lands Day and to celebrate admission to ALL National Parks will be FREE! [Head over here](https://home.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) to see all of the fee-free days and [here to see the places you could see in AZ](https://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm).

To give you an example, admission to the Grand Canyon usually runs $35/car so this is significant savings! If you've never been, there's no time like this Saturday!

Enjoy!
