Krispy Kreme is announcing two weeks of Barbie doughnuts and to celebrate anyone who wears pink on September 7th will get a FREE Original Glazed. [Check out what they said on their press release](https://investors.krispykreme.com/news/news-releases/news-details/2024/KRISPY-KREME-Celebrates-65th-Anniversary-of-Barbie-with-New-Doughnut-Collection-Full-of-Flavor-Flair-and-Fashion/default.aspx):

"To help further celebrate the Barbie™ x Krispy Kreme Collection, all guests who wear pink and visit a participating Krispy Kreme shop on Sept. 7 can receive a free Original Glazed® doughnut, limit one per guest in-shop and via drive-thru only."
