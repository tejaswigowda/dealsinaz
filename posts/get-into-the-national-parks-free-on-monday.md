If you're looking for something to do on Monday (Martin Luther King Jr. day), why not go to a National Park? It's completely free for everyone this Monday the 18th. [Head over here](http://www.nps.gov/findapark/feefreeparks.htm) for more information and to find a park you want to see! If you have lived here for a long time and you still haven't seen the Grand Canyon, here's your chance to get in for free.

Enjoy!
