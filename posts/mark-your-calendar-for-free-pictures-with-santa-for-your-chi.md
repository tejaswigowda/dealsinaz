If your kids are excited to see Santa this year, [head over here](https://www.cabelas.com/shop/en/santas-wonderland?searchTerm=santa) to make a reservation for a time slot to see the man in red at Bass Pro Shops or Cabela's. ![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/274029b6-65d7-421a-8350-6ac660b2a88b-1.png)

If you'd love a picture of your pets with Santa, Petsmart has you covered! December 4th, 5th, 11th, 12th, 18th, and 19th they'll be offering free pictures from 12-3 PM. [Head over here](https://www.petsmart.com/ca-PetSmart-Events.html) for more information (and check out other free pet parties)! I called the location closest to me and they said they'll e-mail you the digital photo (keep checking [dealsinaz.com](../walmart-deals-have-dropp/) for photo freebies to print it out).
