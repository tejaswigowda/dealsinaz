If you're on the west side of town, life could get a whole lot sweeter this weekend. Head to the grand opening of the newest Creamistry, located at 1235 N. 98th Avenue in Park West. From 12-2 PM Saturday, September 18th, they are offering free ice cream to all.

Check out the press release they sent over:

**"**Creamistry, the liquid nitrogen sweet shop that creates custom frozen desserts before guests’ eyes, is celebrating the grand opening of its latest location with a free small ice cream for each guest from noon to 2:00 p.m. on Saturday, September 18. Located at 1235 North 98th Avenue in Park West, Peoria’s premier dining and entertainment center located, this is Creamistry’s first West Valley store.

Former Arizona Cardinal player Andre Wadsworth and real estate professional Ryan Zeleznak, who co-own and operate Arizona’s Creamistry locations, selected Park West for their latest location as they feel it meets a need for fresh, custom ice cream creations in the West Valley.

“With each new Creamistry we open, we look for a dynamic, highly populated and energetic location, which we’ve found in Park West,” says Wadsworth. “We look forward to becoming a part of the West Valley community’s celebrations and everyday occasions for years to come.”

Creamistry uses high-quality premium ingredients with more than 20 flavors and a wide variety of topping options including natural and organic selections such as non-dairy water-based sorbet and gluten-free, egg-free vegan coconut and cashew. Flavor choices include Cap’N Crunch, Fruity Pebbles, Birthday Cake, Cookie Butter, Matcha Green Tea, Reese’s Peanut Butter, Ruby Cacao, Taro and Nutella as well as classics such as Caramel, Chocolate, Madagascar Vanilla Bean, Mint, Sea Salt Caramel and Strawberry.

Toppings and upgrades include mini gummy bears, mini marshmallows, Heath bar, Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups, freshly baked brownie bites, cookie dough bites, cheesecake bites, “unicorn dust,” fresh fruits and nuts, sauces such as caramel, chocolate fudge and strawberry sundae and savory items like candied bacon, chamoy and Tajin. Signature creations, premium ice cream cakes and grab-and-go items round out the selections at Creamistry.

Strategically located in front of the open-air shopping center’s splash pad, the new Creamistry store offers families, shoppers and ice cream lovers of all ages the same fresh and innovative made-to-order liquid-nitrogen ice cream creations as the other Creamistry locations in the Valley of the Sun — including one in North Scottsdale and one in Gilbert.

Creamistry Park West is open daily from noon to 9:00 p.m. For more, visit [www.creamistry.com](http://www.creamistry.com/)."
