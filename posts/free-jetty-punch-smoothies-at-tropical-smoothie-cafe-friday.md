Friday, June 17th from 2-7 PM, Tropical Smoothie Cafe is celebrating National Flip Flop Day with FREE Jetty Punch smoothies for everyone in flip flops. They're raising money for Camp Sunshine, I just called the Camelback location and they said they're going to have a great raffle going on too!

[Head over here](http://www.tropicalsmoothiecafe.com/funraising) for more information.

"Each year, [Camp Sunshine](https://www.campsunshine.org/) offers countless families the opportunity to put aside the stress and severity of having a child with an illness and just have fun. It costs $2,500 to send one family to camp for a week, but thanks to the compassion of people like you, these families are able to attend completely free of charge."

What a great organization!

Enjoy!
