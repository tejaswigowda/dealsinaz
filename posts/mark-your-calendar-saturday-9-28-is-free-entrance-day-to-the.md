If you don't have plans next Saturday (9/28) it's a great day to visit a National Park or two! In honor of National Public Lands Day, admission to all of the National Parks will be free! [Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) for more information. Admission will also be free on Veterans Day (11/11).

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm) to see all of the National Parks in AZ...there are so many beautiful and interesting places to visit!

Enjoy!
