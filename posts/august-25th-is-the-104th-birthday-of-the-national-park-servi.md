August 25th is the 104th birthday of the National Park Service! It's also a fee-free day to visit any one of the beautiful National Parks (including the Grand Canyon)!

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/nps-birthday.htm) for more information and [head over here](https://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm) to check out all 22 of the National Parks in AZ!

Enjoy!
