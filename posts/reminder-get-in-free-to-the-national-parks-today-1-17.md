The National Park Service has [updated its free park days for 2022](https://home.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm)! Mark your calendar now so you're ready to see the beauty these parks have to offer!

- "**January 17:** [Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/mlk-day.htm)
- **April 16:** First day of [National Park Week](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/national-park-week.htm)
- **August 4:** Anniversary of the [Great American Outdoors Act](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/infrastructure/gaoa.htm)
- **September 24:** [National Public Lands Day](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/public-lands-day.htm)
- **November 11:** [Veterans Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/veterans-day.htm)"
