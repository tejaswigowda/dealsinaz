HOW TO ENTER : To enter the Sweepstakes entrants must do the following:

1. [Click here](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=2187&aff_id=4227) via a desktop or mobile browser,
2. Click the "Push to Start" button,
3. Provide First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, valid Email Address, and select your State, City, and nearest Albertsons Store on the Sweepstakes Submission Form,
4. "Spin" to receive four (4) product images,
5. Select an Introduction,
6. Caption each of the four (4) product images with at least ten (10) characters including spaces, but not more than two hundred and fifty (250) characters,
7. Select a Conclusion, and submit your entry.

Sweepstakes entrants can increase the value ("Miles") of the contest prize by submitting more entries and sharing those entries across any number of popular social networks. Entry and social network sharing contributions are accrued at the following rates:

- For each entry submitted via the Sweepstakes submission form the entrant contributes ("Sweepstake Entry"), the entrant contributes one (1) mile to the prize value.
- For each social media network the entrant selects to share their Best Road Trip. Ever! entry on ("Social Media Shares"), the entrant contributes one (1) mile to the prize."

"ODDS OF WINNING: Odds of winning are based on eligible number of entries.

SELECTING THE WINNER: The total number of votes submitted during the Voting Period for each entry will be tabulated by Sponsor and the five (5) Entrants with the most votes, as determined by Sponsor, will be declared the finalists ("Finalists"); of the Finalists, one (1) Grand Prize Winner ("Grand Prize Winner") will be randomly selected. Sponsor will use Random.org ("Random Selection Website") to select the Grand Prize Winner from the five (5) Finalists."
