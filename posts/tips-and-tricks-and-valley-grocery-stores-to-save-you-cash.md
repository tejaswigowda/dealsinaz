Onto the tips:

![albertsons](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/albertsons.png)

[Albertsons:](http://dealsinaz.com/deals/2014/09/its-back-10-off-your-entire-purchase-at-albertsons-when-you-wear-your-az-cardinals-jersey-any-sunday-or-game-day-during-the-regular-season.html) Wear your Cardinals jersey on Sunday or Cardinals game day to save 10% off your ENTIRE grocery purchase during the regular season. If you spend $100 a week on groceries, that's $10 a week!

![bashassss](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/bashassss.png)

[Bashas'](https://www.facebook.com/BashasSupermarkets/app_214309071924895): They will double your coupons up to $.50!

![frys](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/frys1.gif)

[Fry's : They offer facebook coupons each Wednesday](https://www.facebook.com/frysfoodstores/app_528458793852754), usually for $2 off your $40 purchase or $3 off your $50 purchase. You download the coupons directly to your VIP card. They also offer a Fry's Friday freebie many Fridays. [Head back here](http://kroger.softcoin.com/programs/kroger/freefri/?origin=freefri&ytp=001034004077000000000000&banner=Frys) this Friday to get a FREE Greek yogurt (must download the coupon on Friday, you'll have 2 weeks to use it).

![safewayjust4u](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/safewayjust4u.jpeg)

[Safeway:](http://www.safeway.com/ShopStores/Offers-Landing-IMG.page?cmpid=co_kw_swy_j4u_br_ggl) [Safeway will make all coupons worth at least $1. If you see a coupon you like worth $.10 off online or in the paper,](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=118&aff_id=4227&url_id=103&aff_sub5=MTg0NTgxOTE=&affsrc=1) it's still worth a buck at Safeway.

[Use their Just4U app to save 20-30% off things you already buy.](http://www.safeway.com/ShopStores/Offers-Landing-IMG.page?cmpid=co_kw_swy_j4u_br_ggl) If you shop Safeway, you absolutely should be using this app (or you can use your computer).

![sprouts1](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/sprouts1.jpg)

[Sprouts](https://www.sprouts.com/how-to): The day you shop matters big time at Sprouts. Wednesdays are double ad days. You'll enjoy all of the savings from two weeks of ads.

![walmartsavings](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/walmartsavings.png)

[Walmart: They still match ads and now they have a new Savings Catcher program. Click here to check it out.](https://savingscatcher.walmart.com/) Scan your receipt (or type in your receipt number) and they'll check all of the ads in town to make sure you're getting the lowest prices on everything you buy. If you spent more on something than you would have somewhere else, you'll get a Walmart gift card for the difference.

Enjoy the savings!
