I love that we get to celebrate Veterans for two days this year! Here's a list of freebies Vets and Active Duty Military can enjoy TODAY (11/12). Just bring along your military ID and you'll be good to go.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/phoenixzoooooo.jpg)

[Head over here for more information](https://www.phoenixzoo.org/veterans-day-discount/?fbclid=IwAR0ndUYys8Xm8WFcjqniS4hgSNjjbTLNENY3feI_GbuoYs0U7DPQKNrz3QQ).

[Denny’s](https://www.dennys.com/): Vets and active duty military can stop by Denny’s on November 12th from 5 AM to noon for a free create your own grand slam.

They’ll have entertainment all day, and lots of fun for all!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/cleancleanclean.jpg)
