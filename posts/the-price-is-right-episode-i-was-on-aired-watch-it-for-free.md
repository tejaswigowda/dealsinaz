![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/theprice-1.png)

If you would love to “Come on Down” too, [head over here](https://priceisright.com/) to request your free tickets! If you pick Priority tickets, you’re GUARANTEED a seat as long as you’re on time. There are STILL dates available while I’m typing this! You’ll have to get yourself to California for the taping on the date you pick. Once you arrive, you'll wear a number and go in groups to "try out" for the producer, who will choose the contestants. My advice is to bring every bit of energy and enthusiasm you've got into your audition. You may go and not get called (this is probably the most likely outcome), but hopefully you have a great time! No matter what, you'll leave the show a member of the "Price is Right family".
