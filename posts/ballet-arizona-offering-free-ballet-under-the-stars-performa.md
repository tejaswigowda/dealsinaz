I'm so happy to see that Ballet Arizona is doing Ballet Under the Stars again this year! [Head over here](http://balletaz.org/ballet-under-the-stars/) for more information about the performances at locations all over town. They're all free and loads of fun.

**"September 12** – \*\* NEW LOCATION Tempe Sports Complex, Tempe

**September 13** – Fountain Park, Fountain Hills

**September 14** – Estrella Lakeside Amphitheater, Goodyear

**September 15** – Steele Indian School Park, Phoenix

All Performances begin at 7:00 PM

**_Join us September 12-15 as we kick off the season with free events & dance performances for the whole family in Phoenix and across the Valley. During our annual Ballet Under the Stars program, you’ll have a chance to see several exciting pieces of dance! Don’t miss ballet performances in Phoenix, Tempe, Goodyear, & Fountain Hills this September._**

**_Free_** and open to the public, Ballet Under the Stars allows Arizona communities to enjoy dance in a unique outdoor setting complete with a stage, lighting, costumes, and beautiful Arizona weather.

Bring a blanket or lawn chair and enjoy a varied program, ranging from classical to contemporary. You won’t want to miss this perfect show for the entire family – it is a great way to introduce ballet to your children for the first time!"

My daughter loves this every year!

Enjoy!
