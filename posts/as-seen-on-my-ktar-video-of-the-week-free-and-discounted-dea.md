You might already know that I do a weekly video for KTAR's website. This week I talked about all of the fun going on downtown this weekend (a lot of the fun is FREE)!

![comiccon](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/comiccon.jpg)

Phoenix Comicon is coming June 5th-8th. [You can head over here](https://www.facebook.com/phoenixcomicon) to get discounted tickets through Wednesday night. Why pay full price when you don't have to?!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/firstfriday.png)![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/trolley.png)

If you’ve never been to First Friday in Downtown Phoenix, this might be a great week to try it out!

![childrensmusu](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/childrensmusu.png)

Admission is free for everyone to the [Children’s Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM](http://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/events/target-first-fridays-2013-09-06/) and the [Phoenix Art Museum from 6-10 PM](http://www.phxart.org/visit/hours_admission_location). Adults 18 and over get in free to the [Arizona Science Center for their Adults Night Out](http://azscience.org/who_are_you/this_month_at_the_center).

There’s a [free trolley too!](http://artlinkphoenix.com/first-fridays/)

![dunkin2](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/dunkin2.jpg)

Also on Friday keep in mind that it's National Doughnut Day! On your way downtown stop at Krispy Kreme for a free doughnut (no purchase required) or Dunkin' Donuts (buy a drink to get a free donut).

![bobblehead](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/bobblehead.png)

There's lots of fun at the Dbacks game too. The first 20,000 fans through the gates will get a [free Martin Prado bobblehead on June 7th](http://arizona.diamondbacks.mlb.com/index.jsp?c_id=ari).

![lo al](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/lo-al.jpg)

If you feel like eating out while you're downtown, [head over here to check out Local Dines](https://www.localdines.com/phoenix?ref=332). They have half price certificates to all kinds of restaurants you'll enjoy like Nobuo at Teeter House, Province, the Vig, and more!

Enjoy!
