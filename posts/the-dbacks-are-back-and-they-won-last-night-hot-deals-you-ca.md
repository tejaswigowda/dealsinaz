Diamondbacks baseball is BACK! We lost our opening day game to the Giants (who won the world series last year), but we WON last night!

![taco](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/taco.jpg)

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs at home OR AWAY (thanks Mike for the update), the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase. ~~That means today (4/8) is free taco day!~~

![papa](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/papa1.jpg)

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at [Papajohns.com](http://order.papajohns.com/order.html) with promo code DBACKS50! ~~That means today (4/8) is half off pizza day!~~

![jumbo](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/jumbo.png)

Anytime a Dback "jacks" a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! ~~Since we had two big home runs last night, you can take advantage of this deal today (4/8).~~

![stike](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/stike.jpg)

[Download the Circle K app.](http://www.circlekaz.com/dbackspromo) After every home game you'll get a deal based on the "strike-outs"! Check out the "deals" in the menu at the top of the app. ~~Today (4/8) you can get a free cookie, brownie, or slice with your app (no purchase required).~~
