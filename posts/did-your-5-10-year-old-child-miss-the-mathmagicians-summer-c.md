Kaitlyn Markham and Karina Zavari, founders of MathMagicians, had their first 1-week camp for 5-10-year-old kids with ADHD and/or Dyslexia a couple of weeks ago and it was a huge success...there was even a big waitlist! ABC 15 came by and did a story about the camp (you can [see it here](https://www.abc15.com/news/uplifting-arizona/teens-nonprofit-adding-up-to-major-success-as-math-camp-kicks-off)) and Good Morning Arizona interviewed the girls before the camp (you can [see that interview here](https://www.azfamily.com/video/2023/05/18/phoenix-teens-create-summer-camp-kids-struggling-with-math/)). The Catholic Sun wrote an article (you can [see that here)](https://www.catholicsun.org/2023/05/30/news-brief-xavier-2-gators-x-service-math-magicians-camp/).

I was helping out at the camp checking kids in and out (full disclosure Kaitlyn is my daughter and Karina is her lovely friend) and one mom told me that her daughter did not want to go at first but came away saying it was the "best thing ever". Another mom said her son couldn't wait to get home and call his aunt to demonstrate his new division skills. The feedback was incredibly positive and the kids left saying they liked math more at the end of the week than they had at the beginning of the week.
