Scroll through all 297 restaurants [here (they're still adding to the list)!](https://arizonarestaurantweek.com/all-restaurants/?type=restaurants-2&sort=a-z)

My top 5 picks this year:

![Cleaverman and Filthy Martini Bar Open](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSwnCSs-ghpYrCv1YnYMctm1b9sC2UxqupVov9wJk-oLA&s=10)

[**Cleaverman Steakhouse**](https://arizonarestaurantweek.com/listing/cleaverman-steakhouse/) brings **old-school glamour** to the Arizona Center. Occupying the former Hooters location—a nod to the past remains via a commemorative photo in the kitchen—this sophisticated eatery delivers exceptional cuisine and crafted cocktails. Their multi-course Arizona Restaurant Week dining options offer **remarkable value**, featuring curated 3-course menus at **$33, $44, and $55** price points.
