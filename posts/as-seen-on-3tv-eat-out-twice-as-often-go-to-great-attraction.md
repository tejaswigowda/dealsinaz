![dbg](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/dbg.png) ![dbg2](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/dbg2.jpg)We should all take advantage of the fun around town, especially when it's free!

The Desert Botanical Garden is free the second Tuesday of each month from 8AM-8PM. [Head over here](http://www.dbg.org/about-the-garden/before-you-visit#free) for more information about the free admission opportunity!

![heard](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/heard.png)

First Friday is always lots of fun in downtown Phoenix and it's a wonderful way to enjoy culture FOR FREE. Visit [the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program.](http://heard.org/)Everyone will get into the museum for $0 from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids), and there will be lots of fun events for the whole family.

![firstfridayart](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/firstfridayart.png)

Still have energy? [Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event](http://www.phxart.org/event/4185a053-4e19-e0a3-487b-b621d35a9a73). Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids).

![adultsnightout](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/adultsnightout.png)

[If it’s date night and you’re leaving the kids at home on First Friday, head to Adults Night Out at the Arizona Science Center](http://azscience.org/who_are_you/adults_night_out_event). Adults will get in free from 5:30-9 (tickets are usually $16.95) AND they’ll have an open bar and lots of fun!
