If you're anywhere near a Chick Fil A on July 14th you won't want to miss dress like a cow day! Go to the location of your choice partially dressed like a cow to get a free entree or go full out head to toe and get a free meal.

[Head over here](http://chickfila.tumblr.com/faq) for more information and [here](http://chickfila.tumblr.com/tagged/cad_pdf) to actually print off a cow starter kit!

Enjoy!
