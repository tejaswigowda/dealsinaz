Earth Day is Wednesday, April 22nd! There are some deals and freebies to help us celebrate our home planet (and think a little bit more about taking care of it)!

![slashtrash](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/slashtrash.gif)

[Head over here for more information about the Slash the Trash event in Tempe from 11-1!](http://www.tempe.gov/city-hall/public-works/recycling-and-solid-waste) Highlights include free brats and chips courtesy of Gordon Biersch, goodies, music, and more. Thanks Trish!

![disney](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/disney.png)

Head to your closest participating Disney store to get a [free reusable tote bag Wednesday with any purchase](http://stores.disneystore.com/co/aurora/566/?utm_source=Disney+Store&utm_medium=Website&utm_campaign=Store+Locator). I called the Fashion Square and Arrowhead locations and confirmed that both will have the free bags while supplies last.

![rainfor](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/rainfor.png)

If you have a 5-10 year old, [head over here](http://www.anthropologie.com/anthro/category/HOME-EARTH-DAY.jsp?cm_mmc=Facebook-_-2015_Anthropologie-_-4_15_15_Earth_Day_Childrens_Events-_-Earth_Day_Events_&utm_medium=Facebook&utm_source=Social&utm_content=Earth_Day_Events_#/arizona) to check out the free crafts Anthropologie has going on this weekend in honor of Earth Day. You must RSVP to attend!

![Flower](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Flower.jpg)

Why not have a great meal and support a great cause? Head to Flower Child in Arcadia and 100% of Earth Day proceeds will go to benefit three community partners (Arcadia High School's new garden, Arizona Audubon, and the Rotary Club of Arizona). [Click here](http://www.foxrc.com/blog/flower-child-peace-love-and-earth-celebrate-earth-day-with-us/?utm_source=2015_04_20_Flower_Child_Earth_Day&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2015_04_20_Flower_Child_Earth_Day) for more information. I am SO EXCITED for Flower Child to open their new Scottsdale location!

![organic](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/organic.png)

Earth Day is a great time to start thinking about buying organic. Sprouts has a [huge Organic sale going on through Wednesday, April 22nd](https://www.sprouts.com/)! You'll be able to take advantage of double ad day on Wednesday too!

![earthmonth](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/earthmonth.jpg)

Head to your closest participating Origins location to get a free Eco-Chic bag with purchase. I called the Biltmore location and they confirmed that they have bags!
