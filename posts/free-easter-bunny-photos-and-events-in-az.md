If you have small children, you'd probably love to get their picture taken with the Easter Bunny this month! I found a few options in AZ that won't cost a cent:

Desert Ridge Marketplace: Head [over here](https://shopdesertridge.com/event/bunny/) for more information about their Bunny Hop celebration.

"Hop on over for the ultimate spring celebration at Desert Ridge Marketplace on **Sunday, April 13 from 12PM to 2PM** inside The District!

Snap a free photo with some bunny special and enjoy an egg hop throughout The District, live entertainment, giveaways, crafts and so much more.

\*This event is free and open to the public. Activities are available while supplies last.

\*Free and open to the public. Photos are available on a first come, first serve basis. You will need to provide your email address and/or phone number to receive a digital download of your image.

In addition to the Bunny Hop celebration, you can snag a free photo with some bunny special on the additional date below.

**Friday, April 18 – One Last Chance for Free Bunny Photos**

**5:30pm – 7pm**

**Near AMC Theatres inside The District**"

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/download-88.png)

The Shops at Norterra:

Head [over here](https://theshopsatnorterra.com/bunny/) for more information about their Easter bunny photo opportunities this year April 11th-13th and 18th-19th from 10 AM until 7 PM.

Hop close to pose with the Easter Bunny! Families & furry friends alike, don your best dressed against a spring-soaked backdrop.

Photos to be taken on your own personal device on a first-come, first-served basis. No reservations allowed.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/download-89.png)

Downtown Mesa: Head [over here](https://downtownmesa.com/events/easter-eggstravaganza) for more information about their FREE Easter Eggstravaganza on April 19th from 10 AM until 1 PM.

"Start off by hippity-hoppitying down the Main Street bunny trail to hunt for Easter eggs along each side of Main Street and at over 17 participating downtown businesses.

After the egg hunt, join the rabbit of the hour, the Easter Bunny, for free eggs-clusive photos, Easter crafts at the Arts & Crafts station or play some fun Easter games and more!

**Egg Hunt:** Hunt for over 11,000 eggs along Main Street & at participating businesses starting at 10am

**Activities:** At The Plaza located at 56 E. Main Street 10am-1pm"

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/download-90.png)

Bass Pro Shops: Head [over here](https://www.basspro.com/b/easter) to check out their available times for FREE photos with the Easter bunny! They'll give you a 4x6 print. I just called the Mesa location, and they said if you don't see reservations available, you are welcome to come and stand in line; they can't guarantee how long it will take.

Let me know if you know of others to add to the list!
