![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/star.png)

Starbucks has announced that through May 3rd anyone who identifies as a Covid-19 front line responder will get a free tall hot or iced coffee. [Head over here](https://stories.starbucks.com/stories/2020/navigating-through-covid-19/) to read the details.

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/polarpopcoffee.png)

Circle K announced on their facebook page that they're offering free coffee, tea, or Polar Pops to all first responders and health care workers on the front line with ID through April 30th. [Head over here to check out their post.](https://www.facebook.com/CircleKStores/photos/a.1780263635568533/2557613864500169/?type=3&theater)

![](http://52.12.186.239/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/glazed.jpg)

Krispy Kreme has announced that each Monday through May 12th they will be giving away free dozen original glazed doughnuts to all healthcare workers with employer badge. [Head over here](https://krispykreme.com/response-to-covid19) for all of the details.

Enjoy!
