~~If you're looking for an~~ [~~end-of-summer day trip, remember that Friday, August 4th, is a fee-free day for all National Parks!~~](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm)

Check out the remaining fee-free days this year (2023):

- "**August 4:** Anniversary of the [Great American Outdoors Act](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/infrastructure/gaoa.htm)
- **September 23:** [National Public Lands Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/public-lands-day.htm)
- **November 11:** [Veterans Day](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/npscelebrates/veterans-day.htm)"

[Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/state/az/index.htm) to check out a full list of National Parks in AZ. You would usually spend $35 to get your car into the Grand Canyon, August 4th is a great day to go for free and a great excuse to go if you've never been.
