It's the First Friday of the month and that means it's a GREAT night to head to downtown Phoenix for First Friday fun. Admission is FREE to the Children's Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM. [Head over here](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/events/free-first-friday-night-4/) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/women.png)

Admission is free to the Phoenix Art Museum from 6-10 PM. [Head over here](http://www.phxart.org/event/87502147-d4df-3799-5900-f56b5e4cb2a7) for more information about the free day and the special exhibition "In the Company of Women" (also free tonight).

![](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/first.jpg)

Admission to the Heard Museum is free from 6-10 PM. [Head over here](http://www.phxart.org/event/87502147-d4df-3799-5900-f56b5e4cb2a7) for more information. Pick one museum or try to hit all three (save huge and learn a TON)! Enjoy!
