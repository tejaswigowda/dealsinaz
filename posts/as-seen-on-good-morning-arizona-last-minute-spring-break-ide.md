Want to go swimming? [Take the kids to the Kiwanis wave pool in Tempe. It's indoors and the pool is heated.](http://www.tempe.gov/Home/Components/News/News/3643/) There's a big water slide, I used to go here all the time when I was a kid. Through March 19th you'll get up to two kids 7 and younger in for free with each adults admission. Adults are $4 and kids are usually $3.

![cabin](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/cabin.jpg)
