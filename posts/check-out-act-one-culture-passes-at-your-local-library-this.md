Spring break is almost here! If you're staying in town and looking for something to do, you might want to check out your Act One Culture Passes at your local library. [Head over here](https://act1az.org/culture-pass/#) for more information about the program. There are a limited number of passes to many local museums you can "check out" at each library (you must do this in person). At many libraries (like Scottsdale and Tempe) you can look online to find out if the culture pass you're looking for is checked out before you make a special trip (but keep in mind it could be gone by the time you get there). Each pass is good for two free admissions (use within seven days). Check out the list of museums you have to choose from at Central Arizona Libraries:
