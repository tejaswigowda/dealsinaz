Think about reserving produce boxes for MUCH less than you'd pay at the grocery store. [Borderlands Produce Rescue](https://borderlandsproducerescue.org/) offers boxes with up to 70 pounds of fresh produce to pick up on Saturday mornings at select locations around the valley. You can [reserve a drive up and take away box here](https://borderlandsproducerescue.org/programs/redistribute/duta-information/) for $21.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/bor.png)

[Community Action Market and Produce](https://www.farms2tableaz.org/reserve-a-produce-box-prepacks2go) offers prepackaged boxes at locations around the state for $16. You can [reserve a box here](https://www.farms2tableaz.org/reserve-a-produce-box-prepacks2go).

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/download-4.png)
