Would you like to take your family out for a great night of learning and fun FOR FREE? Check out downtown Phoenix! This Friday, September 6th, three fantastic museums will offer everyone free admission at various times.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/cd1e686a-fc62-4482-b206-dbded07ed2a1.png)

First on the list is the Children’s Museum of Phoenix! This fun and interactive place is perfect for the little ones in your life. Admission is usually $17/each (adults must pay too), but on Friday, September 6th from 5-9 PM it will be free for everyone. [Click here](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/event/open-for-free-first-friday-night-3/2024-09-06/) to read what the Children’s Museum of Phoenix wrote about Free Friday capacity limits and recommendations.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/fe.jpg)

Next up is the Phoenix Art Museum. For PhxArt Afterhours they're offering free admission to all from 5-9 PM! They promise live music, art-making workshops, entertainment, and more. Tickets are usually $25 for adults and $18 for kids. There is a charge for the special exhibit, [head over here](https://11000a.blackbaudhosting.com/11000a/tickets?tab=2&txobjid=6f425ad3-14b4-4e0d-bd77-dbaeb8da25b4) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/HEARD1.jpg)

Last but certainly not least on the list is the Heard Museum. [Check out the very important event they have going on this month:](https://heard.org/event/sept-first-friday/)

"Join us this September, the Heard Museum invites you to a special First Friday event in recognition of the National Day of Remembrance for U.S. Indian Boarding Schools. For 24 years, our exhibition _Away From Home_ has shed light on a significant period in American history when Indigenous children were separated from their families to attend government-run boarding schools."

I called to confirm that admission is free for all on 9/6 from 4-8 PM. Admission is usually $22.50 for adults and $9 for kids.

Enjoy!
