Doesn't salad sound good when it's 110 plus outside?! Thanks to Alicia's Deals reader Amy for letting us know that Kids eat free at Sweet Tomatoes on Tuesdays this summer! Limit one free kids meal (12 and under) for each adult meal purchased at regular menu price.

I called the Scottsdale and Desert Ridge locations (they are participating), but you may want to call your location before you make a special trip to make sure that they're honoring the deal.

Enjoy!
