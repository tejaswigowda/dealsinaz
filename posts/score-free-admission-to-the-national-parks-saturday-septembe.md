If you don't have plans on Saturday, September 28th you might want to visit a National Park! Admission will be FREE in honor of National Public Lands Day! [Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm) to see the other fee-free days this year. [Head over here](https://www.nps.gov/findapark/advanced-search.htm?s=AZ&p=1&v=0) to see a full list of the National Parks in Arizona. Of course we have the Grand Canyon, but you might also love exploring Montezuma Castle, Sunset Crater, Walnut Canyon, or one of the other beautiful parks in our state.

Enjoy!
