Hooray! Today (2/6) is First Friday! If you don’t have plans, you may want to head to Downtown Phoenix to enjoy the gorgeous weather, some beautiful art, an Ollie the Trolley ride, and some FREE museums! Anyone that says Phoenix has no culture has obviously never been to First Friday!

![kid](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/kid.png)

Head to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix where everyone will get in free from 5-9 PM. Tickets are usually $11 each, so this is quite an opportunity to play for less!

![colgate](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/colgate.png)

Also at the Children's Museum of Phoenix, from 2-6 PM the Colgate Bright Smiles Bright Futures van will be there offering kids free dental screenings and free dental supplies! [Head over here f](http://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/events/bright-smiles-bright-future-van/)or more information. I would plan it so you can enjoy free admission after your screening!

![heard](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/heard.png)

[Visit the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program.](http://heard.org/firstfridays/) Everyone will get into the museum FOR FREE from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids). This month the theme is “Hoop it Up," and they'll have a hoop dance world championship contest going on! Sounds like fun!

![firstfridayart](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/firstfridayart.png)

Still have energy? [Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event](http://www.phxart.org/event/4185a053-4e19-e0a3-487b-b621d35a9a73). Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids). [Head over here](http://www.phxart.org/event/4185a053-4e19-e0a3-487b-b621d35a9a73) for more information and to see exhibits on display.

The Arizona Science Center isn't participating in Adults Night Out this month, but they'll be back in March!

Enjoy!
