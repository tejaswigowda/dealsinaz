[First Friday](https://dtphx.org/do/first-friday-1) is this week in downtown Phoenix! If you haven't been before, the promoters describe it as: "A neighborhood celebration with art, street vendors, live music, entertainment, amazing food, great craft beer, wine & much more! No cover." One of my favorite things about First Friday is the opportunity to visit three of AZ's best museums FREE OF CHARGE!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/download-2025-09-04T134941.220.png)

[The Children's Museum of Phoenix](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/exhibits-and-experiences/free-first-friday-night/): Admission is free for all on September 5th from 5-9 PM. Take the kids for an exciting evening of fun and learning! Admission is usually $19 for everyone 1 and over...a family of 4 will save $76! Mark your calendar...admission will also be free on October 3rd!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/1c61774d-ee08-4a51-a554-b302ba0d1291.png)

[The Heard Museum](https://heard.org/event/first-friday-sept2025/): Admission is free for all on September 5th from 4-8 PM. Admission is normally $26 for adults and $10 for children.

"Celebrate art, culture, and community with an evening full of inspiration and connection...all of our galleries will be open--including two standout exhibitions you won't want to miss."

"Come early and stay cool as you explore the museum, make memories in the [Heardlings Family Activity Room](https://heard.org/family-fun/), and stop by [The Museum Shop](https://heard.org/shop/) for beautifully crafted items by Indigenous artists and authors. Our Coffee Cantina will also be open until 7:30 p.m. for refreshing Prickly Pear Lemonade, snacks, and adult beverages to enjoy under the twinkly lights outside the museum entrance."

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/download-2025-09-04T140546.428.png)

[Phoenix Art Museum](https://phxart.org/firstfriday/): General admission is free for all on September 5th from 5-8 PM. "Separate tickets are required to view special exhibition _The World of Anna Sui_...at an additional cost. Click [HERE](https://11000a.blackbaudhosting.com/11000a/tickets?tab=2&txobjid=48d0cdb7-008a-44e1-a93e-74e25f38904b) to purchase special exhibition tickets."

"Featured programming includes:

· DIY zine workshop with Wasted Ink Distro

Create your own magazine inspired by punk, rock-and-roll, and other genres and cultural influences that have inspired fashion designer Anna Sui for decades

· Create your own accessory workshop with There Space

Use art nouveau motifs, bohemian patterns, and more to channel your own inner rock-star aesthetic

· Outfit inspo consultations with fashion designer and artist Leonor Aispuro

Learn how to spruce up any outfit drawing inspiration from nature and sustainable fashion sources.

· Pop-up shops with zines and accessories by Wasted Ink Distro and Artdillo Eclectic

· DIY ‘90s slap bracelet activity

· Posing + photoshoot with Mackenzie Rich from Afternoon Studios

· In-gallery sketching with Aileen Martinez

· Nostalgic ‘80s and ‘90s hits with resident DJ JME-LEE

· Drinks with ARTenders

· Docent-led Public Tours @ 5:30 pm + 6:30 pm"

Enjoy!
