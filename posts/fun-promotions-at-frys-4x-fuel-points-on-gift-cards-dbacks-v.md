If you're a Fry's shopper you probably already know about some of the promotions they offer. Paper coupons [are always worth at least $1](http://strk.enlnks.com/aff_c?offer_id=118&aff_id=4227) (at the moment at Phoenix area stores, they can change that policy), they [offer a weekly facebook coupon](https://www.facebook.com/frysfoodstores/app_528458793852754) (this week it's [$2 off your $40 purchas](https://www.facebook.com/frysfoodstores/app_528458793852754)e), and they [offer a Friday freebie](http://kroger.softcoin.com/programs/kroger/freefri/?origin=freefri&ytp=001034004077000000000000&banner=Frys) (on September 26th [head over here for a free bag of 3 Musketeers bites](http://kroger.softcoin.com/programs/kroger/freefri/?origin=freefri&ytp=001034004077000000000000&banner=Frys)).

![Fry s   Digital Coupons](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Fry-s-Digital-Coupons.png)

[Head over here to load a new coupon for 4X fuel points when you buy gift cards](http://kroger.softcoin.com/programs/kroger/digital_coupons/?filtertag=FT4x2014&assettag=at4x2014&origin=4x2014&ytp=001001004159123115000000&banner=Frys). You can also download extra coupons like $5 off your Buca di Beppo gift card, $5 off your Cold Stone Creamery gift card, and special deals from Xbox live and Staples.

![dbacksvip](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/dbacksvip.jpg)

One fun program is the Dbacks Rewards. [Head over here for more information and click "enroll now".](https://www.frysfood.com/topic/d-backs-vip-rewards) You have until September 28th to collect points and until October 28th to redeem them for new merchandise, autograph memorabilia, and more. I just checked my account and I have 142 points...enough for a hat, ball, license plate frame, and more. I'm only 8 points away from other great prizes. Look for Dbacks rewards tags on items you're buying to collect points. They did send me a fun box with fuzzy Dbacks slippers, Shamrock coupons, Gatorade, and a few other things in exchange for blogging about this and I'm just thrilled to really know about the program. I would have had no idea I had a free Dbacks goody waiting for me! Thanks David!

[Head over here](https://www.frysfood.com/topic/promotions) to check out all of the promotions...if you're shopping there anyways, you might as well save!

Enjoy!
