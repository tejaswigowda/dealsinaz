Heading to QT anytime soon? Head over [here to download the QuikTrip app for Apple devices,](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/quiktrip/id1044651221) or [here to download the app for Android devices.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.quiktrip.com.quiktrip)

Log in and check out the "offers" tab at the bottom. You should see a coupon good for a Big Q drink with a one-time use barcode (their unsweet tea is pretty good). I also saw a coupon for a $.50 small Quikshake and other fun deals! The Big Q drink coupon expires 12/18.

Enjoy!
