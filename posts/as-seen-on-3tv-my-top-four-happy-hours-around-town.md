If you keep tabs on what day and time you're going out on the town, you can save a TON of money! Check out my top four happy hours for serious savings (and fun)!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/a10224f6-c89d-478b-b121-0a745f142fb9.png)

[Pita Jungle-](https://www.pitajungle.com/menu/happy-hour-tapas/tapas) If you're ever anywhere near a Pita Jungle from 3-6 any day of the week, I definitely recommend stopping in. The whole family can enjoy amazing tapas for as low as $2 at select locations (I would call before you make a special trip, thanks to Alicia's Deals reader Jenny who let me know the Queen Creek location does not participate in tapas deals), the food is excellent and healthy! Happy hour is good throughout the restaurant and there are great drink specials too for everyone 21 and up.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/wine-by-glass-p40h8rxpdx7g8lx64nhudt4fftide1l0u9bzkmnuoe.png)

[Postino](https://www.postinowinecafe.com/locations/arizona/arcadia/)- The time of day makes a HUGE difference to the price you'll pay at Postino. Glasses of wine and pitchers of beer are just $5 until 5 PM. Also, mark your calendar for Monday and Tuesday nights. After 8 PM you can get a full bottle of house wine and a board of bruschetta for just $25 total.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/bar-happy-hour-area.jpg)

[Roaring Fork -](http://roaringfork.com/scottsdale/) I've always enjoyed the Happy Hour at The Roaring Fork in Scottsdale! From 4-6 PM you can enjoy $6 glasses of wine, $7 cocktails, and discounts on their delicious food. The Roaring Fork "Big Ass" Burger is huge and delicious!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/download-56-2.png)

[Buck and Rider](https://www.buckandrider.com/happy-hour/#drinks)- I saved my favorite Happy Hour spot for last. Every day from 3-7 in the patio bar, Abby's Oyster bar, and alcove you can enjoy delicious food and drink specials. They fly in their seafood each day so it's incredibly fresh, and at happy hour you can enjoy oysters for $2.25 each and Wild Mexican Shrimp for $2.50 each.

Enjoy and cheers!
