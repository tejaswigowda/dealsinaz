If you've been reading Alicia's Deals for awhile, you know how much I love Groupon. They offer great deals on fun experiences all over town. There's a new addition to the [Groupon site you might not know about called Groupon Coupons.](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores) [Head over there](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores) before you shop to see what kinds of FREE offers you can pick up.

![orbitz](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/orbitz.png)

For example, let's say that you're booking a hotel room with Orbitz. [Groupon Coupons](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores/orbitz.com) has codes available that will save you up to an extra $150 off hotel bookings and an extra 15% off hotels.

![sams](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/sams.png)

Want to go to Sam's Club but don't have a membership? [Groupon Coupons has a free one-day shopping pass](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores/samsclub.com) for you.

![birchbox](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/birchbox.png)

Thinking of sending Birchbox gifts this year? [Groupon Coupons](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores/birchbox.com) has codes that will get you $10 off your $60 purchase or 20% off your purchase of $50 or more.

![harry](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/harry.png)

Going to Harry & David? [Take along a 25% off in store coupon from Groupon Coupons.](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/stores/harryanddavid.com)

It's definitely worth checking out before you shop! You could save at Target, Nordstrom, Sephora, Macy's, Victoria's Secret, Loft, Gap, Payless, and lots of other great stores.
