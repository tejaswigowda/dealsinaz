![firedpie](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/firedpie.jpg)

This Wednesday, June 25th the brand new Fired Pie on the Greenway Hayden loop (15425 n Scottsdale rd #100 Scottsdale, AZ 85254 phone 480.636.7704) is celebrating their GRAND OPENING! They’ll be giving EVERYONE who stops in a FREE pizza or salad (one per person). The salads and pizzas are DELICIOUS! Bring a friend and you can share both!

![cowapprecia](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/cowapprecia.png)

You may want to add July 11th to your calendar if you like Chick Fil A! [Head over here](http://www.chick-fil-a.com/Cows/Appreciation-Day) to for more information information about Cow Appreciation Day. If you dress like a cow from head to toe you’ll get a free meal! If you’re dressed in partial cow attire you’ll get a free entree.

True story: Once I was running behind (as always) and feeling the tiniest bit shy dressing up, so the kids and I wore our kids costumes through the drive through and they honored the deal. You might want to ask your location if they’ll do that!

You can actually [head over here](http://media.chick-fil-a.com/Media/PDF/Cows/Appreciation-Day/Cow_Starter_Kit.pdf) for a cow starter kit. My daughter Kaitlyn was so excited to get to be on Your Life A to Z in her cow costume!
