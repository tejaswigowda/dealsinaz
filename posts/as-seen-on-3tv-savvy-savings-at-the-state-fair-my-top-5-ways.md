Check out my top 5 ways to have just as much fun for less: ![feel-good-fridays updated](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/feel-good-fridays-updated.png) Adults will pay $20 at the gate, but EVERYONE can bring eight cans of food on Fridays from noon until 6 PM to get in free. You'll have fun at the fair and feed the poor; definitely a win/win! You'll need eight cans for each free ticket; [head over here](https://azstatefair.com/deals-promotions/) for all of the details. ![Foodie Friday](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Foodie-Friday.png) Also on Fridays, all 93 food vendors will offer $5 tasting portions of iconic faves from noon to 6 PM. I have a list of [ALL available $5 goodies here](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Foodie-Fridays-Map-1.pdf). I'm personally excited about the small loaded nachos at Alicia's Mexican Kitchen. Love her name. ![read and ride](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Untitled-September-25-2026-at-09.59.54-5.png) The State Fair knows that reading and fun go together. Kids 5-14 can fill out [Read and Ride Reports to get up to three free rides. An adult 18+ must be present with the child picking up the free rides, and printable reports are available here.](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ASF2026-Read-and-Ride-Form-1.pdf) ![Featured image for post: Rafi Day at the Arizona State Fair](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Untitled-September-01-2026-at-15.13.16-4.png) Saturday, October 17th from noon-4 PM, everyone can get free admission for Rafi Day when they attempt the Rafi look-alike contest. You must [register and request your free tickets through this link. Winners will take home up to $ 3,000; there's $15,000 up for grabs.](https://arizona-state-fair.hive-pages.com/rafi-look-a-like) ![parking shuttle](https://azstatefair.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/parking-shuttle.jpg) Don't pay for parking! The regular lot will cost $20 per car, but if you park at the FREE Phoenix College starting at 5 PM Friday, they'll offer complimentary shuttles to the entrance. Saturday and Sunday, the free shuttles will run starting at 12 PM. [Head over here](https://azstatefair.com/fair-directions-parking/) for more details.
