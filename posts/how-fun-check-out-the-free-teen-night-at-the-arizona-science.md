If you have a high school student, this looks like LOTS of fun! Saturday, March 30th from 6-10 PM teens can head to the Arizona Science Center for a FREE game night ([request free tickets here](https://www.azscience.org/events-programs/programs-by-age/teens/#)). This is supported by the Flinn Foundation and includes a food and drink ticket.

Check out the details:

### **"Presented by Arizona Science Center's Teen Advisory Board**

**Saturday, March 30 | 6 to10 p.m.**

**High School Teens Grades 9 –12 Only. | FREE General Admission**

**Includes one (1) food ticket and one (1) drink ticket**

Are you game? Grab your friends and get ready for Game Night! Join us for a nostalgic night filled with your all-time favorites — Battleship, Operation, and Candyland! Explore the science from classic games, play life-sized Chess, and ball out at the board game tables! Have a Smash Bros showdown as you play on a five-story screen in our Irene P. Flinn Giant Screen Theater. Discover your parent's childhood while playing Oregon Trail. Chill out and BYOBG (Bring Your Own Board Game), snack on free food and drinks, or compete against your friends to win it all. Come down and show your friends who the real winner is...or take the L.

**SORRY! This event is only for high school teens! BYOBG (Bring Your Own Board Game) "**
