Krispy Kreme is definitely making life sweeter lately! They're turning National Doughnut Day into National Doughnut WEEK! June 1st-5th you can stop by each day for a FREE doughnut. I called the Phoenix location and they said there is no purchase required but you may need to download the app. It doesn't say that online, [head over here](https://www.krispykreme.com/promos/nationaldoughnutweek) for the details, but you may want to [download it or join their rewards program here](https://www.krispykreme.com/more-smiles/rewards) just in case (they give you lots of fun treats).

I think this would definitely be worth some extra walks!

Enjoy!
