Flagstaff Extreme's newest attraction is the [Adventure Zip Lines](http://flagstaffextreme.com/zip-line-course/), boasting more than 30 zip lines suspended from 15 to 80 feet high in the tall Ponderosa Pines. Adrenaline junkies will revel in traversing the more than 40 elements the course has to offer, including ladders, bridges, and more. While the course is self-guided, each guest will receive training to prepare themselves for a day of adventure and exploring among the trees.
