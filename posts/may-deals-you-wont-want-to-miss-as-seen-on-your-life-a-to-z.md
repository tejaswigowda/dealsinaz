There are lots of May deals to take advantage of, you just have to know about them!

![kellys](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/kellys.jpg)

At [Kelly's at Southbridge throughout the month of May](https://www.facebook.com/KellysAtSouthbridge) they're offering half off a burger when you buy one.

![pogopass2](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/pogopass2.png)

[Pick up your POGO passes now](http://www.pogopass.com/) with promo code ALICIA to keep the kids busy all summer (and throughout the year) for just $39.98 each. You'll get into Sunsplash (that almost pays for the entire pass right there), the Sea Life Aquarium, the Phoenix Rock Gym, the Phoenix Zoo, Stratum Laser Tag, Dbacks games, Skateland, and lots more. If you go everywhere the pass is worth $1500! Such a great value for entertainment. No one in Arizona should get bored this summer!

![kidsbowl](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/kidsbowl.png)

If your kids love bowling, you're in luck! With the [Kids Bowl Free program](http://www.kidsbowlfree.com/), kids can sign up to get two free games of bowling every single day at bowling alleys across the state. Glenfair Lanes in Glendale and Let it Roll Bowl in Phoenix are part of the program and [Brunswick lanes have their own program](http://www.bowlbrunswick.com/about-us/114/1). I love that it's air conditioned fun.

Stock up for summer and enjoy the deals!
