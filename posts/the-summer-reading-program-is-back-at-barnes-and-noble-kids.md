If you have a child going into 1st-6th grade, [head over here](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/blog/kids/get-ready-for-bns-summer-reading-triathlon/) for more information about the summer reading program going on at Barnes and Noble. Kids going into 1st-6th grades can read 8 books, [fill out this form](https://dispatch.barnesandnoble.com/content/dam/ccr/pdf/2019/summer-reading/123233A-05-bn-tear-sheet-bndotcom.pdf), and bring it into a B&N store August 1st-31st for a free book (there is a list to choose from).

Enjoy!
