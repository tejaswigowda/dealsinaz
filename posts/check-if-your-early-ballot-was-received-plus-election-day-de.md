I sent in my mail-in ballot last week and I started wondering how to be sure it would be counted. If you're wondering about the status of your ballot too, [head over here](https://recorder.maricopa.gov/earlyvotingballot/earlyvotingballotstatus.aspx) to check. If it says "Ballot was received and counted" you're good to go!

![rec](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/rec.png)

Are you not 100% sure if you're registered for this election? [Head over here](https://recorder.maricopa.gov/earlyvotingballot/voterregistrationlookup.aspx) to check your status, you can also [head over here](http://recorder.maricopa.gov/pollingplace/) to see which polling place to go to.
