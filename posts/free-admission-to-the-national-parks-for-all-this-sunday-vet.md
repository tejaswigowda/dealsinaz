If you're looking for something fun to do this Veterans Day weekend, why not head to a National Park?! [Admission is FREE on Sunday, November 11th for all.](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/fee-free-parks.htm)

If you know a 4th grader, [let them know they can get their family into the national parks free all year with the Every Kid in a Park program, they can head over here to sign up!](https://www.everykidinapark.gov/)

If you live in AZ and haven't been to the Grand Canyon, this is a perfect opportunity!

Enjoy!
