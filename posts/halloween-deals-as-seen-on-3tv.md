One of my favorite deals of the whole year is the Halloween candy buy back program. [Head over here](http://www.halloweencandybuyback.com/) to find a participating dentist or orthodontist near you. Here's the basics...your kids have tons of fun trick or treating then pick their favorite pieces. Then they go to a participating dentist and get paid $1-$2 a pound for the candy. Then the candy gets donated to the troops overseas! The troops know you're thinking of them, the kids get less cavities, and everyone is happy!

If you're in Gilbert, [J. Philipp Centers for Family and Cosmetic dentistry is paying $2 a pound on November 2nd from 8AM until 4 PM](https://www.facebook.com/DrJPhilipp/photos/a.826168854065568.1073741843.370880892927702/1983713241644451/?type=3&theater). You don't have to be a patient!
