Speaking of ballet, mark your calendar for September 22nd-30th. [Ballet Arizona is presenting Ballet Under the Stars, completely FREE performances at five locations across the valley.](http://balletaz.org/community-outreach/ballet-under-the-stars/) They're going to Goodyear, Fountain Hills, Phoenix, Tempe, and Sun City West. This is a great way to expose the kids to ballet, or just enjoy the amazing Arizona weather.

![res](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/res.png)

If you could use a date night, keep in mind that Arizona Restaurant Week is coming September 16th-25th. This is the best week to try that restaurant you've been wanting to try forever. Make [reservations NOW for places like T Cooks at the Royal Palms, Top of the Rock, El Charro, even chef Wiley's own Cafe Zuz](http://arizonarestaurantweek.com/)u. There are three course menus that are $33 or $44, this is one of the best values in dining.

On Thursday nights they do the same thing with whiskey.
