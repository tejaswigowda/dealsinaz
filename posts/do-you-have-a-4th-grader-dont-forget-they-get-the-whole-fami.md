If you have a fourth grader this year, don't forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! [Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids](https://www.everykidinapark.gov/get-your-pass/) (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2017.

My daughter Kaitlyn is in 4th grade and we can't wait to take the family to Yellowstone this summer.

Please share with anyone you know with a 4th grader or 4th grade teachers!

If you don't have a 4th grader, [head over here to see the fee free days for all in 2017](http://dealsinaz.com/deals/2017/01/mark-your-calendar-now-for-fee-free-days-to-see-the-national-parks-there-are-15-in-az.html)!
