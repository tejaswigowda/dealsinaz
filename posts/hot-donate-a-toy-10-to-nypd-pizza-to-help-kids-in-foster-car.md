Here's a way to give back to our community AND get enjoy delicious FREE pizza! [Head over here](https://www.facebook.com/NewYorkPizzaDepartment/photos/rpp.65398751954/10157492349921955/?type=3&theater) for more information about the #everykiddeserves toy drive going on at NYPD Pizza through December 19th. Bring a new, unopened toy worth $10+, get a coupon good for an 8 inch one topping pizza. Bring in four toys, get four coupons. They are donating the gifts to AZ kids in foster care (did you know there are over 17,000?).

General gift ideas include books, makeup, body wash sets, stuffed animals, sports equipment, dolls, Legos, puzzles, movies, jewelry, hair accessories, ear phones, gift cards, what you think a child would enjoy.
