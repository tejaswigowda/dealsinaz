If you've been thinking about getting a newpet for your family to love, you'll LOVE this! Adoption fees are waived June 1st-7th. [Head over here](https://www.azhumane.org/adopt/) for more information, to see available pets, and schedule an appointment.

"To help celebrate the 20 year anniversary of our South Mountain Campus, longtime generous supporters Stuart and Ted Tufty have donated all adoption fees from June 1st to June 7th."

Thank you so much Stuart and Ted Tufty!

Enjoy!
