Cirque du Soleil is coming to town February 18th and the show is supposed to be amazing! They sent me the photo above of a couple of performers having fun on Mill Avenue. I wish I had been there!

![varekai](https://dealsinaz.com/deals/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/varekai.png)

[You can head over here to get tickets.](https://www.cirquedusoleil.com/en/shows/varekai/tickets/phoenix.aspx) The best prices I've found are on opening night and children pay less (be sure to select the childrens seats or the family four pack).

A little bit more about the show:

"Deep within a forest, at the summit of a volcano, exists an extraordinary world-a world where something is possible. A world called _Varekai._

The sky lets go a solitary young man, and the story of _Varekai_ begins. Parachuted into the shadows of a magical forest, a kaleidoscopic world imbued with fantastical creatures, a young man takes flight in an adventure both absurd and extraordinary. On this day at the edge of time, in this place of pure and undiluted possibility, begins an inspired incantation to a life rediscovered and to a newly found wonder in the mysteries of the world and the mind. "

I can't wait to see the talented performers.

Enjoy!
