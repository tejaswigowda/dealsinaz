QT is offering EVERYONE a free self-serve drink in their app to commemorate Teacher Appreciation Week! Head over [here to download the QuikTrip app for Apple,](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/quiktrip/id1044651221) or [here for Android.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.quiktrip.com.quiktrip) Log in and in the offers tab you'll find a barcode good for a FREE self-serve drink (good through 5/11).

Enjoy!
