Arizona Restaurant Week starts TODAY and goes all the way through September 28th. It's a great chance to make reservations for a restaurant you might not visit regularly!

[Head over here](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurants/) to check out the participating restaurants. Prices are $33 or $44 and if you see two forks, that means the price is for two people. Some of the really great deals are the places that are giving three-course meals INCLUDING drinks for two for $44! Places that fall into that category are [The Parlor](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/the-parlor/), [Z Tejas](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/ztejas-southwestern-grill-scottsdale/), [The Dhaba](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/the-dhaba/), [Rock Lobster](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/the-sushi-room/), [Isabel's Amor](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/isabels-amor/), and [Fuego Cityscape](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/fuego-cityscape/).

The places I would be most excited to try are [Donovan's Steak and Chop House](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/donovans-steak-and-chop-house/) (it's our favorite), [Lon's at the Hermosa](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/lons-at-the-hermosa-2/) (so beautiful), and [Binkley's](http://www.arizonarestaurantweek.com/restaurant/binkleys/).

Enjoy!
