What a great win-win! Head to your closest Tropical Smoothie Cafe wearing flip flops or sandals on Friday from 2-7PM (National Flip Flop Day) and get a FREE 24 oz. Jetty Punch Smoothie (strawberry/banana)!

You can [head over here](http://www.tropicalsmoothie.com/funraising) for more information.

They'll be accepting donations for Camp Sunshine, a wonderful retreat where children with life threatening illnesses can go with their families. You can find out [more about the place here](http://campsunshine.org/).

Maybe you can go after you [spend time at Big Surf for the world's largest swim lesson](http://dealsinaz.com/deals/2014/06/get-into-big-surf-for-free-on-friday-for-their-worlds-largest-swim-lesson.html) (you'll get in for free at 7 AM and be able to stay as long as you want)!

Enjoy!
