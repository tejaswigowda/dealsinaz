[Head over here](http://www.chick-fil-a.com/Cows/Appreciation-Day) to for more information information about Cow Appreciation Day on July 11th. If you dress like a cow from head to toe you'll get a free meal! If you're dressed in partial cow attire you'll get a free entree.

True story: Once I was running behind (as always) and feeling the tiniest bit shy dressing up, so the kids and I wore our kids costumes through the drive through and they honored the deal. You might want to ask your location if they'll do that!

You can actually [head over here](http://media.chick-fil-a.com/Media/PDF/Cows/Appreciation-Day/Cow_Starter_Kit.pdf) for a cow starter kit.

Enjoy!
