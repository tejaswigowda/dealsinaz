This is a fantastic win/win deal for all! If your kids have tons of candy they don't really need, why not ask them to do something nice and get paid for it?

[Head over here](http://www.halloweencandybuyback.com/) for more information about the Halloween Candy Buy Back Program along with a list of participating dentists near you. Dentists and orthodontists around town are registered to buy candy back from kids for $1 per pound (up to five pounds per kid). They donate the candy to the troops overseas.

If you're on the East side, check this out (your kid could earn $2/pound):

**"J PHILIPP DENTISTRY ANNUAL CANDY BUY BACK PROGRAM**

Tuesday, November 1st

$2 per pound (up to 5 pounds per child)

Dr. J Philipp Dentristry at 3230 S. Gilbert Road, Suite 4 (Gilbert/Queen Creek Rd)

8a.m. – 4 p.m. to donate

**Halloween Candy Buy Back Program**

Parents can have kids choose their favorite pieces and donate the rest. On Tuesday, November 1, Dr. Philipp will continue his Halloween tradition of paying $2 per pound (up to 5 pounds per child) for candy. The candy will be shipped to American troops as part of Operation Gratitude. Sending candy to troops provides a ‘taste of home’ and lets them know that children appreciate their service. Parents can bring their children to Dr.Philipp’s office in Chandler located at 3230 S. Gilbert Road, Suite 4 from 8 a.m. – 4 p.m. to donate."

Enjoy!
