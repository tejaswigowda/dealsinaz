If you're on fall break this could be an EXCELLENT activity! The Desert Botanical Garden offers free admission the second Tuesday of each month (which means today 10/8 we all get in free)! They are open until 8 PM.

From [their website](https://dbg.org/visit/plan-your-visit/): "The Garden hosts Community Day the second Tuesday of every month, where admission is complimentary._"_

General admission is usually $24.95-$29.95 for adults so this is truly a bargain!

Enjoy!
