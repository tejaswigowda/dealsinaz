If you know a fourth grader (or their parents), they'll definitely want to hear about this deal! All fourth graders are eligible for a FREE National Parks pass good through August 31st, 2025. Head [over here](https://www.everykidoutdoors.gov/how.htm) for more information and to request your pass.

We got these passes for both of our kids when they were in fourth grade and saw some amazing places...Yellowstone, Yosemite, Arches, Grand Teton, and many more! We love exploring America's natural wonders and try to visit new parks often. This summer we're going to see Glacier!

I'm so glad the government is still offering this freebie (for now)!

Enjoy!
