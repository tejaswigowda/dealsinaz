Holiday events can cost your family hundreds of dollars. Check out these fun FREE activities I found that won't break the bank:

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/395341355_828481359285377_6975379453671238072_n.jpg)

Stop by [“Sparkling Surprise”](https://www.surpriseaz.gov/1285/Sparkling-Surprise)! If you happen to be on the west side, you won’t want to miss the “Sparkling Surprise” light display. There are over 200,000 lights, a 40-foot Christmas tree, giant candy canes, light features on the lake, and lots more. Unlike many other events around town, this is COMPLETELY FREE and will be glowing nightly from 5:30-10 PM through January 2nd. [Head over here](https://www.surpriseaz.gov/1285/Sparkling-Surprise) for more information.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/dc43cc12-94c7-4c1c-a99f-44c2d504dc1f-1.png)

Get [Free Pics With Santa at Bass Pro Shops](https://www.basspro.com/shop/en/santas-wonderland?irclickid=1J41OdRXtxyPWc%3A3X%3AWv4TD8UkFStMUnPwOO0g0&irpid=13970&irmpname=ShopHer%20Media&irsharedid=302333&irgwc=1&hvarAID=impact&cm_soc=ShopHer%20Media)! You don’t have to pay anything to get a fantastic pic with Santa. [Head over here](https://www.basspro.com/shop/en/santas-wonderland?irclickid=1J41OdRXtxyPWc%3A3X%3AWv4TD8UkFStMUnPwOO0g0&irpid=13970&irmpname=ShopHer%20Media&irsharedid=302333&irgwc=1&hvarAID=impact&cm_soc=ShopHer%20Media) to make an appointment in Mesa or Glendale. Their Santa is always fabulous, there are lots of activities for the kids, and you’ll go home with a free print. We did this every year when the kids were little. The store is fun to explore too!

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/a0f8a234-0bc4-4f66-8433-271f0d70eb6b.png)

Watch [The Polar Express on Frank Kush Field at ASU on December 16th](https://asu365communityunion.com/polar-express)! Bring a blanket and good cheer to watch The Polar Express FOR FREE on the big screen at Sun Devil Stadium! The kids will love sitting on the field! They’ll have a holiday market starting at 4 PM and the movie will start at 6. [Head over here](https://asu365communityunion.com/polar-express) to register for this completely free community event.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/feat-event-live-nativity.jpg)

Another free stop on the [holiday tour in AZ is Scottsdazzle](https://scottsdazzle.com/events/). Take the family for a FREE walk around the lights and art displays. On December 16th there will be a live nativity happening at the Old Adobe Mission Church downtown from 6:30-8:30, [head over here](https://scottsdazzle.com/event/live-nativity-celebration/) for more information. There will be live animals to pet in the manger, free refreshments, and more. See the beauty of the season come to life! I love that parking is free too!

Enjoy and Happy Holidays!
