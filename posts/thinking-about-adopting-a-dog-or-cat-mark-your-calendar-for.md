If you've ben thinking about adopting a dog or a cat this holiday season, you won't want to miss this! Pedigree is sponsoring "Shelter Sunday". They'll reimburse up to $300,000 of adoption fees for pets adopted on November 26th, [head over here](https://www.pedigree.com/sheltersunday) for more information.

### "**Q: How can I receive my reimbursement for adopting?**

**A:** Pet parents who adopt a dog on Sunday, November 26, 2023, can submit their adoption receipt from 11/28/23 to 12/22/23, alongside your name, email address, physical address and dog’s name, by visiting PEDIGREE.com/ShelterSunday and the PEDIGREE brand will reimburse your adoption fee, while supplies last."

I highly recommend getting your receipt in early on the 28th to make sure you get your reimbursement!

Enjoy!
