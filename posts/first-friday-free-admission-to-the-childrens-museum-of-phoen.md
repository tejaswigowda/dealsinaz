Would you like to take your family out for a great night of learning and fun FOR FREE? Check out First Friday in downtown Phoenix! This Friday, June 7th, three fantastic museums will offer everyone free admission from 5-9 PM.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/cd1e686a-fc62-4482-b206-dbded07ed2a1.png)

First on the list is the Children's Museum of Phoenix! This fun and interactive place is perfect for the little ones in your life. Admission is usually $17/each (adults must pay too). [Click here](https://childrensmuseumofphoenix.org/event/open-for-free-first-friday-night-3-6/) to read what the Children's Museum of Phoenix wrote about Free Friday capacity limits and recommendations.

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/fe.jpg)

Next up is the Phoenix Art Museum. Four times a year they offer free admission on Frist Friday from 5-9 and 6/7 is one of the dates (also mark your calendar for 9/6 and 12/6)! They promise live music, art-making workshops, entertainment, and more. Tickets are usually $28 for adults and $18 for kids, but know that even on First Friday you'll have to pay for the Barbie special exhibit [(click here](https://11000a.blackbaudhosting.com/11000a/tickets?tab=2&txobjid=f527a98e-d8c5-43d9-8eb8-91de1e0eb9f8) to see the details).

![](https://dealsinaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/HEARD1.jpg)

Last but certainly not least on the list is the Heard Museum. This is a beautiful place with an incredible collection of American Indian Art and much more. For First Friday they promise quite a dance party ([click here](https://heard.org/event/june-first-friday/) for the details) and I called to confirm that admission is free for all on 6/7 from 5-9 PM. Admission is usually $22.50 for adults and $9 for kids.

Enjoy!
