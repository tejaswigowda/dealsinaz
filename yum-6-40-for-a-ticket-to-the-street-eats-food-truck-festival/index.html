YUM! $6.40 for a Ticket to the Street Eats Food Truck Festival (Usually $13.83)!

Jan 10, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you love food trucks, you won't want to miss the Street Eats Food Truck Festival coming to Salt River Fields at Talking Stick February 11th and 12th. Head over here and use code SALE3 to get a ticket for $6.40 (usually $13.83). Code SALE3 expires 1/11/17.

"Street Eats Food Truck Festival

  • The food: Dozens of food trucks gather for this weekend-long festival, doling out everything from burgers and barbecue to sweets and fried treats. Every food truck will have sample items for sale, and most will have full meals available, as well.
  • Other food-related activities: Some of the Valley’s top chefs will share tips during cooking demos, and eating contests will challenge brave participants to stuff themselves with hot dogs, saltine crackers, pies, and more.
  • Extras: In addition to all the food, the festival will also feature live music, a massive kids zone, and games."
