I just called the Desert Ridge office and confirmed you CAN buy gift cards with the $300 if there's nothing you need this second (Desert Ridge has an Albertsons grocery store, a Target, a Nordstrom Rack, a Kohl's, and lots of other shops and restaurants you probably visit all the time). I called Tempe Marketplace as well and they are getting lots of calls. They confirmed you can buy gift cards and recommend getting your tickets soon.

Note: I would call ahead to make sure tickets are still available before you make a special trip.
