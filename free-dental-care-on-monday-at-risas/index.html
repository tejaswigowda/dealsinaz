FREE Dental Care on Monday at Risas!

Aug 31, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you need dental work done but you're low on the money you need to do it, check this out. On September 4th Risas Dental locations (except Tempe) will be providing free dental care.

Check out the specifics:

"We are so excited to celebrate our 6th annual Labor of Love day! From 8am to 12pm at 16 of our 17 locations across Phoenix and Denver, we'll be offering free dental. This is completely free of charge and no insurance is necessary to be treated.

Bring the whole family as we'll have music, food, face painting, snow cones, bounce houses, & more at all participating locations. Be sure to get there early- patients in line before 8am are guaranteed treatment.

Chose one of the following procedures:

- Basic Cleaning

- 1 Filling

- 1 Extraction

- Exam & Xray

Please call (623) 900-7596 with any questions.

All participating locations:

Phoenix West** - 1630 N. 51st Ave.

*Tempe office will be closed due to renovations. Nearest locations would be Chandler or Mesa. "
