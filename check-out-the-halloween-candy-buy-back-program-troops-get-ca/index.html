Check Out the Halloween Candy Buy Back Program...Troops Get Candy, Kids Get Cash from Dentists!

2014-11-03 · Alicia Markham

Don't know what to do with all of the Halloween candy in your house? Think about participating in the Halloween candy buyback program. Head over here to find a participating dentist near you. Most offices have a specified time to come with candy. Kids will earn around $1/lb and the troops will get a sweet treat in the mail.

Participating dentists include:

J. Phillip Centers for Family and Cosmetic dentistry in Chandler. Bring in candy Monday, November 3rd from 9am-5pm and earn $1/lb. (up to 5 pounds).

Affiliated Pediatric and Orthodontics in Scottsdale. Bring in candy Monday, November 3rd or Tuesday, November 4th to the Scottsdale office or Tuesday, November 4th or Wednesday, November 5th to the Grayhawk office.

Feldman Orthodontics in Surprise. Bring in candy all week during normal office hours and kids will earn $1/lb.

And many others! Click here to see the full list.
