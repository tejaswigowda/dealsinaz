As Seen on Good Morning Arizona...Shopping Tips at Safeway!

May 13, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Thanks so much for tuning into Good Morning Arizona today where I'm sharing my shopping tips at all of the stores all week long. I hope you'll "like" Alicia's Deals on facebook where I'll help you "live life better for less" every single day. In case you missed Monday's Walmart segment you can check it out here.

Video

Today we're talking Safeway. Be sure to download the Just for U app to your phone via iTunes or Google Play (or log-in on your computer). The app ties directly to your Safeway Club Card and gives you discounts based on what you buy on a regular basis. So even if an item isn't on sale, it very well may be on sale for you. The savings are significant! On my app I had free Pillsbury cookies to add to my card, a gallon of milk for $1.99, a veggie tray for $4.50, and lots of other great deals that tie in with my shopping habits. You'll save around 20% (I've saved more than that at times) and you can add the deals while you're shopping (no planning ahead). It actually makes grocery shopping more fun.

Another app you won't want to miss is ibotta. Head over here to download it to your phone. This app allows you to save even more on things you’re already buying (on top of any sales or coupons or Just for U deals). It works at Safeway, Costco,Walmart, Target, Fry's, Walgreens, CVS, Dollar General, Sam's Club, Whole Foods, Sprouts, PetSmart, even AMC movie theaters. You add products to your app and learn about them. When you buy them, you take a picture of your receipt, and you'll get the rebate amount directly into your ibotta account. You can cash out once you hit at least $5 and send the money directly to your Paypal account or get Starbucks or iTunes gift cards. Examples of ibotta rebates at Safeway (each store has a list):

Earn $.50 when you buy a gallon of white milk (any brand) and take a poll and take a survey (takes seconds).

Earn $1.00 when you buy O Organics salad, learn a face, how to, and post on facebook

Earn $1.25 when you buy Banana Boat sunscreen, play trivia, and post on facebook

There are 88 possible rebates at Safeway and if there are items on your list, you can get back a bunch every shopping trip wherever you shop! Be sure to download the app!

Another tip to keep in mind when you shop is that ALL MANUFACTURERS' COUPONS are worth at least $1 at Safeway. You don't need to get the paper to enjoy the savings, you can head over here to find a huge assortment of printable coupons. You only print the coupons you'll use!

When you match coupons to sales, you'll do well. For example, Honey Nut Cheerios are on sale for $1.88 a box through Tuesday, May 13th. Use this coupon for $.50 off a box of Honey Nut Cheerios, it'll be worth $1. That means an $.88 box of Honey Nut Cheerios! It's pretty hard to beat that price.

Safeway also has fuel points. You can earn up to $1 off per gallon at Safeway, Chevron, and Texaco stations. Head over here for more details.

If you keep these tips in mind when you shop Safeway, you'll save a bunch! Enjoy!
