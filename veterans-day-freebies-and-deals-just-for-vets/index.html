Veterans Day Freebies and Deals Just for Vets!

2014-11-10 · Alicia Markham

Veterans Day is coming up Tuesday, November 11th. It's a great chance for all of us to thank the men and women who have served to protect our country.

Lots of valley businesses are thanking valley Vets with freebies. Please share this with all of the Veterans you know! All deals are good Tuesday, November 11th only and require ID. If you're new to Alicia's Deals, welcome! I would love to help you "live life better for less" every single day! I hope you’ll “like” Alicia’s Deals on facebook, follow me on Twitter, and join the Alicia’s Deals e-mail list.

For the first time ever, Cobblestone Auto Spa is offering veterans and active military a FREE express car wash AND oil change on Tuesday, November 11th. Head over here for more details.

For Vets who need a ride, Clean Air Cabs is offering FREE rides to Veterans with military ID or documents from the Department of Veterans Affairs. Call (480) 777-9777 to schedule a ride. This could be a great way to get out and enjoy some of these freebies!

Sassi in Scottsdale is offering Veterans and those currently serving a FREE three course meal Tuesday, November 11th from 5:30-9. The menu (you can check it out here) will be $39 for everyone else, and a 20% service charge will be added to checks (including for Veterans). Make your reservations NOW as they go fast! Call 480-502-9095 to make a reservation. Sassi is located at 10455 East Pinnacle Peak Parkway Scottsdale, AZ 85255.

Cantina Laredo is offering Veterans and active military a complimentary entree up to $15. Tax and gratuity are excluded.

All 8 valley Fired Pie locations will be offering a FREE pizza or salad to all Veterans with proper military ID.

Famous Dave's BBQ locations in Chandler at 3250 West Frye Road; Peoria at 16148 North 83rd Avenue, SanTan Village in Gilbert at 2206 E Williams Field Rd and Mesa Riverview in Mesa at 1011 Dobson Road will be offering veterans a FREE 2-meat salute from 11 AM-10 PM.

Applebee's will be offering Veterans and active military a free meal from this special menu from open-close with ID. Drinks and gratuity not included.

All Veterans and military personnel with ID can get 6 oz. of frozen yogurt FREE any time of day at Menchie's.

Uptown Alley in Surprise is giving Veterans a FREE game of bowling and shoe rental all day long with ID. They are located at 13525 N. Litchfield Road Surprise, AZ 85379.

Butterfly Wonderland is offering FREE admission to Veterans and active duty military with ID.

The Refuge, located at 4727 N. 7th Ave, is offering all active and retired military a free cup of coffee.

Veterans and active duty military will enjoy a free meal at California Pizza Kitchen from this special menu.

Veterans and Active Duty military members will get a free entree from a special menu at Olive Garden.

Denny's is offering Veterans and active duty military a free build your own Grand Slam from 5AM-noon. Vets should have breakfast, lunch, and dinner covered!

Red Robin is offering Vets and active duty military a free Tavern Double burger and bottomless steak fries.

Veterans and active duty military can stop into Starbucks for a FREE tall coffee.

After you've gotten your fill of food and drinks...there are some shopping deals too!

Veterans get 10% off groceries ALL WEEK LONG at Albertsons! Veterans, wear your Cardinals jersey on Sunday to get 10% off for that too! 20% off groceries is AWESOME!

Veterans and active military will get 20% off their ENTIRE PURCHASE November 8th, 9th, 10th, and 11th at Bed, Bath, and Beyond! Head over here for more information.

Enjoy the deals and please share!
