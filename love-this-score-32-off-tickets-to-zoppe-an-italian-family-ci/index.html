With authentic roots in the Old-World Italian tradition since 1842, this one-ring circus features astounding acrobatics, equestrian showmanship and of course, Nino The Clown. Held in the spectacular 500-seat tent on the grounds of the Chandler Center for the Arts, the Zoppé Circus invites modern families into a Felliniesque experience free of bothersome subtitles.
