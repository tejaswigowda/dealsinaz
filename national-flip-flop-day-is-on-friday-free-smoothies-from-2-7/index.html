National Flip Flop Day is on Friday! FREE Smoothies from 2-7 at Tropical Smoothie Cafe! Donations Benefit Camp Sunshine!

Jun 19, 2014 · Alicia Markham

What a great win-win! Head to your closest Tropical Smoothie Cafe wearing flip flops or sandals on Friday from 2-7PM (National Flip Flop Day) and get a FREE 24 oz. Jetty Punch Smoothie (strawberry/banana)!

You can head over here for more information.

They'll be accepting donations for Camp Sunshine, a wonderful retreat where children with life threatening illnesses can go with their families. You can find out more about the place here.

Maybe you can go after you spend time at Big Surf for the world's largest swim lesson (you'll get in for free at 7 AM and be able to stay as long as you want)!

Enjoy!
