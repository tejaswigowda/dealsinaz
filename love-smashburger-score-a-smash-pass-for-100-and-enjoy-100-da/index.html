Love Smashburger? Score a Smash Pass for $100 and Enjoy 100 Days of FREE Entrees November 5th-February 14th!

Sep 28, 2018 · Alicia

If you love Smashburger this could be a DEAL! Head over here to score a Smashpass for $100 and get 100 days of free entrees (no purchase necessary) November 5th-February 14th. This could save you $600 if you go everyday (you could make it a goal to save as much as possible). Salads are included as entrees! Check out the details: :What is the Smash Pass™?

With the Smash Pass™, you’ll be able to treat yourself to one entrée every day from November 5, 2018 to February 14, 2019, at participating locations. Add-ons, salad protein, sides, and beverages are not included.

What entrées are included with a Smash Pass™?

All signature beef burgers, chicken sandwiches, turkey burgers, black bean burgers, and salads. The base Craft Your Own sandwiches are included, but all premium add-ons will be charged at regular price. The base salads are included, but protein and premium add-ons will be charged at regular price.

What’s not included with a Smash Pass™?

Add-ons, salad proteins, sides, and beverages are not included with the Smash Pass™.

How much does the Smash Pass™ cost during the sale?

Just $100!

Is tax applicable?

Sales tax does apply to the online Smash Pass™ purchase. Sales tax will apply to the entrées covered by the Smash Pass™. Sales tax will be applied to any remaining balance (non-qualifying purchases such as extra entrees, sides, desserts, beverages etc.)

Am I able to get a refund on the Smash Pass™?

No. All sales are final. The Smash Pass™ is not a gift card. It is non-refundable and non-transferable. If a participating location permanently closes during the Smash Pass™ redemption period, a refund may be arranged.

How do I use the Smash Pass™?

By purchasing the Smash Pass™, you are also signing up to be a part of our SmashClub™ rewards program (which means more free/discounted food for you). Every day from November 5, 2018 to February 14, 2019, one Free Entrée coupon will be added to your SmashClub™ account. To redeem, you simply show the coupon on our Smashburger Rewards app or give the cashier your account email or phone number."
