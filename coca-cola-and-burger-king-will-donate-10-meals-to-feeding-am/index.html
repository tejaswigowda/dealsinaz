Coca Cola and Burger King Will Donate 10 Meals to Feeding America With Your Help (No Purchase Required)!

Dec 26, 2014 · Alicia Markham

What a great way to help a little bit! Head over here and find our more about the #ShareHappinessYourWay promotion from Burger King and Coca Cola!

Upload a picture of yourself Sharing Happiness at Burger King restaurants OR elsewhere (anywhere you choose) on Twitter OR Instagram with the hash tag #Sharehappinessyourway.

Here's the legal language:

"How to Participate: During the Program Period, log-in or create your Twitter or Instagram

account. Creating a Twitter or Instagram account is subject to their terms and conditions

(https://twitter.com/tos) or (http://instagram.com/legal/terms/). Tweet a message on Twitter (may

include an optional photo) or post a photo on Instagram (herein, "Instagram Photo") and include

the hashtag #ShareHappinessYourWay. Entries that follow these directions will be posted in an

online gallery at www.sharehappinessyourwaypromotion.com. Your account settings must be set

to "unprotected" and/or "public" in order for your Tweet or Instagram Photo to be viewable by

Sponsor and in order to count your post. For each Tweet or posted Instagram Photo, Sponsor

will donate $1 to the Charity (up to $25,000 total). For every dollar donated, the Charity's network

of food banks secures and distributes 10 meals to people facing hunger. The Charity is leading

the fight against hunger in America. Limit: You may Tweet one (1) time during the Program

Period and post one (1) Instagram Photo during the Program Period."

If feels great to help a little bit!

Enjoy!
