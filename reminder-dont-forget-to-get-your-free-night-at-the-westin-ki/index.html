Reminder: Don't Forget to Get Your FREE Night at the Westin Kierland!

2014-06-06 · Alicia Markham

UPDATE: I just spoke with the marketing manager at Scottsdale Quarter and I just want to reiterate that you must have at least one additional receipt from a Scottsdale Quarter store to get the deal (you can't just buy a gift card and that's it). Stop into a restaurant, buy that Mac you've been wanting, get a new swimsuit, even grab a cup of coffee and enjoy the beautiful shopping experience! I LOVE Scottsdale Quarter and shop and have lunch there all the time! The American Girl store is coming soon and they have H&M, the Apple Store, Bath and Body Works, Gap, and tons of other great places! You can use the gift card to make up the difference to $450.

This is SUPER HOT! Head over here for more information about an AMAZING promotion at Scottsdale Quarter! You can get a FREE night at the Westin Kierland (worth $149) when you spend $450 at two or more shops and/or restaurants at Scottsdale Quarter. They have all kinds of stores like Sephora, Lululemon, H&M, and tons of others. The promotion goes from June 2nd-June 15th.

Here’s what I did (I called to confirm that were eligible as part of the promotion and got the deal this afternoon):

I went to C3 and bought an iced tea (they have really delicious tea). I have lunch there all the time! I would have shopped more but I was running short on time that day!

Then I went to the Scottsdale Quarter management office with my tea receipt and bought a $450 Visa gift card. There was a $2 fee but I figured since I used my credit card, I’m getting more than that in cash back. I’ll use the gift card everywhere they accept Visa until it’s gone (I hate carrying cash and always use my card and pay it off at the end of the month, so it won’t change how I do things except for using another card until it’s gone). The tea and the gift card counted as buying things in two separate shops or restaurants, so I got the voucher for the FREE night at the Westin Kierland.

We made our reservation and are so excited! Using my Visa gift card has been working out great, I just recommend that you read the information that comes with it so you know how it works!

The Scottsdale Quarter management office is open Monday-Friday from 9-5 and is located next to Urban Outfitters.

Here are the terms and conditions of the free night:

Advance reservations are required. Offer does not include resort service charge (I called and found out the resort fee is $28.07). You don’t have to pay the room tax!

Offer is valid for one-night stay from June 2nd, 2014-August 29th, 2014. Offer is based on availability.

Black out dates: 6/14, 6/21, 6/28, 7/5, 7/12, and 8/2

Voucher must be received and stay booked and redeemed by same individual. Identification will be required at check-in.

Voucher has no cash value

Guests must cancel 48 hours prior to stay

Not valid with other offers or promotions.

Enjoy!
