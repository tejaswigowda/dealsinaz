The Dbacks WON Wednesday 8-7! Free Tacos, Free Jumbo Jack, and Half Price Papa Johns!

Jun 25, 2015 · Alicia Markham

The Dbacks beat the Colorado Rockies 8-7 last night with a 9th inning rally. Tonight (6/25) they'll have their 11th chance to get to .500 (they've lost all 10 of their last ten tries...definitely seems like a curse)! We are hopeful that they'll pull off a win and it'll all be uphill from there!

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. That means today (6/25) is free taco day!

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at Papajohns.com with promo code DBACKS50! That means today (6/25) is half off pizza day!

Anytime a Dback “jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their closest Jack in the Box for a FREE Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase! Since Paul Goldschmidt scored a home run yesterday, you can take advantage of this deal today (6/25).

Enjoy!
