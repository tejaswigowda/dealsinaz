Tips and Tricks and Valley Grocery Stores to Save You Cash!

2014-10-28 · Alicia Markham

Thanks for tuning into Your Life A to Z on 3TV and checking out my KTAR.com video. I hope you’ll “like” Alicia’s Deals on facebook, follow me on Twitter, and join the Alicia’s Deals e-mail list where I'll help you "live life better for less" every single day (and don't forget I put together a list of my favorite deals at valley grocery stores each Wednesday.

Items you won't want to miss:

Ibotta: Click here to sign up for this great app. You'll save on all kinds of things at valley grocery stores in addition to any sales or coupons you have. Money will go directly into your Ibotta account which you can transfer to your Paypal account (I've gotten over $800 back) or choose to get a gift card. Yesterday I got money back on the Halloween candy, cereal, and milk I bought. It is really easy once you get the hang of it. You can use Ibotta at Albertsons, Sprouts, Fry's, Safeway, Whole Foods, Safeway, Target, CVS Pharmacy, WinCo Foods, Walgreens, Costco, Sam's Club, 7-Eleven. It's not just good on groceries, you can also use it at Aeropostale, Loft, Soma, Sports Authority, Quiznos, Home Depot, Lowe's, Petco, Petsmart, Babies R Us, Toys R Us, and AMC Movie Theaters.

Sam's Club: Head over here to snag a Sams Club Plus Membership for just $45. It includes a $20 gift card, coupons for a rotisserie chicken, Artisan Fresh spinach artichoke dip, Daily Chef cookies, and a French baguette, and a complimentary spouse or other household member (all in all a $142.18 value). You get money back for buying with your plus membership and your membership must have lagged 6 or more months (this is the first item I've seen that doesn't require you to be 100% new)!

Onto the tips:

Albertsons: Wear your Cardinals jersey on Sunday or Cardinals game day to save 10% off your ENTIRE grocery purchase during the regular season. If you spend $100 a week on groceries, that's $10 a week!

Bashas': They will double your coupons up to $.50!

Fry's : They offer facebook coupons each Wednesday, usually for $2 off your $40 purchase or $3 off your $50 purchase. You download the coupons directly to your VIP card. They also offer a Fry's Friday freebie many Fridays. Head back here this Friday to get a FREE Greek yogurt (must download the coupon on Friday, you'll have 2 weeks to use it).

Safeway: Safeway will make all coupons worth at least $1. If you see a coupon you like worth $.10 off online or in the paper, it's still worth a buck at Safeway.

Use their Just4U app to save 20-30% off things you already buy. If you shop Safeway, you absolutely should be using this app (or you can use your computer).

Sprouts: The day you shop matters big time at Sprouts. Wednesdays are double ad days. You'll enjoy all of the savings from two weeks of ads.

Walmart: They still match ads and now they have a new Savings Catcher program. Click here to check it out. Scan your receipt (or type in your receipt number) and they'll check all of the ads in town to make sure you're getting the lowest prices on everything you buy. If you spent more on something than you would have somewhere else, you'll get a Walmart gift card for the difference.

Enjoy the savings!
