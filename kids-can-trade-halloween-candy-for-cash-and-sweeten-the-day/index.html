Kids Can Trade Halloween Candy for Cash (and Sweeten the Day of the Troops Overseas)!

Nov 2, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Is all that candy around the house driving you insane?! It is way too tempting to have around the house for me (and the kids definitely don't need all that sugar on their teeth)! Head over here to check out the Halloween Candy buy back program and find a location near you. Participating dentists and orthodontists will pay kids around $1/lb. for their candy (usualy up to 5 lbs.). I would DEFINITELY call ahead of time to confirm when they'll be accepting candy.

The best part? They send the candy to the troops overseas (a sweet remembrance of home)!

This is definitely a win/win.
