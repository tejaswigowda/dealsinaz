About Pangaea Land of the Dinosaurs

Pangaea Land of the Dinosaurs takes guests on a journey back to the Mesozoic Era through numerous interactive exhibits and educational stations. The indoor center features more than 50 life-sized animatronic dinosaurs, including the velociraptor, triceratops, and the iconic Tyrannosaurus rex, who roam and pose through lifelike recreations of prehistoric habitats. For some hands-on learning, the facility's interactive stations include fossil digs, sifting, and excavations, as well as the inner workings of a dinosaur with the Dinosaur Obstacle Course. Additionally, the Pangaea Dinosaur Grill serves dinosaur-themed entrees like Pangaea Pizza and Raptor Wraps, and the gift shop offers a variety of memorabilia like t-shirts and hats to commemorate the trip."
