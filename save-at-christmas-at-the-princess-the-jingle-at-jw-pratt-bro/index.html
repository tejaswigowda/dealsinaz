Christmas at the Princess: Head over here and use code TIME4FUN to get two GA wristbands for $39.78 (usually $86.44). Note that with these tickets, you will have to pay for parking, so you might want to get dropped off.

Jingle at the JW: Head over here and use code TIME4FUN to get two activity passes, including ice skating, snow tubing, ski simulator, and more for $42.49. Note: You can walk around for free.

Pratt Brothers Christmas at Rawhide: Head over here to get tickets for $24/each instead of $38.01. It looks like this one doesn't include the promo code or the guaranteed Santa experience (limited walk-up spots are available).

Phoenix Ballet's The Nutcracker: Head over here to get tickets for $29 instead of $72 (shows December 12, 13, and 14 at Arizona Financial Theater, promo code does not apply.

Zoolights: Head over here to get tickets for $35/each.
