It's a great time to buy a new car. The lots need to make room for the new models and may have excellent deals now.

My tips to getting the best deals:

Competition almost always helps you. Get multiple quotes from multiple dealers. They all want to beat each other and you can get quotes online without leaving your home. I always ask for an out the door price (including tax and all fees).

Keep in mind that there might be secret incentives on the car you want. Secret incentives can be $1000-$6000 in addition to known rebates. Invoice is usually not the price the dealer actually paid for your car.

Remember the power of walking out. If a negotiation isn't going the way you want, leave!

Head over here to check out edmunds.com. They'll have dealerships competing for your business (and the whole process can be a lot easier). Remember, I always ask for the out the door price! If you can pay cash (or get a loan somewhere else if there aren't great financing options) you'll know exactly what you're paying. Try to negotiate the trade in separately from the car price.
