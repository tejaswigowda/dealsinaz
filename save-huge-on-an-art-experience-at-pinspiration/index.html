Save Huge on an Art Experience at Pinspiration!

2015-09-09 · Alicia Markham

I have to start out by saying I've been wanting to try this place forever! A friend from my daughter's preschool, Brooke Roe has always been incredibly creative and talented. She had this idea for Pinspiration, a beautiful place where people get together to create, with lots of ideas coming from Pinterest! It's located on High Street (formerly City North) and they offer beer, wine, small bites, and crafts for all ages!

Head over here to save big on a package that you'll enjoy!

  • $69 for a Splatter Date for two ($99 value)- $55.20 with code GREAT20 (good through 9/10)
  • $37 for a Pinspiration Project experience and a glass of wine for one (up to $54 value)- $29.60 with code GREAT20
  • $69 for a Pinspiration Project experience and wine for two (up to $108 value- $55.20 with code GREAT20
  • $10 for a story time and craft/snack experience for one child at 10 a.m. Wednesdays ($16 value)- $8 with code GREAT20

The Splatter Date includes one large canvas or two small canvases with chocolates and champagne; view photos of this experience here. The Pinspiration Project experience includes one project and one drink for each person.

Enjoy!
