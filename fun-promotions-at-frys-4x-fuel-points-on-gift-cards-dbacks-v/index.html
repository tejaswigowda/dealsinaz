If you're a Fry's shopper you probably already know about some of the promotions they offer. Paper coupons are always worth at least $1 (at the moment at Phoenix area stores, they can change that policy), they offer a weekly facebook coupon (this week it's $2 off your $40 purchase), and they offer a Friday freebie (on September 26th head over here for a free bag of 3 Musketeers bites).

Head over here to load a new coupon for 4X fuel points when you buy gift cards. You can also download extra coupons like $5 off your Buca di Beppo gift card, $5 off your Cold Stone Creamery gift card, and special deals from Xbox live and Staples.

One fun program is the Dbacks Rewards. Head over here for more information and click "enroll now". You have until September 28th to collect points and until October 28th to redeem them for new merchandise, autograph memorabilia, and more. I just checked my account and I have 142 points...enough for a hat, ball, license plate frame, and more. I'm only 8 points away from other great prizes. Look for Dbacks rewards tags on items you're buying to collect points. They did send me a fun box with fuzzy Dbacks slippers, Shamrock coupons, Gatorade, and a few other things in exchange for blogging about this and I'm just thrilled to really know about the program. I would have had no idea I had a free Dbacks goody waiting for me! Thanks David!

Head over here to check out all of the promotions...if you're shopping there anyways, you might as well save!
