Thursday is FREE SALAD DAY at Mad Greens in Scottsdale and Tempe!

Jun 23, 2015 · Alicia Markham

How fun! This Thursday, June 25th is FREE SALAD DAY at Mad Greens in Scottsdale and Tempe!

Check out the details for the Scottsdale location:

"Do you love free food? Do you love supporting local charities? Then MAD Greens (www.madgreens.com), a new restaurant specializing in premium made-to-order salads, fresh-blended juices, paninis, wraps and soups, is inviting YOU to help us celebrate the opening of our FIRST Arizona restaurants! On June 25th, head to the new MAD Greens at N. Scottsdale Rd. and Acoma (14418 N. Scottsdale Rd., Suite 185, Scottsdale, Arizona 85254) to enjoy a FREE regular-sized salad & drink on us. How cool is that?!

You don’t have to bring anything but your appetite to participate, but all day we will be collecting cash contributions to benefit Boys & Girls Clubs of Greater Scottsdale – Thunderbirds Branch. "

Head over here for information about the Tempe location (located at 2000 E. Rio Salado Parkway, Suite 1215, Tempe, Arizona 85281). They'll be raising money for the Ladmo branch of the Boys and Girls Club!
