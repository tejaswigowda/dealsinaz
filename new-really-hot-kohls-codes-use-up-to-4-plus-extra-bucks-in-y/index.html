New, REALLY HOT Kohl's Codes (Use Up to 4)! Plus Extra Bucks in Your Inbox If You Get Kohl's E-mails!

Jul 27, 2014 · Alicia Markham

I LOVE Kohls and was so happy to see this little present in my Inbox today! If you get Kohl's e-mails too, check to see if you got $5, $10, or $15 off your $25 purchase (I got $10). You DON'T have to have a Kohl's charge for this one, the deal is good TODAY ONLY.

If you don't get Kohl's e-mails, click here to start getting them (you probably won't get the e-mail today but you will get future e-mails).

What's so amazing about Kohl's is that you can use UP TO FOUR promo codes TOGETHER on the same order (I recommend using a desktop or laptop computer to get the savings, mobiles seem to only let people use two codes).

Check out some of these codes you can use to save HUGE:

URSPECIAL: $10 off a $30 purchase (good for anyone)

POOLSIDE: 25% off your order (good for Kohl's cardholders)

SPENDNSAVE: 15% off your order (good for non-Kohl's cardholders)

JULY15SHIP: FREE SHIPPING (good for everyone)

HOMESALE10: $10 off your $50 home purchase (good for everyone)

LUGGAGE50: $50 off your $200 luggage purchase (good for everyone)

KIDS15: 15% off your kids purchase (good for everyone)

BRITAX30: $30 of your Britax car seat purchase (select models) (good for everyone)

SUMMERFUN20: 20% off select Women's and Junior's summer tops, bottoms, dresses, and swimwear (good for everyone)

I used my code they e-mailed me along with URSPECIAL, POOLSIDE, and JULY15SHIP and got my $37.98 order (including all sales) down to $13.48 SHIPPED. If you don't have a Kohl's charge, use code SPENDNSAVE instead of POOLSIDE and you'll do almost as well!

For me, since I can use the codes unlimited times (except for the one they e-mailed, that one can only be used once), it makes more sense to do multiple small orders (since shipping is free). Although you do get $10 Kohl's cash for every $50 you spend, I'd rather have the $10 right now! :-)

Enjoy!
