Is the Government Shut Down Affecting Your Paycheck? Freebies for Federal Employees Good Until It Ends (Hopefully Soon)!

2019-01-22 · Alicia

The government shut down is affecting all of us in different ways but the employees not getting paid are definitely feeling the pain. If you're one of the furloughed workers, check out these freebies just for you.

If you haven't been skiing in awhile, Arizona Snowbowl is offering federal workers a free lift ticket on Tuesdays until the shut down ends. Head over here for more information and bring your furlough letter and federal ID to the ticket window.

You can eat every day until the shut down ends at Romano's Macaroni Grill. "For every day the government shutdown is in effect, Macaroni Grill is offering all furloughed federal government employees a free Mom's Ricotta Meatballs + Spaghetti. All you have to do is show your federal ID.

(Valid through 1/23/19 or until the Government shutdown ends, with valid federal ID. Valid 11 a.m.-4 p.m. Not valid at airport or international locations.)" Head over here for more information.

Waldo's BBQ is offering free meals to federal employees and their families on Thursdays until the shut down ends. "Waldo’s BBQ and Catering would like to announce a special offer for all federal employees currently affected by the government shutdown. Effective immediately, all federal government employees and their families not receiving paychecks may receive free dine-in meals at Waldo’s BBQ, Mesa or Gilbert location, every Thursday from 5 to 8 PM until the shutdown is resolved. Simply show a federal government employment ID and you will receive a complimentary dinner including entrée, side and fountain drink or iced tea from our menu created especially for this difficult time. " Head over here for more information.

Barrio Cafe is offering federal employees a free meal and dessert. Bring along your Zero Neet federal goverment pay stub and matching ID. Head over here for more information.

Modern Market is offering federal employees a free grain bowl Fridays through Sundays with ID from 5 PM until close until the shut down ends. Head over here for more information.

RigaTony's is offering out of work government employees a free lunch or dinner and a soft drink, tea, or coffee every day until the shut down ends. Head over here for more information.

Please let me know what freebies you know of and I'll add them to the list.
