Only $20 for Spays in May! Vaccines Included Too!

Apr 14, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you have a female dog who needs to be spayed, there's a huge deal in May!

Check out what they sent over:

"Altered Tails Celebrates No Mother’s Day As Nina Mason Pulliam Charitable Trust Grant

Provides $20 Spay Surgeries For Female Dog Owners in May

Available to all Maricopa County female dog owners, the offer also includes rabies, DA2PP and Bordetella vaccinations.

PHOENIX (April 14, 2017) – This May, thanks to a grant from Nina Mason Pulliam Charitable Trust, Altered Tails, a 501(c)(3) organization and Arizona’s largest spay/neuter clinic specializing in high-quality, low-cost spay/neuter services, is offering a No Mother’s Day $20 spay surgery promotion for all female dog owners in Maricopa County. This special, $20 rate also includes rabies, DA2PP and Bordetella vaccinations.

“Spaying and neutering is one of the most effective ways to reduce the homeless pet population and is safe for puppies and kittens as young as 12 weeks of age, according to the American Veterinary Medical Association,” said Ellen Clark, executive director, Altered Tails. “Spaying a female dog before the first heat cycle can be beneficial, improving the health of the pet by reducing the risk of certain reproductive cancers and infections.”

“Our goal is to enlist the residents of Maricopa County to assist us in controlling the animal population. We also want to help keep healthy dogs in their homes by helping to vaccinate them while they are getting fixed.”

This special rate for spay surgeries is available to all female dog owners in Maricopa County simply by making an appointment. Female dog parents who wish to take advantage of this offer must mention the “No Mother’s Day” campaign when they schedule their appointment. This campaign is based on availability.

Please visit www.AlteredTails.org or call (602) 943-7729 (Phoenix), (480) 807-1200 (Mesa) for more information or to schedule an appointment.

About Altered Tails

Altered Tails is the only National Spay/Neuter Response Team trained clinic in Maricopa County. We provide high-quality, high-volume spay/neuter services. We have three locations, 950 West Hatcher Road, Phoenix, AZ 85021, 7246 East Main Street, Mesa, AZ 85207 and 225 East Valencia Road, Tucson, AZ 85706. In 2016 Altered Tails sterilized more than 23,000 cats and dogs throughout Maricopa and Pima Counties. "
