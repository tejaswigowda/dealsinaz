The Arizona Cardinals are BACK! They won in huge fashion yesterday (10/11) and that means lots of great deals and freebies today (10/12) for all of us!

Head to Sonic for half price drinks and slushes the day after every Cardinals win.

Love pizza? Head over here and use code CARDINALS50 to get 50% off your Papa Johns order the day after a Cardinals victory!
