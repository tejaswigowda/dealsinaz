One of the best parts of this place is the people. It's family run and operated and it's always a treat to stop in and say hello. They have China Mist iced tea (they'll put in the most delicious fruit if you'd like to make it incredible) and tons of other menu items you'll enjoy. Our whole family loves the place and we stop in as often as we can.

"When the family behind Piccolo Family Italian Ice moved to Scottsdale, they were disappointed they couldn't find a place with East Coast–style pizza and sweet italian ices. They spent years dreaming about their old favorite noshes, before eventually deciding just to open up their own "Friday night slice and ice" joint.

Piccolo Family Italian Ice now serves up New York–style pizza (available whole or by the slice) made with imported crust from Brooklyn and italian ices in such flavors as lemon, Swedish fish, and rainbow. It also treats visitors to other East Coast favorites, from Hebrew National hot dogs to old-fashioned penny candy."

Check out their website and facebook page, and please say hello to Robin and the rest of the family for me if you stop in! So nice to support small business in our community!
