Have You Been to Butterfly Wonderland? Save 34% off Tickets Along With a Souvenir Cup and Drink!

Nov 28, 2018 · Alicia

If you've never been to Butterfly Wonderland, here's a great way to go for less! Head over here to pick up adult tickets to the butterfly conservatory along with a souvenir cup and drink for $19, usually $28.45. Kids will pay $14 (usually $20.45).

"Butterfly Wonderland is the largest butterfly conservatory in America. It features an indoor rainforest where thousands of butterflies fly freely amongst custom botanical glass art pieces called Glass in the Garden. There are interactive exhibits, a Butterfly Emergence Gallery, 3D movie theater, Spineless Giants exhibit, Café, Gift Shop and a Rainforest Reptile exhibit. Butterfly Wonderland is open daily from 9am-5pm. Cameras are welcome."
