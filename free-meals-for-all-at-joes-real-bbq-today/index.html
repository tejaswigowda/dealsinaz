FREE Meals for All at Joe's Real BBQ Today!!!

May 2, 2018 · Alicia

Today (5/2) is customer appreciation day at Joe's Real BBQ in Gilbert and that means FREE meals for all! Head over here for more information on their facebook page.

"Wednesday, May 2nd from 11am-3pm and again from 4pm-8pm.

PLEASE consider bringing canned foods or cereals to donate to Open Arms Care Center!

Check out @funkfit while you’re here and during dinner @romenbuffalo will be playing. There will be balloon artists for the kids too."

Come ready to stand in line and have a great time! Could be fun to do with the kids who don't have school.

They are located at 301 N Gilbert Rd, Gilbert, AZ 85234.

Free meals will include a pulled pork or chicken breast sandwich plus coleslaw, beans, a chocolate chip cookie and a drink.

Enjoy!
