Macayo's is Celebrating 75 Years With $.75 Tacos Today Only (1/26)!

Jan 26, 2021 · Alicia#EXPIRED

This is definitely the best taco Tuesday deal of the week! Head to Macayo's for $.75 crispy tacos all day (1/26) to celebrate 75 years in business!

Check out their press release:

"September 26 marks the official 75th anniversary of Macayo’s Mexican Food, with seven locations around the valley. But why wait to celebrate until then? On the 26th of each month throughout 2021 leading up to the anniversary, the restaurant locations are offering delicious discounts, and more, in honor of this historic milestone.

“We’re honored and grateful to have been a part of families traditions and celebrations every day and on special occasions for 75 years,” says Nava Singam, owner of Kind Hospitality, which owns Macayo’s Mexican Food. “We aim to continue to be the place where people come to enjoy a traditional Mexican meal and a great experience and to provide the delicious food that people bring into their own homes for many more years to come.”

Macayo’s kicks off this milestone year with specials such as 75 cent crispy tacos on Tuesday, January 26, 2021, 75 cent cheese enchiladas on Friday, February 26 and 75 cent cheese crisps on Friday, March 26 (cents-ing a theme here?).

Throughout the diamond year celebration leading up to September 26, 2021, Macayo’s locations valley wide are also offering giveaways for VIP e-Club members, contests, new merchandise and ever-popular recipe cards, cooking classes, and more.

The actual anniversary date, Sunday, September 26, 2021, also coincides with National Chimichanga Day, which, as the originator of this beloved Mexican specialty, Macayo’s officially declared in 2020. All day long, specials and exciting events will take place. To join Macayo’s VIP e-club and for more information, visit www.macayo.com."
