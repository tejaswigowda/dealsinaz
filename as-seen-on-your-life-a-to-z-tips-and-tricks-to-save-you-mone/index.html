If you take a few moments each week checking out the ads (or reading my weekly best deals at the grocery stores post), you can save HUGE at Walmart. You can ad match and you don't have to physically have the ad with you!

Head over here to check out their ad match guarantee.

Here are a few highlights:

  • “We will match any local competitor’s advertised price.
  • We do not require customers to have the ad with them to honor a competitor’s ad. (this is HUGE…people think it’s really hard and they have to actually have the ads with them)
  • Items purchased must be identical to the ad (size, quantity, brand, flavor, color, etc.), for fresh produce and meat items, the price is offered in the same unit type (lb. for lb; each for each)”

If you’ve been reading Alicia’s Deals in AZ for awhile, you know how much I love Ibotta. This app allows you to save even more on things you’re already buying (on top of any sales or coupons). I have saved $643 total with this app…I just got $.25 back from milk! You just redeem the offer and upload a picture of your receipt…super easy. They are always adding new products that qualify for cash back.
