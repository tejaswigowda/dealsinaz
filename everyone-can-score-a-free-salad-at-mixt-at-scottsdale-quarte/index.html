Who says there's no such thing as a free lunch (or dinner)?! We can all head to Scottsdale Quarter Thursday, June 16th to check out the new restaurant Mixt. From 11 AM until 8 PM they're giving away free salads (up to $15 value). They are located across the street from Press Coffee, next door to Purple, and catty-corner to the Sorso Wine Room. Head over here to see their menu (and get your mouth watering) and head over here to check out the details of the deal:

"The secret’s out. This Thursday June 16, MIXT is dishing up free* salads, bowls, and sandwiches all day long at the Scottsdale Quarter. Swing by from 11 am – 8 pm for your favorite Do-It-Yourself, or try our Elote salad and experience summer in a bowl, on us.

In lieu of payment, we invite our guests to make a donation to our local non-profit partner, The Blue Watermelon Project, a grassroots group of chefs, restaurateurs, farmers and community food advocates that seek to rethink our relationship with food, starting with school lunches.

Limitations / Exceptions

*Offer valid on June 16, 2022 from 11am - 8 pm at MIXT Scottsdale only, while supplies last. Off­er limited to entrees (salad, bowl or sandwich) valued at $15 or less, per guest. If the entree price exceeds $15, the difference will be due. Drinks, alcoholic beverages, and treats are not included. O­ffer valid on in-person orders only; not valid on orders placed through the Mixt app, online, or through third parties such as DoorDash or UberEats. Offer subject to change or cancellation without notice."

Enjoy!
