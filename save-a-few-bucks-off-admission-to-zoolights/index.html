If you're excited to take the kids to Zoolights this year, head over here to get tickets through Groupon for $30 ($35 at the gate). Not only will you save $5/ticket you won't have to wait in the admission ticket line (just go straight to the turnstile). You can also buy them directly through the Phoenix Zoo for $30, but they tack on a $2 convenience fee. These are the least expensive tickets I've found (if you find them for less please let me know)!

  • "Valid days and hours: Event is open daily from 11/22/23 through 1/14/24, 5:30 p.m. to 10:30 p.m.
  • The Night Time Admission Ticket grants you access to the enchanting ZooLights event at Phoenix Zoo.
  • Immerse yourself in a winter wonderland as you stroll through the zoo adorned with millions of twinkling lights.
  • Marvel at the stunning light displays, including animated animal sculptures and sparkling trees.
  • Enjoy the festive atmosphere and take memorable photos with your loved ones.
  • Children 2 and under can join the fun for free, making it a perfect family outing."
