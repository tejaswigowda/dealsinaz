FREE Breakfast Thursdays at Participating Chick Fil A's in October!

Oct 9, 2014 · Alicia Markham

This is DELICIOUS! If you're driving around you still have an hour to get a FREE breakfast burrito at participating Arizona Chick Fil A's (Thursday, October 9th until 10AM).

Thursday, October 16th and Thursday, October 30th you can snag a free 3-count chicken mini (I'll be in line for these)!

Thursday, October 23rd you can score a free chicken biscuit!

Be sure you call your Chick Fil A before you make a special trip to make sure they are participating.
