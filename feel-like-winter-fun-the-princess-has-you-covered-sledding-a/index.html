Feel Like Winter Fun? The Princess Has You Covered...Sledding, Axe Throwing, and Tons of Other Activities Available for a Family of Four for $48!

It's winter and if you're in the mood for some cold-weather fun we usually don't get in the Phoenix area, the Scottsdale Princess has you covered! Head over here to get wristbands for up to four people including self-parking for one car on the night of your choice (you have to choose ahead of time) for $48 (usually $60). About This Deal

What’s included

  • Self-parking for one vehicle
  • General admission wristbands for up to four people (based on the number of vehicle occupants)
  • The Ice Pass available for additional $25 fee per person and includes unlimited one-hour sessions at The Rink and the Alpine Skate Trail.
  • All food and beverage at an additional cost

Winter at the Princess

This year, Fairmont Scottsdale invites guests for a brand new family-friendly event designed to help relax and unwind during Wintertime.

Attractions:

  • Ice skating at the 6000-sq. ft. rink
  • Ice Skating through the Alpine Skate Trail of more than 5,200 -sq ft. of real ice
  • The village campfires
  • Dining and games at The Lodge
  • Taking pictures with stunning views such as the glacier summit or the ski lift
  • Sled down the Snow Mountain
  • Axe throwing
  • Glazier Ice Maze
  • The Kids Village, good for ages 2–8
  • The Chalet Sun + Ski Shop
  • Mount Everest Climbing Walls
  • Chair Lift Ride
