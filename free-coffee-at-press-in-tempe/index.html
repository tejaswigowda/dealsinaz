FREE Coffee at Press in Tempe!

Jul 28, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Thanks to Alicia's Deals reader Angelica for letting us know about free cold brew and drip coffee at Press in Tempe (1221 East Apache Blvd.) through Saturday, 7/29. Head over here for more information.

"It’s about to get easier for East Tempe and light rail riders to get their daily cup of Press Coffee. Our newest cafe at Apache and Terrace opens on Thursday, July 27 and to celebrate we’ll be giving free small cups of drip and cold brew coffee for three days.

Press Coffee at the Nexa Apartments (1221 E. Apache Blvd) is our second Tempe location. The 1,300-square-foot space will serve espresso drinks, pour overs and cold brew made from our award-winning roasts. It will also feature our menu of breakfast and lunch items including our new specialty avocado toasts.

Stop in for a free cup of small drip or cold brew coffee on July 27, 28 and 29. Press Coffee at Nexa will be open Monday – Saturday 6:30 am – 7 pm and Sundays 7 am – 5 pm."
