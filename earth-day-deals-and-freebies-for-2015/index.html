Earth Day Deals and Freebies for 2015!

Apr 21, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Earth Day is Wednesday, April 22nd! There are some deals and freebies to help us celebrate our home planet (and think a little bit more about taking care of it)!

Head over here for more information about the Slash the Trash event in Tempe from 11-1! Highlights include free brats and chips courtesy of Gordon Biersch, goodies, music, and more. Thanks Trish!

Head to your closest participating Disney store to get a free reusable tote bag Wednesday with any purchase. I called the Fashion Square and Arrowhead locations and confirmed that both will have the free bags while supplies last.

If you have a 5-10 year old, head over here to check out the free crafts Anthropologie has going on this weekend in honor of Earth Day. You must RSVP to attend!

Why not have a great meal and support a great cause? Head to Flower Child in Arcadia and 100% of Earth Day proceeds will go to benefit three community partners (Arcadia High School's new garden, Arizona Audubon, and the Rotary Club of Arizona). Click here for more information. I am SO EXCITED for Flower Child to open their new Scottsdale location!

Earth Day is a great time to start thinking about buying organic. Sprouts has a huge Organic sale going on through Wednesday, April 22nd! You'll be able to take advantage of double ad day on Wednesday too!

Head to your closest participating Origins location to get a free Eco-Chic bag with purchase. I called the Biltmore location and they confirmed that they have bags!

Click here to get $10 off your $50 Living Social purchase with code EARTH10 in honor of Earth Day!

Enjoy the deals and I'll keep updating this list as I find more (please e-mail me, alicia@dealsinaz.com, if you know of a great Earth Day deal)!
