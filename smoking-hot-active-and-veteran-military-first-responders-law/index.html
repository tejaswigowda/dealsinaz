SMOKING HOT...Active and Veteran Military, First Responders, Law Enforcement Officers, and Educators Sail Can Enjoy a FREE 3-Day Margaritaville at Sea Bahamas Cruise!

2022-09-12 · Alicia

If you or someone you love is a current or former member of the military, a first responder, a police officer, or an educator you won't want to miss this deal from Margaritaville at Sea! Head over here for more information about their Heroes Sail Free program. Please share!

"For your dedication to serving our communities at home and abroad, Margaritaville at Sea invites all active and veteran military, first responders, law enforcement officers and educators to sail free on a much-deserved 3-day, 2-night cruise to Grand Bahama Island."

"Terms and Conditions: Offer is valid for new bookings on sailings from September 9, 2022, through December 29, 2023, and is not combinable with other promotional offers. This offer is available for current and former U.S. military, First Responders, Government employees and Teachers that are verified through the GovX ID platform. Members will receive a FREE fare on an inside or ocean view stateroom. Upgrades to suites available for an additional charge. Discounted fares apply ONLY to verified member(s). Offer applies to up to two qualifying members per stateroom. When booking two qualifying members, please call our Contact Center as this option is not available online. Additional guests traveling on the same reservation as the verified member(s) pays prevailing retail rates. The free fare for members excludes port taxes and fees. Onboard fees apply to all guests. Offer is also valid for single travelers. Singles are capacity controlled at the discretion of Classica Cruise Operator DBA Margaritaville at Sea. This offer is per booking and applies to a 2-night cruise only. One sailing per member, per year. Member must be present at the time of sailing. All components included in this offer are non-transferable and must be used at time of sailing. Capacity for this offer will be limited and will vary based on sailing date. Blackout dates are December 30, 2022, and December 31, 2023. Additional Blackout dates may be added at the discretion of Classica Cruise Operator DBA Margaritaville at Sea. Port taxes, fees, and port expenses are additional. Full payment must be made at time of booking. Subject to availability. Classica Cruise Operator DBA Margaritaville at Sea reserves the right to correct any errors or omissions and to change any and all fares, fees, surcharges and offers at any time. Void where prohibited by law. Ships’ registry: The Bahamas."

Enjoy!
