We should all take advantage of the fun around town, especially when it's free!

The Desert Botanical Garden is free the second Tuesday of each month from 8AM-8PM. Head over here for more information about the free admission opportunity!

First Friday is always lots of fun in downtown Phoenix and it's a wonderful way to enjoy culture FOR FREE. Visit the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program.Everyone will get into the museum for $0 from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids), and there will be lots of fun events for the whole family.

Still have energy? Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event. Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids).

If it’s date night and you’re leaving the kids at home on First Friday, head to Adults Night Out at the Arizona Science Center. Adults will get in free from 5:30-9 (tickets are usually $16.95) AND they’ll have an open bar and lots of fun!
