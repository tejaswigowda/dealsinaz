It's First Friday and That Means FREE Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix and The Phoenix Art Museum!

Nov 4, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Happy First Friday! Don't forget that you can get in FREE to the Children's Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM!

Head over here for more information.
