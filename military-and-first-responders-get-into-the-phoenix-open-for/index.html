Military and First Responders Get Into the Phoenix Open FOR FREE...Plus Free Tickets With Purchase at Safeway for All, and More!

2020-01-27 · Alicia

The Phoenix Open officially starts today! Everyone gets in FREE today and tomorrow (1/28) for Ford Free Days. Keep in mind that kids under 17 get in free with an adult.

If you know someone who has served in the military or as a first responder (EMT, firefighter, or police officer), let them know that they can head over here to get a FREE voucher for themselves and a guest.

"FREE TOURNAMENT ADMISSION FOR FIRST RESPONDERS AND U.S. MILITARY

The Waste Management Phoenix Open tournament hosts, The Thunderbirds, invite all First Responders, as well as all Active, Reserve, Veterans and Retired U.S. Military personnel, plus one accompanying individual, to be their guests (free of charge) at the 2020 Waste Management Phoenix Open. This marks the 19th year The Thunderbirds have extended this small gesture of thanks to the heroes who’ve given so much for our country.

TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THE FREE ADMISSION OFFER:

  • You must verify your status through Sheer ID.
  • Eligible personnel must present their official ID or badge along with ticket voucher at the main tournament entrance.
  • Business cards and pay stubs will not be accepted as forms of ID.
  • Tickets are limited to two per day, per each official ID.
  • Standard tournament spectator policies will be in enforced, including no firearms allowed."

"As a further compliment to Military personnel, the Birdies for the Brave Patriots Outpost hospitality tent will be available Wednesday through Sunday of tournament week. Located on the 18th hole near the tee box, all Active, Reserve, Veterans and Retired U.S. Military can invite one guest to enjoy complimentary food and beverages while watching PGA TOUR golf in a relaxed and comfortable setting.

To ensure that all our military heroes can experience the Birdies for the Brave Patriots Outpost, official military ID’s must be presented at the Outpost entrance and the one guest maximum will be strictly enforced."

If you stop by Safeway you can get a free ticket with purchase of $50 of participating alcohol and other products (look for signs).

Head over here for more information about how to get two free general admission passes from Desert Ridge Marketplace or here for information about how to get the freebies from Tempe Marketplace when you spend $300 in one day. I love to buy gift cards at Target or other stores I shop at with this promotion. You could even buy yourself a Visa gift card! It’s definitely a deal, Friday and Saturday Phoenix Open passes are $60 EACH (you’ll save $120)!

NOTE: They are running very low on tickets so I would definitely call ahead before you make a special trip.

I am most excited about the freebies for the brave men and women who have sacrificed serving our country!

Enjoy!
