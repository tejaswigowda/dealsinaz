In Honor of Giving Tuesday, Donations to DonorsChoose.org Will Receive a 50% Match Today! Fund Projects in Classrooms in Your Neighborhood!

Dec 3, 2019 · Alicia

This is pretty cool! In honor of Giving Tuesday, an anonymous supporter is matching 50% off all donations given at DonorsChoose.org today. Head over here to see all of the projects that need funding in classrooms near you. In addition to that, I just heard Sonic Drive Ins is giving $500,000!

Here's how it works:

  • "Find a classroom project that inspires you and give as little as $1. We're a public charity, so your donation is tax-deductible.
    • When a project is fully funded, we purchase all the requested items and ship them directly to the school.
    • Every donor gets a thank-you letter from the teacher, photos from the classroom, and a report of how each dollar was spent."
