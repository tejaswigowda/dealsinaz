About Christmas at Schnepf Farms

Christmas at Schnepf Farms "A World of Illumination" is a magical event that welcomes entire families and invites them to celebrate Christmas together. It features plenty of holiday attractions so everyone has a chance to find something they'll like. Kids and adults can choose from the Illumination Express train ride and a Christmas carousel to cookie decorating with Mrs. Claus and snow globe mini golf. They get an opportunity to spend quality time together, have the children meet Santa himself, and catch some holiday thrills.

  • Illumination Express: Visitors journey through breathtaking Christmas light displays on a train ride presented by World of Illumination.
  • Activities galore: They meet Rudolph on a hayride, test their skills at Snow Globe Mini Golf or Santa's Obstacle Course, go ice skating, and hop aboard the historic 1955 Christmas Carousel.
