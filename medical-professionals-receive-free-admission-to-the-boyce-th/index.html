Medical Professionals Receive FREE Admission to the Boyce Thompson Arboretum Through This Summer!

May 11, 2020 · Alicia#Free

Thanks to Alicia's Deals reader Amy for letting me know that the Boyce Thompson Arboretum is offering medical professionals FREE ADMISSION with credentials through this summer (2020). They want to thank these brave men for their bravery and hard work..

Admission is usually $15 for adults and $5 for kids 5-12.

The Arboretum is located an hour outside of Phoenix.
