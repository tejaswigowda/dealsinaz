Near Fountain Hills? FREE Papa Murphy's Pepperoni Pizza to the First 100 in Line for Grand Opening! Deals All Day Saturday, January 17th!

Jan 16, 2015 · Alicia Markham

If you love Papa Murphy's, you may want to stop by their newest location in Fountain Hills Saturday morning (January 17th)! Starting at 10 AM the first 100 people in line will receive a free pepperoni pizza! For the rest of the day, pepperoni pizzas will be on sale for just $4!

Even if you're not near Fountain Hills, participating Papa Murphy's around the valley will offering 20% off all day (I would call ahead to make sure that your location in honoring the deal).

"Papa Murphy’s, the country’s largest take ‘n’ bake pizza chain, has opened a brand new store in Fountain Hills at 13525 North Fountain Hills Blvd. The Fountain Hills store is open Sunday-Thursday from 10:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 10:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m. Owned by David and Spencer Perry, the newest Arizona Papa Murphy’s has received a warm welcome from the local community.

“As soon as we found our location in Fountain Hills we knew it would be perfect fit for a Papa Murphy’s--call it a gut feeling. We are delighted with the response and welcome we’ve received from our neighbors and we look forward to serving them for years to come,” said David Perry.

On Saturday, Jan. 17, the first 100 customers in line at the new location will receive a free large signature pepperoni pizza. This promotion starts at 10:00 a.m. and will last until 100 people have received their free pie. For the rest of the day on Jan. 17, large signature pepperoni pizzas will cost $4 each.

Additionally the new location will offer customers pizza tasting, games, prizes and other kid-friendly activities throughout the day on Jan. 17.

Customers can text AZ095 to 90421 for a chance to win pizza for a year as part of the grand opening festivities.* A winner will be drawn on Monday, Jan. 19 and the winner will be notified by the store.

The Perrys and their team are committed to creating excellent pizzas for every guest. Papa Murphy’s dough is scratch-made with love daily using high protein wheat flour. Each pizzais handmade to order and features fresh-grated 100 percent whole-milk mozzarella, cheddar, and provolone cheeses. We use butcher-quality meat toppings, with no fillers, which include crispy bacon, Canadian bacon, salami, pepperoni, Italian sausage, ground beef and grilled chicken. Other sliced-by-hand toppings include Roma tomatoes, mushrooms, onions, green peppers, and zucchini. Pizza lovers can also add unique toppings like spinach, sun-dried tomatoes, and artichokes.

With over 1,400 locations, Papa Murphy's is widely acknowledged for bringing families together through fresh, high-quality, convenient food they love.

The Fountain Hills location can be reached at 480-837-4669."
