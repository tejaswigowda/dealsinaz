Today Only: Free Admission to the State Fair Until 6 PM With Food Donation and Three Free Rides for Reading (Kids 5-14)!

2019-10-23 · Alicia

The State Fair is happening through Sunday only and today is our last shot to get in free! Every Wednesday we can all get free admission from noon-6 PM when we bring in six cans of non perishable food for St. Mary’s Food Bank (note that’s 6 cans per person). If you can’t go then, head over here to find discount tickets. You’ll save almost half off what you’d pay at the gate and you won’t have to stand in line. If you have kids 5-14 make sure you have them participate in the Read and Ride Program! Fill out a sheet you’ll find here with three books your child has read and they’ll get three FREE carnival rides!

My daughter is dancing there today too...extra bonus!

Enjoy!
