Someburros is Turning 30! Deals All Week to Celebrate!

Oct 24, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Someburros (a fantastic local Mexican restaurant with locations all over town) is turning 30! Head over here to check out the deals you can score all week to celebrate. Today (10/24) you can get a pollo fundido for $6 (I love to get this with no cream cheese or cheese but substitute guacamole with lots of hot sauce). These are delicious! Tuesday pick up $2 tacos, Wednesday get margaritas for $1, Thursday get $2.50 bean burritos, Friday get a free churro with combo or specialty purchase, and Saturday enjoy free dessert, giveaways, pinatas, and more!

There are lots of locations around town (and one in Flagstaff)!
