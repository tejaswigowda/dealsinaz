FREE GAS on Wednesday With GasBuddy App!

Nov 1, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Who wouldn't like a free tank of gas?! Head over here for more information about the GasBuddy "Tanks" giving event! Tomorrow (11/2) from 9-11 they'll be giving away free gas at a yet undisclosed location to everyone with the GasBuddy app.

Corner Store and GasBuddy have come together to launch ‘Tanks’giving, where brand representatives from both companies will surprise drivers who have the free GasBuddy app downloaded and a GasBuddy profile created with upwards of $20 worth of fuel per vehicle at select Corner Store locations. The first ‘Tanks’giving event will be in Phoenix, Ariz. on November 2, with Denver, Colo. to follow.

“Millions of Americans are making plans this month to visit friends and family,” said Walt Doyle, CEO of GasBuddy. “Corner Store and GasBuddy share a commitment to helping people on the road, whether it is fueling up the car or getting a good cup of coffee. This is why we are excited to work together and make this travel season as easy and enjoyable as possible.”

#Tanksgiving Rules:

• Must have the GasBuddy app downloaded on your phone and be a GasBuddy member with a profile created

• Up to $20 worth of free gas per vehicle. We have a budget and really want to spread the love as much as possible

• No external containers like buckets, jars, tubs, etc. Just straight up gas into your car

• First-come, first-served. While supplies last!
