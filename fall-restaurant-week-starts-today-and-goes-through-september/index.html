Fall Restaurant Week Starts TODAY and Goes Through September 28th!

Sep 19, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Arizona Restaurant Week starts TODAY and goes all the way through September 28th. It's a great chance to make reservations for a restaurant you might not visit regularly!

Head over here to check out the participating restaurants. Prices are $33 or $44 and if you see two forks, that means the price is for two people. Some of the really great deals are the places that are giving three-course meals INCLUDING drinks for two for $44! Places that fall into that category are The Parlor, Z Tejas, The Dhaba, Rock Lobster, Isabel's Amor, and Fuego Cityscape.

The places I would be most excited to try are Donovan's Steak and Chop House (it's our favorite), Lon's at the Hermosa (so beautiful), and Binkley's.

Enjoy!
