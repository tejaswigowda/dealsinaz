This Friday is a GREAT day for free fun!

If the kids are bored I have ideas that won't break the bank!

Click here to pick up discounted passes to AZ Airtime. This trampoline place lets kids use up LOTS of energy (and it's indoors)! You'll pay $19 for two kids for two hours including foam pit passes (usually $44).

Head to Desert Ridge Marketplace or Tempe Marketplace. If you collect $150 of same day receipts, you'll earn two all-day passes to Dave and Busters (worth $70). Pick up your passes at the management office. Fill the cars up with gas, go to the grocery store at Desert Ridge, buy a gift card, fill up the wine fridge, go to Target, it's really easy to spend $150 in a day on things you're buying anyways!

If you're heading up north, the family will enjoy this deal. Click here to get a scenic chairlift ride at Snowbowl for a huge discount. You'll pay $17 for two (usually $30) or $32 for four (usually $60).

We love our POGO passes (and they're perfect for summer fun)! Head over here to pick one up for just $39.98 with promo code ALICIA, usually $99.95 and worth $1500 if you go everywhere! You'll enjoy admission to Sunsplash, the Sea Life Aquarium, the Phoenix Zoo, JumpStreet, the Phoenix Rock Gym, Dbacks games, the list goes on and on. Even better, you can head over here and win a pair (I'm picking a winner on Friday).

I am loving the summer reading programs! Head to your local library and get the kids (and yourself) signed up! The Scottsdale Library has prizes from Chipotle, Benihana, Chick Fil A, and more! Encourage reading and win prizes...definitely a win/win!
