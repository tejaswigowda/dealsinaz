Half Off a Family Membership to the Desert Botanical Garden!

Aug 20, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Head over here to snag a family membership to the Desert Botanical Garden for $45 (usually $90). Beautiful weather will be here before we know it and this deal will let you enjoy the beautiful garden as often as you like for a full year.

Here's what's included:

  • "Unlimited admission to the gardens, seasonal butterfly exhibit, and flashlight tours for a family of two adults and children or grandchildren under age 18, including special members-only hours
  • Two guest passes
  • Subscription to the Sonoran Quarterly
  • Discounts on concerts, workshops, gifts, plants, and guest admission
  • See full membership benefits here"

You'll also enjoy reciprocal admission to over 300 gardens, arboretums, and conservatories across the country (including one in Flagstaff and one in Tucson).

Just to give you an idea, normal admission for adults is $22 and for children is $10. This membership will more than pay for itself the FIRST TIME you use it!
