Have You Gotten Your POGO Passes Yet? Still $39.98 With Code ALICIA! We've Having "Camp Markham" This Summer!

May 31, 2014 · Alicia Markham

If you haven't yet, you can still head over here to get POGO Passes for just $39.98 each (usually $99.95) with promo code ALICIA. We got three and the kids and I are having "Camp Markham" for at least a week this summer! We can't wait to visit all of the places! Remember that the passes are good for a FULL YEAR (and have a full value of $1500 each if you go everywhere)!

Here's a sampling of where you'll get to visit:

Sunsplash: Each pass holder can attend Golfland Sunsplash one (1) time. Your pass is good for one (1) SunSplash General admission ticket. Simply present your Pogo pass at Sunsplash Guest Service window to be scanned to obtain waterpark wristband. Regular price to get into Sunsplash is $29.99 so this almost pays for your pass! The park is open to passholders now!

Sea Life Aquarium: Each Pass holder can attend Arizona Sea life Aquarium two (2) times. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front desk to gain admission to the Aquarium.

Phoenix Zoo: Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to the Phoenix Zoo two (2) times. To gain admission to the Zoo, simply present your Pogo Pass at the admission window. Some experiences require an additional fee. Admission to ZooLights and select special events is not included.

Stratum Laser Tag: Pass holders can attend stratum two (2) times. Each pass holder receives one (1) complimentary game of laser tag each time they attend. Pass is valid for 12 months.

Arizona Diamondbacks: One (1) ticket per game per pass holder for two (2) home games at Chase Field for the MLB Regular Season. Check out theselect dates here.

Phoenix Jump Street: Each Pass holder can attend Phoenix Jump Street two (2) times. Pass is good for a one (1) hour jump session each attendance. Pass is valid for twelve (12) months. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front counter to gain admission to Phoenix Jump Street. Limited capacity, first come first serve.

And LOTS LOTS more! Rattlers games, Brunswick bowling, Phoenix Rock Gym, Enchanted Island, Comedy Theater, Skateland, and Tempe Paintball are all included! Head over here to see everywhere the pass is taken. Don’t forget to use promo code ALICIA to get your passes for $39.98 each (everyone over two will need their own pass).
