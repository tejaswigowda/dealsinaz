Give Blood 1/24-1/31, Get a FREE Dozen Doughnuts from Krispy Kreme!

Jan 24, 2022 · Alicia#EXPIRED

Our country has a huge shortage of blood right now and Krispy Kreme wants to help make it more delicious to donate! Give blood 1/24-1/31 and bring your proof of donation to the participating Krispy Kreme closest to you to get a FREE dozen original glazed doughnuts. Head over here for more information or see below:

"With the nation facing its worst blood shortage in over a decade, Krispy Kreme wanted to find a way to show our support for those willing to roll up a sleeve and give blood during January, National Blood Donor Month. For one week, 1/24 – 1/31, anyone who donates blood or platelets will receive a free Original Glazed® dozen. It’s our way of helping the American Red Cross increase awareness of this urgent need and saying “thanks” to those who step up to help their community.

Here’s how it works: Give blood any time between 1/24 – 1/31, then stop by your local Krispy Kreme for your free Original Glazed® dozen. Just show your proof of donation and date via your donor band or sticker, or flash your donor app.

FAQS

WHERE CAN I GIVE BLOOD?

Click Here to find your closest American Red Cross donation site and book your appointment online!

WHO CAN GIVE BLOOD?

Click Here to access the American Red Cross site to see if you are able to give blood.

WHAT IF I CAN'T BOOK AN APPOINTMENT BETWEEN 1/24 – 1/31?

Appointment availability is updated regularly; continue to check the site for openings by Clicking Here. We can only give a free dozen to those who give blood between 1/24 – 1/31, but we encourage you to donate when you can to help combat the national blood shortage.

DO BLOOD DONATIONS TO ORGANIZATIONS OTHER THAN THE AMERICAN RED CROSS QUALIFY FOR A FREE DOZEN?

Yes! Just show your proof of donation and date via your donor band or sticker, or flash your donor app.

WHEN CAN I REDEEM MY FREE DOZEN?

The free Original Glazed® dozen with proof of blood donation must be redeemed between 1/24 – 1/31. Qualifying blood donation must also be completed between 1/24 – 1/31.

WHAT IS THE LIMIT FOR THE BLOOD DONATION OFFER?

Each individual guest who qualifies is limited to one (1) Original Glazed® dozen per blood donation, no purchased required.

WHERE CAN I REDEEM THIS OFFER?

This offer can be redeemed while in a Krispy Kreme shop or drive-thru. This offer is not available through online ordering or at grocery or convenience stores.

IS THIS OFFERED AT ALL LOCATIONS?

Offer is valid at participating US locations only."

Please donate if you can and enjoy the doughnuts!
