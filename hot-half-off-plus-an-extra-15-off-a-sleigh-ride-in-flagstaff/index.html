HOT! HALF OFF Plus an Extra $15 Off a Sleigh Ride in Flagstaff!

Nov 5, 2014 · Alicia Markham

What a FUN memory this would be! Head over here to get a sleigh ride for four at Hitchin Post Stables in Flagstaff for just $90, usually $180. Use promo code ALICIA15 to get the price down to $75! That's $18.75 per person for a trip that's usually $45 each. How fun would it be to sing Jingle Bells on a real sleigh ride?

"Sleigh bells jingle pleasantly while the rhythmic trot of horses takes you through the Coconino National Forest. After a 30-minute ride, you'll stop at the cowboy campfire for a cup of hot cocoa or coffee, (cowboy meals and smores kits are optional). After stretching your legs, load up for the return trip. Just long enough to enjoy the outdoors without getting restless (or freezing)! Hitchin' Post Stables has been open since 2012, guiding guests on horseback trails through the 10,000 acres of the Coconino National Forest while discovering the home of cliff and cave dwelling Indians."

One note: I would try to go when there's snow on the ground. If there isn't enough, they'll change to a wagon ride (which is still fun, but not technically a "sleigh")..
