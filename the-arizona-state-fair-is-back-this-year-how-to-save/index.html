The Arizona State Fair is BACK This Year...Three Ways to Save!

2021-10-06 · Alicia

If you missed the Arizona State Fair last year you'll be happy to know you can enjoy it again this year! They are open Wednesday-Sunday through October 30th at the Arizona State Fairgrounds (1826 West McDowell Road). They aren't having big concerts this year, but there will be plenty of fried everything. If you want to enjoy the fun and avoid paying a ton, check out these deals:

We Care Wednesday is happening October 6th, 13th, 20th, and 27th. You can enjoy free admission (usually $10 for adults) when you bring in 10 donation items. Head over here for more information.

Everyone 55 and better can enjoy up to two free tickets on October 13th. Head over here for more information, you must head over here to request your tickets with the special password 21FAIRFUN.

If you have kids 5-14, make sure you head over here to print out the Read and Ride report form. If they read 3 books, they'll get 3 free rides. You'll bring the completed form to the Guest Services booth, I've done this with my daughter the last several years and it has been great!

Let me know if you hear of other ways to save!

Enjoy!
