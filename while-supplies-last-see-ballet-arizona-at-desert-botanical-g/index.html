While Supplies Last...See Ballet Arizona at Desert Botanical Garden for Just the Service Fee!

Jun 2, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you'd love to see Ballet Arizona perform Topia at Desert Botanical Garden, head over here to check out Goldstar. They have a very limited number of comp tickets available (you'll still pay the service fee).

"Expect the unexpected as Ballet Arizona's artistic director Ib Andersen continues his vision for dance in the desert. With Topia, the ongoing outdoor ballet series -- set in the Desert Botanical Garden -- continues to evolve, take chances and challenge emotional boundaries. Topia is a sensory experience unlike any other, filled with movement, sound and multimedia presentations, all embracing Phoenix's desert as a stunning backdrop. You'll see dancers chassé amidst the succulents and celebrate the beauty of the Southwest as the sun sets."

This sounds amazing! Enjoy!
