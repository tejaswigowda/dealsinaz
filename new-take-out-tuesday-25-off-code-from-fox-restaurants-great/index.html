New Take Out Tuesday 25% Off Code From Fox Restaurants! Great Time to Try Fly Bye To Go If You Haven't Yet...I Loved It!

A huge thank you to Alicia's Deals reader Angelica for sharing this goodie with us. If you're not in the mood to cook today (Tuesday, January 5th), head over here to order your choice of Fox Restaurants food online (you must create an account) and use code TUESDAY25 to save 25% off curbside take out today only (and possibly Tuesdays in the future, I would definitely try it).

This deal is good at Culinary Dropout, Blanco, The Henry, Dough Bird, Fly Bye To Go, Zinburger, The Arrogant Butcher, Olive & Ivy, The Greene House, and Wildflower (it is NOT good at Flower Child).

You can pay with a Fox Restaurant Concepts gift card (and last time I was at Costco I saw $100 worth for $79.99). Savings on top of savings are the best kind!

We tried Fly Bye To Go last week and the pizza was DELICIOUS! I loved the Copper Hat with light goat cheese.
