Buy One Holiday Drink Get One Free at Starbucks November 10-14 from 2-5!

Nov 9, 2016 · Alicia Markham

The holiday drinks are BACK at Starbucks! Here's something we can all celebrate, November 10th-14th get one free when you buy one from 2-5 PM.

Check out the details:

"To celebrate the return of Starbucks red holiday cups, customers who buy one holiday beverage and can get another one free* to share at participating Starbucks® stores in the U.S. and Canada November 10-14 from 2-5 p.m. Eligible holiday beverages include: Chestnut Praline Latte, Peppermint Mocha and Skinny Peppermint Mocha, Caramel Brulée Latte, Gingerbread Latte, Eggnog Latte, Teavana Joy Brewed Tea, and Spiced Sweet Cream Nariño 70 Cold Brew. Holiday Spice Flat White will also be eligible once it arrives in stores November 10."

Thanks Bargain Believer!
