SUPER HOT! FREE Ticket to Disney on Ice Let's Celebrate With Toy Donation on Saturday!

2014-12-04 · Alicia Markham

What a FUN give and get deal! Head to CityScape Saturday between 10 AM and 2 PM with a toy to donate and you'll get a FREE voucher to see Disney on Ice Let's Celebrate! I had no idea that Arizona leads the nation in the number of children per capital in group or shelter care. :-( Let's make sure everyone has a happy holiday season! Here's the information they sent me: "Disney On Ice presents Let’s Celebrate! – Presented by Stonyfield YoKids Organic Yogurt will celebrate the holiday season by bringing toys to deserving children from A New Leaf fostering program. Anyone donating a new/unused toy at CityScape Phoenix starting December 6th will receive one complimentary voucher from Feld Entertainment to see Disney On Ice presents Let’s Celebrate! when the show plays the US Airways Center between January 7–11, 2015 (ticket voucher will be good for select shows; while supplies last). The State of Arizona currently leads the nation in the number of children per capita in group or shelter care. Disney On Ice is happy to partner with CityScape Phoenix on this special toy drive to help ensure every child gets to celebrate this holiday season. WHERE: CitySkate Ice Rink at CityScape Phoenix Corner of Central Ave & Jefferson St. www.cityskatephx.com WHEN: Saturday, December 6, 2014 Donations accepted between 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM NOTE: Anyone that donates a toy after this Saturday event at CityScape will receive a 20% off coupon for tickets to Disney On Ice. CityScape will be open to the public for skating at 4 p.m.

Disney On Ice presents Let’s Celebrate!–Presented by YoKids will perform at the US Airways Center from January 7–11, 2015. Tickets are available at

Ticketmaster.com or by calling 800-745-3000."
