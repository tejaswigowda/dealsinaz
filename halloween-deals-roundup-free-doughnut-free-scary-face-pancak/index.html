Halloween Deals Roundup...Free Doughnut, Free Scary Face Pancake, $4 Boorito, and $1.50 Ice Cream Cone!

Oct 31, 2018 · Alicia

Happy happy Halloween! Check out some sweet treats you can enjoy around town!

At Krispy Kreme, wear your costume for a totally FREE doughnut all day long. Head over here for more information.

Take the kids to IHOP where they’ll get a FREE Scary Face pancake from 7 AM until 10 PM. Check out the details on their event page.

Check out the fine print:

“Come get ghoulish with us. Just rock your Halloween costume to Chipotle from 3pm to close on 10/31 to get a $4 burrito, bowl, salad, or order of tacos, or use code BOORITO in our app or online to get yours. Let’s boo this thing."

And my personal favorite...ice cream! Since Halloween happens to fall on the 31st of the month, we can stop into our closest Baskin Robbins location for $1.50 ice cream cones all day! Head over here for more information.

Enjoy and wishing you tons of treats!
