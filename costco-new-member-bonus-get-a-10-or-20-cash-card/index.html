Costco New Member Bonus...Get a $10 or $20 Cash Card!

Jan 24, 2020 · Alicia

If you still don't have a Costco card, head over here to buy a membership and get a bonus! The Gold Star Membership is $60 and they'll send you a $10 cash card. The Executive Membership is $120 and you'll get a $20 cash card.

I LOVE Costco if only for the gas deals (I have to put premium in my Volvo and it's SO MUCH LESS EXPENSIVE than anywhere else). It's so nice to get a bonus for joining!

"A Costco membership includes one additional Household Card for anyone over the age of 18 and living at the same address. A Costco membership is valid for one year at all Costco locations worldwide and online at Costco.com.

Join as a Gold Star Executive Member and receive an annual 2% Reward (up to $1,000)* on eligible Costco and Costco.com purchases, plus additional benefits on select Costco Services."

There are three steps to this deal:

1. Purchase your Costco Membership Activation Certificate here.

2. Receive your Costco Membership Activation Certificate by email.

3. Bring your Costco Membership Activation Certificate to any U.S. or Puerto Rico Costco location to join as a new member.

After you activate your membership they will mail you your cash card in 4-6 weeks.
