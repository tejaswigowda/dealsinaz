Smoking Hot! Skiing at Snowbowl for $29!

Mar 9, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you'd like to go skiing this spring break, this is an outstanding deal! Head over here for more information about the offer at Snowbowl in Flagstaff perfect for a day trip!

"New for Spring Break 2017, Arizona Snowbowl is offering a 74% discount with "The World's Best $29 Spring Break" package -and as always, guests 7 and younger with a paying adult get a free lift ticket.

$29 Ski & Snowboard Includes

· All-day adult lift ticket for unlimited skiing/snowboarding

· Gift card with $15 value that may be used on food, bar or retail purchases only on the same day for which it was purchased. All gift cards expire on the day of use. This $29 package can have a value of up to $90 dollars. Online, advance purchase required.

$29 Winter Scenic Chairlift Ride Includes

· All-day lift ticket for unlimited round-trips on Snowbowl's new, first-ever Winter Scenic Chairlift Ride on the new Grand Canyon Express high-speed chairlift (image attached)

· Gift card with $15 value that may be used on food, bar or retail purchases only on the same day for which it was purchased. All gift cards expire on the day of use. Each ride is roundtrip and offers exquisite panoramic views that include the Grand Canyon (no unloading at the summit). Online, advance purchase required.

$10 Equipment Rentals

· At time of purchase, after the $29 Spring Break package is put in the online "shopping cart," equipment rentals can be added for $10

$10 Two-Hour Group Lesson

· At time of purchase, after the $29 Spring Break package is put in the online "shopping cart," two-hour group lesson can be added for $10.

NOTE: Both versions of this $29 package are available for guests ages 8 and older from March 4-26, 2017 and must be purchased online 48 hours in advance of the date it will be used. Tickets, rentals and lessons are valid only for the date purchased. These specials cannot be transferred nor combined with other specials and discounts.

Expanded Operating Hours:

· March 3-26: the new, high-speed Grand Canyon Express chairlift is now open until 5:00 p.m. -the same for the Hart Prairie chairlift and the conveyors, which serve beginners. The other chairlifts close at 4 p.m. All lifts open at 9:00 a.m. "
