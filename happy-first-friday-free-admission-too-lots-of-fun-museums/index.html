Happy First Friday! FREE Admission to Lots of Fun Museums!

2016-05-06 · Alicia Markham

Hooray! Today (5/6) is First Friday! If you don’t have plans, you may want to head to Downtown Phoenix to enjoy some FREE museums! Anyone that says Phoenix has no culture has obviously never been to First Friday!

Head to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix where everyone will get in free from 5-9 PM. Tickets are usually $11 each, so this is quite an opportunity to play for less!

Still have energy? Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event.Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids).

They have tons of fun planned tonight:

"La Dolce Vita Garden Party, Dorrance Sculpture Garden

FREE, 6-10pm, Cash Bar

In celebration of our current fashion exhibition The White Shirt According to Me: Gianfranco Ferré, we’re throwing a fabulous Italian themed garden party for everyone to enjoy.

Activities:

- Outdoor film screening of Italian Classic La Dolce Vita outside in the Dorrance Sculpture garden at 7pm.

- Glamorous vintage fashions on view from Rare Scarf, Vintage by Misty and Fashion by Robert Black.

- Fashion presentation by student designers from the Art Institute of Phoenix.

- Italian cocktails, popcorn and espresso.

- Arts and crafts inspired by Italian fashion.

- FREE photo booth.

- Guided exhibition tours.

Free exhibition admission, one night only.

phxart.org / @phxart"

If it’s date night, you might want to think about checking out the Arizona Science Center. Adults 18 and over will get in for free (general admission). Head over here for more information.

Visit the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program.Everyone will get into the museum FOR FREE from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids).

Enjoy and have fun!
