Hooray! 37% off Admission to the Flagstaff Extreme Adventure Course!

Mar 19, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Head over here to enjoy 37% off admission to the Flagstaff Extreme Adventure Course on select dates (4/1-4/19, 6/15-6/21, or 9/14-9/20). You'll pay $124 for four people (usually $196).

We did this and had a wonderful time! It really is an adventure!

"Admission includes access to five elevated obstacle courses for adults, each course increases in difficulty. The entire experience takes about 2–3 hours to complete and consists of 10–17 obstacles, which range from rope swings to wobbly bridges and ziplines. Before exploring the courses, visitors must pass a 30-minute safety training, where they learn how to put on their harnesses and navigate the courses. For more information, see the park hours and the FAQs."

Enjoy!
