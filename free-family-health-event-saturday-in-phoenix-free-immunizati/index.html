FREE Family Health Event Saturday in Phoenix...Free Immunizations, Free Dental Screenings, Free Health Screenings, FUN, and More!

Mar 9, 2018 · Alicia

Thanks so much to Alicia's Deals reader Rachel for letting me know about this incredible family health event happening Saturday. It is 100% free and there are absolutely no strings attached!

Head over here for more information.

"Hosted by the Junior League of Phoenix (JLP), HealthFest is a FREE Health & Wellness family-friendly event that features health and wellness vendors with FREE health screenings, bounce houses, cooking and fitness demonstrations, and FREE raffles & giveaways including, bikes, Fitbit, workout equipment, and much more. Each family attending will leave with FREE Health Kit including personal hygiene products, fruit, and more!*

All health screenings below are FREE to all attendees:

- Children’s Immunizations

- Prenatal ultrasounds, lung/respiratory and pancreatic screenings, physical therapy thanks to the HUG Clinic at Gateway Community College

- Dental screenings and fluoride treatments to children 0-18

- Dental screenings for adults

- Smoke Cessation Assistance

- Blood pressure, BMI, and Glucose tests

- Stress Reduction

- Biometric Measurements, skin, nails, tongue, and taste exams, GI Health, & Hormone Symptoms testing

Plus more!

When: March 10, 2018 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Where: Gateway Community College, 108 N. 40th Street, Phoenix, AZ

Conveniently located on 40th Street & Washington Light Rail Stop

SPONSORSHIP: Generously sponsored by a grant from The Junior League of Phoenix Foundation

For any questions or to participate as a vendor, please email jlphealthfest@gmail.com

*Health Kits are first come, first serve basis"

Enjoy and please tell a friend if you think they could benefit!
