If you're a Southwest Rapid Rewards member, check your email now for a FREE companion pass offer. Basically, if you register and book a round-trip or two one-way flights by 2/5 (travel by 3/31), you'll get a free companion pass good 8/10-10/7. If you're going on a trip or two with a companion during the free dates, this could be an excellent deal for you (though I am very sad about the new fees for bags and assigned seating). Keep in mind that you MUST register for this deal.

Check out the details:
