As Seen on Your Life A to Z...Sandra Lee and Save Big When the Dbacks Do Well!

2015-04-27 · Alicia Markham

Thanks so much for tuning into Your Life A to Z today on 3TV! It was so much fun to fill in as co-host. Sandra Lee from Food Network was in the house whipping up EASY cocktails and desserts. I've been a fan of hers forever and she is genuinely nice, so much fun to be around, and beautiful inside and out!

I got to talk about deals we have to look forward to when the Dbacks do well.

Anytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs, win or lose, home or away, the next day we can all head to Taco Bell. From 4-6 PM score three free tacos with the purchase of a large drink.

If you love pizza, you'll definitely want to keep tabs on the score of the Dbacks games. The day after a win, Papa Johns will offer 50% off online orders with code DBACKS50.

The day after the Dbacks score a home run, we can head to Jack in the Box to get a free Jumbo Jack with large drink purchase all day long.

After home games, check the circle K app. They'll give deals based on the strike out meter. So far this year they've all been freebies. Head over here to get the app...today (4/27) you can get a free cookie, brownie, or slice!

There are some great deals for kids this season! They can join the No Chew Crew. For promising to avoid chewing tobacco they can get a free toothbrush and a free ticket to select Dbacks games! You can join online here or at Chase Field.

Also for the kids is the Dbacks kids club. Kids will get a prize for every game they attend. Prizes include magnets, towels, hats, even a glove! Click here for more information and a list of the prizes!

Enjoy the deals and GO DBACKS!
