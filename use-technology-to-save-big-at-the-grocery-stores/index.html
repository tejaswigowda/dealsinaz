Ways to save with technology:

Make sure you have all the grocery apps on your phone. Albertsons', Bashas', Fry's, Safeway, and Sprouts have apps that tie to your rewards account. They offer digital coupons each week that can add up fast.

Get the Ibotta app. This can be used at any store and offers cash back on hundreds of items...many of them are probably already on your list and the savings add up fast! You'll also get a $10 bonus after you redeem your first deal!

Think about buying groceries at unusual places. I love grocery shopping at Target.com...the prices are decent and I can drive up and have my purchases delivered straight to my car within two hours. They offer grocery coupons and deals each week for Target Circle rewards members...it's free to join!
