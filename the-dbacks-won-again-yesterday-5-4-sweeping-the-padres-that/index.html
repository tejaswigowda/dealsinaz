The Dbacks Won AGAIN Yesterday 5-4 Sweeping the Padres! That Means Free Tacos, Jumbo Jacks, Chips, and More!

Aug 17, 2020 · Alicia#EXPIRED

The Dbacks got ANOTHER big win against the Padres last night, beating them at home 5-4 (it was a SWEEP)! That means today (Monday, 8/17) we get deals (AGAIN)!

Papa John’s DBACKS50

When the D-backs win at home or away, fans get 50% off all pizzas at regular menu price at papajohns.com the next day when they use promo code DBACKS50. That means we can enjoy the deal today 8/17! Cannot be combined with any other offer. THIS OFFER VALID FOR PIZZA ONLY.

Taco Bell 5-Run Promo

Each time the D-backs score five (5) runs or more at home or away, fans can get three (3) free seasoned beef crunchy tacos with the purchase of a large drink from 4-6 p.m. the following day (8/17) at participating Arizona Taco Bell locations.

CIRCLE K STRIKEOUT METER

Download the Circle K app to get your strikeout meter coupon offer after every home game. Items available on the coupon are determined by the number of batters D-backs pitchers strikeout during each game. Today (8/17) the deal is a free Circle K Chips!

D-BACKS JACK HOME RUN PROMOTION

If any D-backs player “Jacks” a home run out of the park, fans can go to their local Jack in the Box restaurant the next day to receive a free Jumbo Jack with the purchase of a large drink. Today (8/17) we can take advantage of the deal since there were a couple of home runs!

Promotions are subject to change and may not occur at every game.

Enjoy and GO DBACKS!
