Buy One Discounted Day at Universal Studios Hollywood Get One Free!

Jul 23, 2024 · Alicia#Gift ideas#HOT DEAL

If you haven't visited Universal Studios Hollywood lately, there's lots to see! Super Nintendo World opened last year and they still have The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Jurassic World, Minion Land, the Studio Tour, Springfield (who doesn't love the Simpsons?!), Transformers Megabase, Water World, and more! If you want to take your time and see it all, head over here to get a FREE second day when you buy a one-day discounted ticket through ARES (a Universal Studios Hollywood Authorized Reseller). Ticket prices start at $101.99 for adults and $96.99 for kids, though adult summer prices are in the $135-$140 range.

Why pay full price for tickets when you don't have to?! The buy one day get one free deal goes all the way through 12/18/24 and works on the weekends (blackout dates do apply)!
