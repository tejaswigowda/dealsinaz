Mark Your Calendar for FREE PANCAKES at Scramble in Scottsdale Monday, February 9th!

Feb 5, 2015 · Alicia Markham

There are so many fun freebies this month! Mark your calendar for Monday, February 9th, Scramble in Scottsdale will be giving away free pancakes all day long!

"Scramble – a Breakfast Joint is giving away free pancakes all day at its Scramble-Scottsdale location (NW corner of Lincoln Blvd and Scottsdale Rd.) in celebration of the restaurant’s one year anniversary. No purchase is necessary. Dine in only. Not to be combined with any other specials, deals or coupons.

As a way to say thank you for the support in its first year of business in Scottsdale, Arizona residents and visitors alike are invited to stop by for a free order of Scramble’s Buttermilk or Multigrain Pancakes featuring two large fluffy cakes served with butter & syrup. (Sides not included.)"
