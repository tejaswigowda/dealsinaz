Memorial Day Deals for Veterans and Active Military!

May 25, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Memorial Day is a wonderful day to remember the people who put everything on the line to protect our country. As I'm typing this, my hubby is putting out the American Flag. I hope you'll share these freebies and deals with veterans and military service men and women you know.

Active military and veterans can head to Danny's Family car wash for a FREE basic wash with ID. Head over here for more information.

Active and retired military will get 10% off groceries through Tuesday with ID at Albertsons! Head over here for more information and to check out a $10 off your $100 purchase coupon!

Lowe's offers at veterans and active duty military 10% off on Memorial Day and certain other holidays. Head over here to check out the details (military discount is toward the bottom of the page...you may be eligible for a 10% off discount every day)!

The Melting Pots of Phoenix are proud to honor vets and their families with a special three-course fondue experience for $29.95 per person. They'll have select cocktails and wine for $6 too!

National Parks: Head over here to find out how military members and their families can get a FREE annual pass to national parks and federal recreational lands.

Enjoy the deals and thank you so much for your service!
