Money Saving Apps You Should DEFINITELY Have on Your Smart Phone...As Seen on 3TV!

Aug 5, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Thanks so much for tuning into my Your Life A to Z segment today! If you haven’t already, I hope you’ll “like” Alicia’s Deals on Facebook, follow me on Twitter and join the FREE Alicia’s Deals e-mail list (unsubscribe at anytime). You can now download my FREE app “The Deals Diva” on iTunes here or on Google Play here! This is probably the BEST money saving app you'll find!

Ibotta: Head to over here to download this totally free app. You'll save money at stores all over town like Target, Walmart, Fry's, Safeway, Bashas, Albertsons, Walgreens, even Sprouts and Whole Foods. Answer a question about an item you're buying and upload your receipt to get cash back on top of coupons and sales. I especially love this app because you can save on many fresh fruits and veggies that you usually never see coupons for. It is possible to eat healthy on a tight budget!

Ebates: If you shop online, you should make sure you're signed up for Ebates. You'll even get a free $10 gift card after your first $25 purchase when you go through my link. The way it works is that you'll get a percentage back from the things you're already buying online when you first go through Ebates. You'll get cash back from Bath and Body Works, Ann Taylor, tons of stores you're already shopping at! They actually send you a check a couple of times a year.

Target Cartwheel: If you ever shop at Target, you should absolutely have the Cartwheel app. They add extra discounts to items all over the store. I love that there is a scanner, so you can scan anything you're buying to see if there's an extra Cartwheel discount. The savings can be significant and it's so easy and fun to use once you get the hang of it. Don't forget that you can get a Target Red Card to come right out of your bank account and they'll take 5% off all of your Target purchases. Head to my site to find out more and get yours.

Walmart Savings Catcher: We should all know by now that Walmart will match competitors ads on their products. If you're too busy to deal with the ads, they have a great solution. Snag the Savings Catcher app and take a couple of seconds to scan your receipt or type in the numbers after you shop Walmart. They will check the competitors ads and if they find that you paid more than you would have somewhere else, they will give you a Walmart eGift Card for the difference. It gives you a little bit of piece of mind that you really are getting the best prices.

Enjoy the deals and let me know what you love to use to save money!
